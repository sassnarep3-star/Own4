Why correlation is not a parlay price

For binary legs, P(A and B) = P(A)P(B) + Cov(1A, 1B). If both variances are nonzero, covariance equals phi × √[pA(1−pA)pB(1−pB)]. Estimates vary by line, player, availability and game state. Even zero correlation in one sample does not establish conditional independence in another. More than two legs require the full joint distribution, not just pairwise coefficients. Push, void and bookmaker repricing alter settlement.

No sample size, confidence interval, coefficient or derived price is provided until independently verifiable inputs are permitted and available.
