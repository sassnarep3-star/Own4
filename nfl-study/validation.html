Proposed ranking policy

Qualify only when all applicable gates pass. Rank matched out-of-sample forecasts by improvement in mean proper loss versus the same baseline, lower loss being better; display uncertainty and eligible sample size. Compare separate market cohorts, not unlike prop and game targets. Treat ties as unresolved if the preregistered tie rule says so. Rewards require separate funding and legal approval; scores do not guarantee profit.

Randomness is irreducible. Forecast scores, clustered uncertainty, multiple-testing controls and prospective replication help distinguish skill from noise; they cannot prove an individual result was luck-free.
