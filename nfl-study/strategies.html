Fair pricing specification

For a binary no-push event with probability p, fair decimal odds are 1/p. With push and void outcomes, use the full settlement-state payout distribution; 1/p alone is not sufficient. Compare expected net return against the actual executable price, with all settlement terms and fees. Model prices are derived estimates—not NFL-published facts—and none are available here.
