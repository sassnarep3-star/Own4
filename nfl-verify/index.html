What this session changed

The single largest finding: the repository treats official inactive lists as unobtainable. They are published. nfl.com carries a dated inactives article naming 138 players across 24 club sections for Week 10, and the same article publishes the venue string for 12 games. That converts one of the biggest listed blockers from “the source does not exist” into “a parser has not been written”. See the availability evidence.
The single largest non-finding: market prices still do not exist. The NFL published kickoff time, network and venue for all 13 Week 11 fixtures and no line of any kind. 9 of the traced requirements ultimately depend on that one missing input, which is why it is question Q01 and suggestion S01, not a build ticket.
