Flagged for review

14 irregularities found this session. Each one names what is wrong, what the official source actually says, and why it is not blocking. None of these blocks publication; all of them should be read before the numbers are trusted.

The distinction this page protects. A stale value and an invented value are not the same defect and must not be treated the same way. In every case below the peer value was correct when captured, or the peer treated a published source as absent. Calling that fabrication would be wrong, and fixing it as if it were fabrication would destroy real work.

R-07lowA name-join defect was found in this session's own code and fixed before publicationThe first draft of the inactives-to-report join stripped generational suffixes and then took the last token, which mapped 'Deebo Samuel Sr.' to 'sr' and produced a false match against a different 49er. The published join removes the suffix from the token list before selecting the surname and additionally requires a first-name match when two players in one club share a surname. The corrected join moves the joinable count from 65 to 68 and the outlier cells from 3 to 2.It was caught by inspection during this session and the corrected numbers are the ones published.source

R-14highThe live injury page rolls to the next week's fixture list while still showing the previous week's reportAt capture the page heading read 'Injuries - WEEK 10' and the player tables were Week 10 content, but the fixture headers around them were already the Week 11 matchups. Two exact identities hold, and they invert: the six clubs in the report that are NOT on the Week 11 schedule (ATL, CLE, GB, LA, NE, SEA) are precisely the official Week 11 bye list, and the four clubs on the Week 11 schedule that are absent from the report (CHI, DEN, PHI, TB) are precisely the official Week 10 bye list. A scraper that keys players to the fixture header instead of to the club would silently attribute Week 10 injuries to Week 11 matchups.This session's transcription keys every row to the CLUB, never to the fixture header, and the verifier asserts both bye-list identities on every build.source
