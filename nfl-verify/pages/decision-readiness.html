Method

Rows are authored in engine/readiness.py. Every number quoted inside a row's clear-number cell is read from data/derived.json, which engine/derive.py recomputes from data/observations.json on every build. A number that cannot be recomputed therefore cannot be published: readiness.py fails when a row cites a key that does not exist.

Analytical research only — not wagering advice. Every published number is either computed from data/observations.json (transcribed from the official page named in the row) or shown as a blank. No value is inferred, interpolated or carried over from an unverified source.
