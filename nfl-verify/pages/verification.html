Independent verification

Every other namespace in this repository verified its own work. Nothing re-checked it. This page does: peer-published values are turned into claims, and each claim is resolved against a fresh official nfl.com read taken at 2026-11-15T23:27:10Z — never against another peer file.

Be precise about what a fail means here. A fail is not an accusation of invention. In every case where a peer value differs, the peer value was correct when captured and has since been overtaken, or the peer treated a source as absent that is in fact published. Both are process defects, not fabrication, and both are fixable.
