Executive summary

What is true about this project at 2026-11-15T23:27:10Z, in the order that matters.

1. The project is not blocked on engineering

78 requirements are traced. 21 are met and re-verified. 31 are implemented and limited by a named evidence gap. 13 are blocked and 13 were never started. The distribution is not a capacity problem: most blocked rows are blocked by one missing input that five separate requirements share.

2. That input is a market price, and it does not exist

The NFL publishes kickoff time, network and venue for all 13 Week 11 fixtures and no line of any kind. Every ROI, every closing-line value, every fair-versus-market residual and the entire rewards pool depend on it. Nine traced requirements reduce to one procurement decision (Q01 / S01). Until it is made, the honest product is a proper-score skill competition, not a betting product.

3. The biggest under-utilisation is an availability source the repository already had access to

The repository treats official inactive lists as unobtainable and accordingly never ingests them. They are published. A single dated nfl.com article names 138 inactive players across 24 club sections for Week 10 and also carries the venue for 12 games. That reframes the largest listed data gap from “impossible” to “a parser and a cron entry”.

4. The inactive list answers the calibration question partially, and the partial answer is useful

68 players appear in both the official report and the official inactive list. All of them carry an explicit Out, Doubtful or Questionable — not one named inactive has a blank status. So the designation column is a usable filter. But 70 named inactives never appear in the report, so a blank status still cannot mean “available”. Any model that treats absence from the report as health is wrong today.

5. The depth feature is real but not yet buildable, and the blocker is measurable

Two club depth pages were probed. Green Bay publishes a structured table with 44 named players and its own date. Houston publishes an image with no table and no date. So the per-club route works for some clubs: 2 of 32 verified, 30 unprobed. The brief's instinct was right — this cannot be derived from jersey numbers, and the Packers prepend jersey numbers to their names precisely so a naive parser will mistake a shirt for a rank.

6. Nothing published here is a pick

No forecasts are made on this site. It publishes what is known about the project, not what will happen on Sunday. The one strategy leaderboard in the repository awards 0 of 1,000 points because 0 of 5 strategies clears its own gates, and that zero is the correct output.
