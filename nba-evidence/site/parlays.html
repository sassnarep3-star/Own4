Interpretation

The joint residual method shifts training margin/total residual pairs to each pregame forecast and counts simultaneous hits, with 0.5 smoothing. It preserves the observed training residual pairing but is highly uncertain with 23 pairs. The lower test Brier is a hypothesis-generating result, not a validated advantage.

Cross-game examples use different dates and no shared teams. Shared league-wide shocks can still create dependence. The examples also reuse teams across separate tickets, so ticket results are not independent observations. No aggregate significance test is applied.
