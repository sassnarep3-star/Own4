Why the adjustment matters

Covariance reference ↗

For each player statistic, fit a linear relationship with minutes across the pooled player sample, then correlate the residuals. This removes a shared opportunity proxy, not all role or game-state confounding. Points/rebounds falls from the raw pooled estimate to the minutes-residualized estimate; points/assists is near zero in this tiny sample.

These are between-player observations in two games. A same-player parlay needs repeated within-player residuals conditional on pregame information. Neither Pearson r nor binary φ alone specifies the joint tail probability that determines a parlay payout.
