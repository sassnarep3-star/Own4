Reward rule

Selection-bias reference ↗

The research pool is 1,000 non-monetary points. Only strategies passing all ten gates enter the reward ranking. If at least three qualify, award 600 / 300 / 100 points by frozen prospective risk-adjusted net return, with ties sharing the affected places. If fewer qualify, leave their shares unawarded. This release has zero qualifiers and zero awards.

No money, token, payment, sportsbook credit or guaranteed prize is offered. Diagnostic Brier rank never substitutes for the release gates.
