Selection discipline

Record every candidate and failed experiment. Freeze feature definitions, thresholds and model versions before the evaluation window. Compare against a matched baseline on identical observations. Use the actual available price—not a convenient assumed price—and account for pushes, voids, limits and correlated exposure.

For parlays, model the joint event. For player combinations, condition on the same player, role and pregame information. A positive correlation is not automatically a positive expected return.
