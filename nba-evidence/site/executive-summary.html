NBA line origination research

Executive summary

The findings, decision and limits—without the sales pitch.

View sources ↗

The headline: a working research system, not a proven edge

This release contains 62 official NBA game records, 38 participating-player records from two complete box scores, 39 reproducible historical fair-line sets, eight strategy hypotheses and ten release gates. It is a deliberately bounded pilot, not a complete NBA database or a current betting slate.

Decision: do not deploy capital based on this pilot. No strategy passes all gates; no reward points are awarded. The model's test Brier score is 0.3076, worse than the frozen baseline's 0.2720.

What the evidence supports

  • Reproducibility: features use earlier dates only; training, validation and test windows are separated. All 100 input records link to official NBA pages.
  • Line origination: shrunk scoring-for/against estimates generate team scores, margins and totals. Logistic probabilities produce zero-margin fair decimal prices. These are model outputs, not sportsbook quotes.
  • Dependence matters: the same-game residual experiment scored 0.1092 Brier versus 0.1199 for multiplying marginals on the same 24 games. That is an exploratory comparison, not proof of a stable joint model.
  • Player combinations: pooled player correlations change after removing a linear minutes effect. Two games cannot identify player-specific conditional distributions or personalized prop value.
  • Luck remains: a proper score, baseline comparison and leakage tests are useful controls. They do not eliminate variance, hidden selection or non-stationarity.

What is missing

Authorized point-in-time sportsbook quotes, limits and ticket rules; a larger multi-season sample; pregame injury/lineup/rest/venue information; repeated player histories; and a genuinely prospective frozen prediction log. Unsupported values remain blank.

Competition design

The diagnostic leaderboard ranks probability forecasts on the same locked test set. A separate betting division requires every gate to pass before eligibility. The proposed 1,000-point non-monetary pool would split 60% / 30% / 10% among the top three eligible strategies; with zero eligible strategies, the entire pool remains unawarded. No funded prize is represented.

Repository and publication integrity

Concurrent sessions added several dashboards while this work was in progress. Their files are preserved. This workspace is isolated under nba-evidence/. The repository audit distinguishes a mechanical scan of every tracked text line from targeted primary-source checks; it does not claim every inherited number was independently re-fetched.

Recommended next research sequence

  1. Resolve access rights and obtain official operator price history.
  2. Expand the league-verified, point-in-time dataset without using future information.
  3. Freeze a future protocol and log every prediction and every research trial.
  4. Validate singles first; then joint distributions and actual quoted parlay prices.
  5. Require dependence-aware out-of-sample improvement and prospective replication before any reward or deployment.
