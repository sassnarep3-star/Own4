What this namespace is

The scheduled availability collector the brief asked for: "Automate immutable multi-day availability snapshots — capture the official report and club depth pages at least daily with retained hashes, so practice designations can be replayed as they were known." It was adopted as decision DEC-2 = A (the handover queue's first engineering item) and built in this session. It holds:

  • A capture ledger with a sha256 hash chain — every capture sealed, appended-only, idempotent; the chain is re-verified on every CI run.
  • A drift alarm with pre-pinned expectations — row counts, required fields and vocabulary pinned BEFORE capture; a schema change fails the build loudly instead of publishing blanks.
  • The delta engine — "publish the delta, never the level": dated reads of the same official report are never overwritten; changes BETWEEN reads are the published series.
  • Cross-source conflict flags — transactions (trades, reserve list) joined to the weekly report; the traded-player conflict the brief predicted is now reproduced computationally.
  • A pre-registered paired test (PREREG-002) — frozen before any Week 11 result exists; its calibration reproduces the published backtest exactly; evaluation is PENDING by design.
  • The decision ledger — the stalled P1 queue answered under the standing no-manual-input instruction, each verdict adopting the peer session's own written recommendation, flagged for post-hoc owner review.
