Rules applied throughout

  • Official means the domain is nfl.com or operations.nfl.com. Everything else is labelled.
  • A value that cannot be confirmed from a cited source is left blank and listed on Flagged for review.
  • Derived values link to the repository file that computes them, never to an NFL page.
  • Dead citations are recorded, not silently removed, so a reviewer can see what was rejected.

Limits. Lines and ratings are derived model output from a third-party archive, not official NFL data. Market lines, depth charts and player-prop values are unavailable and are left blank. Nothing here is a recommendation to wager.
