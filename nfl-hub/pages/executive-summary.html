Executive summary

What is verified, what is missing, what the numbers actually say, and the five decisions that unlock the next phase.

Bottom line. The plumbing works and the evidence is honest: official data reconciles 32/32, the lines are generated before kickoff without look-ahead, and every strategy fails. The next decision is not a model change — it is whether to buy a real market feed (D2) and which dashboard is canonical (D1).

1. Situation

The repository already contained four separate NFL dashboards built by earlier sessions, publishing different lines for different weeks with no canonical marker. Two cite NFL.com URLs that return 404. This build adds a fifth, self-contained workspace that does three things the others do not: it reconciles every number to the live official source, it pre-registers the gates before measuring, and it publishes a decision register instead of only results.

4. Findings that change decisions

  1. The market is a better forecaster than the model. On 2,383 holdout games the closing line beats the model on margin MAE (9.87 vs 10.39) and totals (10.47 vs 11.97). A standalone model cannot be the product; a residual model judged by closing-line value is the defensible path.
  2. Luck is not removed by a good story. S1 earns +4.6% on the 2013-2016 validation seasons and then loses 5.3% on the holdout. The placebo test puts that result at p=0.60 — indistinguishable from random side selection.
  3. Same-game legs are close to independent in the game markets. Favourite-cover vs under phi = 0.0117 (95% CI -0.0225 to 0.0459). Player-leg dependence is unknown, not small.
  4. Availability is the biggest unexploited input — and the least verifiable. Three quarterback changes are confirmed, but defensive depth is not published officially, so any "backup deficiency" number would be invented.
  5. Four dashboards are a decision hazard. They disagree, and two carry dead citations. One must be canonical.

6. What exists in this build

15 pages: dashboard, executive summary, requirements coverage, suggestions & decisions, open questions, originated lines, injuries, strategy catalogue, validation, correlations, leaderboard, official data, sources, repository review and flagged items. Every table row carries a source link or an explicit DERIVED badge. The pipeline rebuilds every number from the committed data; the verifier fails the build on an unsourced row or a broken internal link.

Limits. Lines and ratings are derived model output from a third-party archive, not official NFL data. Market lines, depth charts and player-prop values are unavailable and are left blank. Nothing here is a recommendation to wager.
