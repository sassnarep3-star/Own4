Leaderboard & rewards

A competition that pays only for evidence: confidence-bound ranking, non-monetary points and a pool that rolls over when nothing qualifies.

The pool is unawarded. A strategy must reach Gold (provisional) to earn points. None did, so 1,000 points roll over. That is the reward rule doing its job, not a bug.

Reward policy

Rules

  • Pool: 1000 reputation points; non-monetary.
  • Split equally among strategies reaching Gold (provisional).
  • Roll over when nobody qualifies.
  • Silver/Bronze receive recognition only.
  • G8 (one full forward season) caps every strategy at provisional.

Why non-monetary

Paying out on wagering outcomes raises licensing, tax and consumer-protection questions that are not resolved for this project; see the Open Questions register (Q-14).

Q-13 must be answered before any prize is offered.

Why ROI alone would be the wrong score

With a few hundred bets, a strategy can show a double-digit ROI and still be pure noise. The placebo test measures exactly that: random side selection with the same weekly exposure produces a distribution of ROIs, and S1's observed result sits inside it (p=0.60). Ranking on the confidence bound, not the point estimate, is the difference between a competition that rewards skill and one that rewards variance.
