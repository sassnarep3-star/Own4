What the official report actually shows

  • Only six players league-wide carried a formal game designation (Out/Questionable) on the official Week 10 report at capture time. Every other listed player had a practice status only; final designations are published closer to kickoff.

The defensive backup problem — why it is blank

Requested: know when defences are down to 2nd, 3rd or 4th-string players.

Verified blocker: the NFL.com team depth-chart URL returns "404 - Flag on the Play" (SRC-14) and no official depth publication was located. The official injury report names the player and his practice status, but not his place on the depth chart.

Therefore: the starter-weighted availability index is not computed — no number is published, because any number would imply unverified starter status. Offensive "starters vs backups" has the same limitation.

What would settle it: a licensed snap-count feed or an official depth chart, plus an explicit definition of "starter" (see Q-05 and Q-21).

Limits of this module

  • Snapshot, not a live feed. The official page updates through the week and again 90 minutes before kickoff.
  • The 'Injuries' column is blank for most players, so cause cannot be attributed from the table alone.
  • Practice status is not a projection of availability.
