Validation & luck

Validation gates & luck removal

Eight pre-registered gates, a one-shot holdout, week-clustered intervals and a placebo test — the machinery that stops a lucky run from looking like skill.

Read the gates before the ROI. A positive ROI with a confidence interval that includes zero is not evidence. The holdout was scored once; the thresholds were frozen beforehand.

The clearest lesson on this page: on validation (2013-2016) S1 returned +4.6% and S3 returned +9.8%. On the untouched holdout they returned -5.3% and -8.0%. That gap is what a holdout is for.

G5RobustnessPositive ROI in >= 60% of holdout seasons and no single season contributes > 50% of total profit.DERIVED

G7Pricing realismBets priced at archived closing lines/odds. Parlay payouts use a stated assumption and are flagged; proxy markets FAIL G7 by construction.DERIVED
G8Forward replicationProspective timestamped predictions for >= 1 full season. Cannot pass in this session; caps any strategy at Gold (provisional).DERIVED
