How severity is used

  • Blocking for claims — a conclusion that cannot be stated until the item is fixed.
  • Contradiction — two official sources disagree; neither is assumed correct.
  • Verified absence — an official source was checked and the data does not exist there.
  • Disclosure / method limit — true by construction, repeated wherever the number appears.
  • Resolved — a trap that was found and explained, recorded so it is not reintroduced.

Limits. Lines and ratings are derived model output from a third-party archive, not official NFL data. Market lines, depth charts and player-prop values are unavailable and are left blank. Nothing here is a recommendation to wager.
