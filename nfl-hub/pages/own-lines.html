Originated lines — Week 10

Fourteen model lines with the method, the ratings and the look-ahead tests that prove no future result was used.

These are not sportsbook lines. They are this model's estimates for 14 Week 10 games, generated 12 Nov 2026 before the first kickoff. The market column is blank because no official or licensed 2026 market price exists in scope. Do not compare these to a book you have not verified.

Method

Model

  • Elo rating, K=20, home-field 48 Elo, season regression 75%.
  • Exponentially weighted offence/defence in points above league average (α=0.25).
  • Blend chosen on 2013-2016 validation: 50% points model, 50% Elo.
  • Elo-to-points slope 0.0602 fitted on 1999-2012 only.
  • Win probability from a normal model with σ=13.182 fitted on validation residuals.

Integrity

  • Predictions use only games before the current week; updates happen after the whole week is played.
  • Look-ahead test 1: delete every post-Week-9 row → identical.
  • Look-ahead test 2: rewrite every post-Week-9 result with random scores → identical.
  • Archive reconciled to official standings before modelling: 32/32.
