Suggestions & decisions

Fifteen proposals to improve decision quality, three decision gates that unblock everything else, and a workflow for choosing a path before implementing it.

How to use this page. Nothing here has been implemented silently. Each suggestion states the problem, the options, the recommendation and what it depends on. Choose an option, record it in the decision log, then build.

Decision gates

Nothing else should be built until these three are answered. They are ordered by how much downstream work they unblock.

D1 Which NFL workspace is canonical?

Five earlier NFL surfaces publish different material (four dashboards plus a decision desk), and two decision registers now exist. A reader cannot tell which is current, and search authority is split across all of them.

OptionNameProsConsEvidence
D1-aKeep nfl-hub and freeze the other fourOne maintained surface; this hub reconciles to official standings and publishes its own limits.Older pages remain reachable and may contradict the hub.DERIVED
D1-bKeep nfl-quant and extend itIt already has a pipeline and pre-registered gates.Its lines are Week 9 and its 2026 market inputs are stale; extending it would mix two methodologies.DERIVED
D1-cPublish a comparison page and keep all fiveMaximum transparency.Worst for a casual reader; the contradiction remains.DERIVED
D1-dKeep nfl-hub for lines/evidence and nfl-decisions for governance, cross-link themEach surface keeps one job; no register is discarded.Two registries to maintain; the reader must know which to open.DERIVED

Recommendation: D1-a plus D1-d: nfl-hub becomes the single lines/evidence surface, nfl-decisions is kept only as its governance annex (or its unique questions are merged in and it is banner-archived). Banner the four dashboards. Do not delete anything.

D2 Buy or license a real market feed, or stay archive-only?

With no timestamped market/player feed there is no way to measure closing-line value, price props, or validate parlay payouts. Every strategy is capped at 'research-only'.

OptionNameProsConsEvidence
D2-aLicense a commercial odds + player-prop API with historical timestampsUnlocks CLV, prop lines, SGP pricing and honest G7.Recurring cost; contract review; redistribution limits.DERIVED
D2-bStay with free archivesZero cost, fully reproducible.No 2026 lines, no props, no parlay prices; cannot validate a real edge.DERIVED
D2-cManual price snapshots at fixed timesCheap.Not reproducible, not auditable, and conflicts with the no-manual-input requirement.DERIVED

Recommendation: D2-a if the project is meant to produce executable lines; otherwise D2-b and accept that the output stays a research demo.

D3 What does the leaderboard actually reward?

Rewarding raw ROI rewards luck. A strategy can top a table with 40 bets and no skill.

OptionNameProsConsEvidence
D3-aReward the lower confidence bound of net ROI after gatesPunishes small samples and variance; aligns reward with evidence.Harder to explain.DERIVED
D3-bReward raw ROI with a minimum sampleSimple.Still luck-dominated until n is large.DERIVED
D3-cNo rewards until G8 forward replication passesMost conservative.Nothing to compete over for a full season.DERIVED

Recommendation: D3-a, with the pool rolling over when no strategy qualifies. This is what the current leaderboard implements: 0 points awarded because no strategy passed.

Review workflow

  1. Start with the three 'decision gates' D1-D3 below. Nothing else should be built until they are answered.
  2. For each suggestion, read the evidence links. A suggestion that depends on missing information is marked BLOCKED and points to the question that must be answered first.
  3. Pick one option per decision, write it in data/decision_log.json with your name, date and reason, then open a task. Undecided items stay proposed; they do not block the site.
  4. Re-check any suggestion whose assumption is 'the market is inefficient'. The current evidence says the opposite for game markets.
