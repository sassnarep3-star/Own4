Repository review

A line-by-line read of the four prior NFL workspaces, with measured sizes, live-tested citations and a self-review of this build.

Review method. Every file in the four NFL workspaces was read; page counts and sizes were measured; a sample of cited URLs was fetched live; and each workspace's published slate was compared with the current Week 10 slate.

What is actually wrong, in one line

The repository has five NFL surfaces and no canonical marker. The most serious defects are dead citations (2), a stale slate presented as current (2 workspaces), and a missing official depth-chart source. None of these blocks publishing this hub, because the hub states each limitation in place.

What this review does not do

  • It does not certify factual truth of every row in the other workspaces — only the specific claims tested.
  • It does not re-run their pipelines; it reads their committed outputs.
  • It does not delete or rewrite another session's work. Fixes are logged for the owning session.

Limits. Lines and ratings are derived model output from a third-party archive, not official NFL data. Market lines, depth charts and player-prop values are unavailable and are left blank. Nothing here is a recommendation to wager.
