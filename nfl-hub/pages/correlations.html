Correlation register

Measured dependence for spreads, totals, same-game combos and cross-game parlays — with the two prop correlations blocked rather than guessed.

Correlation is not an edge. These coefficients describe dependence between outcomes. They do not establish a profitable relationship, and none of them is an official NFL statistic — all are computed from the pinned archive.

What this means for parlays and combos

  • Cross-game parlays: legs are effectively independent (C-05 phi -0.0122). Multiplying probabilities is defensible; the book's payout assumption is the weak link, not the maths.
  • Same-game game-market combos: favourite-cover vs under is near zero (C-03 phi +0.0117), so an SGP on those two legs carries no meaningful dependence correction.
  • Player props: the two correlations that matter most for prop combos (C-06, C-07) are blocked. They are not approximated. Any prop-combo pricing before Q-07 is answered would be fabricated.

Method notes

  • Correlation is not causation and not an edge.

  • Confidence intervals are Fisher-z approximations and ignore week clustering; treat them as indicative.
