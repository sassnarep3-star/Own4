Executive summary

Updated on 2026-11-17 from official NFL page reads and committed repository captures. Read windows and coverage are recorded on the Sources page. Where official sources do not establish a value, it stays blank and the uncertainty is flagged.

1. Availability is the strongest dataset in the repo and it is still not sufficient

The committed Week 10 capture gives 326 rows across 28 clubs and 14 fixtures (nfl-arena/data/raw/nfl_injuries_week10_2026.md). But joining the live report to the official inactive lists for the eight clubs where every row was read shows that 26 of 46 inactive players — 56.5% — never appear on the injury report. Excluding the two emergency third quarterbacks, 24 of 44 (54.5%). The injury report is a partial view of unavailability, not the view. The league publishes a dated reserve list (S10 ↗, 25 moves in November 2026), which explains 2 of the 3 Out players who were missing from the inactive list. A 25-row structured capture and ledger hash already exist in nfl-collector (S17 ↗); this namespace has not wired that capture into its own weekly join, so cross-week reserve membership remains open.

An Out designation does not guarantee inactive. 14 of 17 Out players were inactive; 2 more were placed on the official reserve list during the week (TreVeyon Henderson NE; Tyler Goodson DAL), so 16 of 17 (94.1%) are explained across the injury report, inactive list and reserve-list record. The injury report lists Kerby Joseph (DET) as Out, but the inactive list and November reserve-list record do not account for this case. No official page read this session states why.

3. Depth has no verified input on the official domain

The league does not publish a depth chart: the club depth-chart path returns the official 404 page and the club page offers Info, Roster and Stats only. No snap-count table and no starters list was found on the club page, the Game Center, the schedule page or the scores page. The backup-deficiency feature therefore has no input today. The brief already says not to build it until one route is verified for all 32 clubs; that condition is unmet.

4. Prices do not exist on the official domain

No spread, total or moneyline appears on any official page read this session. That single fact blocks the residual, the closing-line-value column, the ROI leaderboard, the correlation of residuals and the parlay joint-evidence gate. Those columns are blank here and should be blank everywhere until the owner chooses a price policy (suggestion SG-B).

5. The repository disagrees with itself

Three earlier sessions published 254, 279 and 347 rows for the same Week 10 injury page. This session's parse of the committed capture gives 326. And the live page read today differs from that capture again. The fix is not more counting; it is one canonical capture file with every count computed from it at build time (suggestion SG-H).
