Executive summary

The repo contains multiple NFL implementations, while the canonical landing page remains NBA-centric. Preserve existing research but do not treat archive-based calculations as newly verified official facts. First acquire timestamped official observations and replacement-role evidence. Then originate probability distributions and fair lines with uncertainty, validate chronologically, and compare against simple baselines. Only evaluate economics when recorded, executable operator prices and settlement rules exist.

Pairwise correlation does not specify a multi-leg joint probability. Same-game and prop combinations need separate joint calibration. Statistical testing can quantify uncertainty; it cannot eliminate luck or guarantee profitable bets.
