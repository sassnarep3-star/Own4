Proposed reward policy

Freeze strategy versions and all attempted hypotheses; accept immutable pregame forecasts only. Separate singles, cross-game parlays, same-game parlays and prop-combo divisions. Rank only gate-passing entries by preregistered baseline-adjusted scoring with game/week-clustered uncertainty. Publish sample size, stability, drawdown and score intervals; no award when evidence is insufficient. Cash rewards require a funded, legally reviewed policy; no budget or payout is invented.

Dependence specification

Store leg definitions, event and cutoff IDs, aligned sample size, coefficient method, interval, missingness, training window and held-out joint calibration. Estimate binary hit association and continuous forecast-residual correlation separately. A model-derived coefficient is not an official league statistic. For two binary legs, joint probability must lie between max(0, pA+pB−1) and min(pA,pB). For three or more legs, pairwise coefficients do not identify the joint distribution.

Fair line specification

Fit a pregame joint home/away score distribution on prior data. Win probability is the mass where the selected team wins; fair spread balances cover probabilities accounting for pushes; fair total balances over/under probabilities. State sign conventions and settlement rules. Quotes must come from an operator, not from inferred consensus or fabricated odds.
