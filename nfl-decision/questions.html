Strategy and validation

Q06Does a model built only from archived scores beat the closing line? known blocks: nothing - answered
Answer
No. Holdout (2018-2025): the best spread strategy returned +1.09% at an ASSUMED -110 with a 95% bootstrap CI of [-4.65%, +7.03%] and FDR q = 0.199. It cannot be distinguished from luck.
Known
Full backtest in data/backtest.json; methodological detail on the Validation page. [repo: nfl-decision/data/backtest.json]
Not known
Whether additional inputs (injuries, depth, pace, weather) would change this. That is a hypothesis, not a finding.
Why it blocks
It does not block; it sets the honest baseline. 0 of 1000 reward points are awarded.
Suggestion
S08 - stop refining the spread model and redirect effort to the forecast leaderboard, which is price-independent.
Q07What sample size do we need before a 55% hit rate is distinguishable from luck? partial blocks: Gold tier for any strategy
Answer
At 80% power and a two-sided 5% test, detecting 55% against the 52.4% break-even needs roughly 620 bets.
Known
Computed in 03_backtest.py; the holdout supplied 948 S1 bets but only across 8 seasons with 1 dominant season. [repo: nfl-decision/data/backtest.json (G2/G5 diagnostics)]
Not known
Whether the owner is willing to fund a multi-season forward paper-trading period. That is the only way to reach that n honestly.
Why it blocks
Rewards paid on fewer than 300 bets would be rewarding luck.
Suggestion
S09 - pay forecast-skill rewards now and hold betting rewards until a forward record exists.
Q08Are player-prop combos positively correlated enough to break independent pricing? partial blocks: prop strategies
Answer
Yes, strongly. P(QB pass-yards over) = 0.4645, P(WR1 rec-yards over) = 0.4072, joint = 0.2740 vs 0.1892 if independent - a lift of 1.45x, phi = 0.346 with a 95% CI of [0.284, 0.406].
Known
Measured on 803 team-games from 2021-2022 archived per-player box scores. Thresholds are PROXY lines (rolling means), not sportsbook prices. [repo: nfl-quant/data/prop_combo_legs.csv (third-party PFR-schema archive)]
Not known
The price a sportsbook would charge for the same combo - books reprice correlated SGP legs, so the lift may already be in the price.
Why it blocks
G7 blocks it: without archived SGP prices the +45% lift is not demonstrably tradeable.
Suggestion
S10 - pursue archived SGP prices; this is the single most promising unpriced signal in the repo.
Q09Can parlay legs be multiplied together as if independent? known blocks: nothing - answered
Answer
Yes for cross-game legs. phi between two same-week cover legs = -0.0057 with a bootstrap CI spanning 0, and the same-week cover-count chi-square/df ratio is close to 1.0 (no overdispersion). Same-game cover and over legs are also near-independent (phi = 0.0077).
Known
Computed over 7,087 games and 472 weeks. [repo: nfl-decision/data/correlations.json (D1, D1b, D2)]
Not known
Nothing for cross-game parlays. For same-game parlays, prop-level dependence is material (Q08) and must be priced.
Why it blocks
It does not block; it licenses the S4 cross-game parlay construction.
Suggestion
S11 - keep cross-game parlay multiplication and never multiply same-player prop legs without a repricing model.
Q10What is our definition of 'skill' that cannot be faked by re-running the search? unknown blocks: any claim of skill
Answer
no verified answer — left blank
Known
The repo pre-registers gates (data/gates.json), freezes model knobs on 1999-2012, and scores 2018-2025 exactly once. [repo: nfl-decision/data/gates.json]
Not known
How many strategies may be tried before the family-wise error rate must be re-controlled, and who approves a new strategy as pre-registered.
