Validation gates

Eight pre-registered gates, and the statistical machinery used to remove luck rather than hide it.

Gates were committed before the backtest ran. Pre-registered 2026-11-12; thresholds are project choices, not official NFL values.

The eight gates

IDGateRuleSource
G1Provenance & integrityEvery input row carries an official URL or a cited archive; the 2026 score subset must reconcile 160/160 cells to nfl.com standings.repo

Power: how many bets to prove a 55% edge

Two-sided 5% test at 80% power against the 52.38% break-even at -110 requires roughly 620 settled bets. The holdout produced 948 S1 bets but concentrated in one season, so the effective evidence is far smaller than the raw count suggests.

G8 cannot pass in one session. Forward replication needs at least a full season of timestamped pre-kickoff predictions. >= 1 full season of timestamped, pre-kickoff predictions.
