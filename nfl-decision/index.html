What this site is

A decision surface, not a picks page. Three things, in order:

LayerWhat it doesWhere
1. QuestionsLists what must be answered before any decision is reasonable, separating what we know from what we do not.open the register
2. SuggestionsPuts options, evidence, cost and risk side by side so you can pick a path instead of accepting one silently.open the board
3. EvidenceThe measured results those suggestions rest on — every number computed here or transcribed from an official page.open the gates
