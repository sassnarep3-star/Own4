What this is

An origination competition with a pre-registered rule set. Six models forecast three markets — the side, then the total, then the moneyline — from stored official results only. They are scored on an untouched holdout by proper score, with a week-cluster interval and a multiplicity adjustment. The staking division is published empty, because the repository contains no officially sourced price whose capture time is known.
