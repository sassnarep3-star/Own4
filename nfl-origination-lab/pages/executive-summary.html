Overview · executive summary

Executive summary

The short version

Skill exists in one market, not three. On 150 graded 2026 games, a recency-weighted team-strength model beats a no-information climatology benchmark on the moneyline with a Brier skill of +0.1509 (90% week-cluster interval [+0.0795, +0.2143], Holm-adjusted p 0.0085). The same forecasts show no total-market skill that survives the multiplicity adjustment, and no margin-market skill at all. That is the honest result, and it is the opposite of a green light.

Nothing can be staked, and no substitute is used. The repository's price store has 12,573 rows. Every row with a known capture time carries a non-official source; every row with an official source has no capture time. The intersection is empty, so the staking division is published empty rather than filled with an assumed -110.

Availability is the biggest missing input. There are no snap counts, no inactive lists and no game books anywhere in the repository, and one club publishes its depth chart only as an image. The brief's own precondition for a backup-deficiency feature is therefore unmet, and no fixed points penalty was substituted for it.
