Price-data policy

The decision

the intersection is empty. No officially published number in the store has a known capture time, and no number with a known capture time is officially published.

An opening number and a closing number are both required before ROI can exist. This repository has neither in claimable form, so the policy is: no ROI, no CLV, no units, no bankroll, and no assumed vig. An origination residual against the official posted line is still published — that only needs a published number, not a timestamped one.
