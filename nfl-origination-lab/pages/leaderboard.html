Leaderboard

How this is ranked

Ranking is by benchmark-relative proper score: Brier skill on the moneyline, Brier skill on the total threshold, and CRPS skill on the margin. Each comes with a 90 percent week-cluster bootstrap interval (the week is the unit of independence; 10,000 resamples, seed 20261119) and a Holm–Bonferroni adjustment over the whole declared family of 18 tests. Raw win rate is not a ranking metric and is not shown as one. The staking division is a separate table and it is empty.

Benchmark = M0 climatology: training-window base rates with no team information. That is the floor any real model has to clear.
