Demotion gate

Sends a live strategy back to paper when its signal or its data fails.

No strategy has priced selections. All strategies are registered; none may be live.

Stages: registered → paper → live. Promotion needs ALL of P1–P5. Missing data: Any missing input for a live strategy = demote. Any missing input for promotion = stay paper.

IDRuleStatusSource
