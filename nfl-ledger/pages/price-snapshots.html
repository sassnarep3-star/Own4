Price-snapshot store

Opening and closing numbers for every market we originate — schema ready, data not available.

Empty by policy. EMPTY BY POLICY — 0 rows. No official source publishes betting lines (see F-L1).

Schema

FieldMeaning
snapshot_idUnique id for one captured price
game_keyJoins to results_feed (e.g. 2026-W11-IND@HOU)
marketspread (side) · total · moneyline
snapshotopen (first capture) · close (last before kickoff)
bookSource of the number (must be named)
captured_utcCapture timestamp (required)
line / price_americanThe number as captured
source_urlPage the number came from (required)
source_is_officialtrue only for official nfl.com pages
statusok · missing (never back-filled)

Validation rules

  • snapshot_id unique; game_key must exist in results_feed.json
  • market in {spread, total, moneyline}; snapshot in {open, close}; close captured_utc < kickoff
  • source_url required; source_is_official must be true for any value shown on an official-only page
  • a missing open or close is stored as status=missing (never back-filled or estimated)
  • a row without a capture timestamp is rejected
