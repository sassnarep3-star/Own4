Methodology

The model

Each team carries an offensive rating (points scored above the league mean) and a defensive rating (points allowed below the league mean). Ratings are updated after every game with the residual from that game's forecast, re-identified each game by shifting the mean offensive rating into the defensive ratings, and reverted toward the league mean. The league mean and the residual standard deviation use expanding windows with the pre-registered priors. A game is forecast with the information available before it.

Home field advantage. 1.5 points, applied only when the designated home team hosted the game. The 2026 season has 7 neutral-site games, where no home-field advantage is applied.

Splits. Development 1999-2022 (model may be inspected; no threshold is tuned on the holdout). Validation 2023-2024. Holdout 2025-2026 (scored once; no parameter is changed after the holdout is read)

Look-ahead test. 0 forecasts made before the holdout changed when the outcome of all 422 holdout games was shifted by seven points, out of 6991 earlier forecasts.

Pre-registration

The gates and the model parameters were committed to git before the engine was first run. One dated amendment followed, recording a defect found on the development window, the fix, and the parameters selected on that window alone.

The price source

The NFL publishes no betting lines, so every price in this project comes from a named third-party archive with a recorded commit and checksum, and every such value is labelled third-party. Take the vig-removed moneyline probability of the home team from the archive, which is the only verified market probability in the archive. Convert it into a market expected margin using the pre-registered residual standard deviation prior of 13.5 points: mu_market = sigma x inverse_normal(P_home). Evaluate the market's own line against that margin: P(home covers) = normal_cdf(inverse_normal(P_home) - home_line / sigma), where home_line = -spread_line. Turn that fair probability into a decimal settlement price and charge the archive's median observed overround of 0.0247 against it, so the settlement price is what a bookmaker charging that margin would quote.
