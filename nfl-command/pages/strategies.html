Luck-removal design

Skill is separated from luck by: (1) walk-forward-only prediction; (2) a frozen holdout (weeks 8-10) never used for tuning; (3) 10,000 label-shuffled placebo runs — a signal must beat the 95th percentile to be called skill; (4) week-clustered bootstrap CIs (game outcomes within a week are dependent); (5) pre-registered gates committed to git before results existed; (6) no strategy may claim skill from in-sample fit.
