Depth & Deficiencies

Who is missing, which unit is thin, and what the league does — and does not — publish.

The hard limit. The NFL publishes no depth chart and no snap counts on its public pages. The official injury report gives practice/game status only. Therefore "starter vs 2nd/3rd/4th string" is unverified everywhere on this site (flag F-08) and is never inferred. What is official: (1) absences (Out/DNP/Questionable) per team and unit, (2) nfl.com news statements about who starts or is replaced.

How to read the deficiency picture

  • Baltimore (defense & offense affected): QB1 Lamar Jackson on IR (timeline inconsistent across official articles — F-07); QB2 Tyler Huntley out for season (official news). The QB room is down to unconfirmed backups; this site lists the absences and does not assign string numbers.
  • Miami: QB1 Malik Willis on IR; backup Kyle McCord in line for his first start (official news) — an official, named backup-to-starter transition.
  • Seattle: QB1 Sam Darnold ruled out; backup Drew Lock expected to start (official news).
  • Cleveland / Arizona: official starter designations (Watson; Beck) after earlier QB changes.
  • Unit-level absence load (Out + DNP by side) is on the Injuries page — e.g., Dallas carries 10 DNP-practice players, many on defense; the register shows exactly who and at which position.
  • Roster moves change depth: BUF acquired S Amani Hooker; DEN acquired LB Daiyan Henley from LAC (official news).

What would make string-level tracking possible

Extract snap counts from the official game-book PDFs linked on every game page (suggestion SUG-02/03): a player taking starter snaps is empirically a starter. Until that exists, any "2nd/3rd/4th string" label from any source should be treated as unverified.
