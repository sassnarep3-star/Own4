Why nothing wins (honest reading)

  • The margin model's holdout improvement is real-looking (placebo p < 0.01) but its week-clustered CI includes zero — on 30 games, luck cannot be excluded at the pre-registered standard.
  • Sample size (G2) can only be met across multiple seasons — that is deliberate; it is what separates "selectively skill-based" from "lucky so far".
  • Market gate G6 cannot even be evaluated without licensed prices; betting-market ROI claims are impossible in this repository today.
  • Player-prop strategies have zero graded selections — the required official data does not exist in scope.
