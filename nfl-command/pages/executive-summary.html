What we built

  • Own lines — ridge-regularised margin ratings + tendency totals from official PF/PA and the verified game archive; fair win probability from a frozen residual sigma. No market input anywhere.
  • Strategy competition — 6 registered strategies (spread/total/ML singles, cross-game parlays, player-prop combos, SGP), 10 pre-registered gates committed to git before the backtest ran, leaderboard with 6,000 points available and 0 awarded.
  • Luck removal — walk-forward-only predictions, frozen holdout, 10,000 label-shuffled placebos, week-clustered bootstrap, split-half stability. Skill must clear all of these; the current margin signal clears direction and placebo but not the CI gate.
  • Injury & depth deficiency tracking — 326-row official register, per-unit absence aggregates, official-news starter changes (Jackson, Willis/McCord, Darnold/Lock, Watson, Beck). The league publishes no depth chart, so "2nd/3rd/4th string" is blank and flagged — never inferred.
  • Decision layer — 16 requirements with status, 20 questions split known/unknown/answer/unlock, 16 suggestions with impact/effort/risk for reviewer selection.

What is blocked, stated plainly

  1. Market-priced bets (S1-S4). The NFL publishes no betting lines; no licensed feed exists here. All market columns are blank. Gate G6 cannot be evaluated.
  2. Player props and prop combos (S5-S6). No official per-game player logs in scope; no prop prices exist officially. Nothing graded.
  3. String-level depth (2nd/3rd/4th). No league depth chart; no snap counts. Absence-based tracking only.

Recommended path (reviewer decides on the suggestions board)

  1. Freeze model v1 and grade Weeks 11-18 forward (SUG-06) — free, and it is the only way to grow the holdout.
  2. Decide on a licensed odds feed (SUG-01) — it unlocks every betting-market requirement.
  3. Sample-extract official game books for player stats (SUG-02) — it unlocks props and depth tiers.
  4. Pick one canonical dashboard among the 12 NFL workspaces (SUG-12).
