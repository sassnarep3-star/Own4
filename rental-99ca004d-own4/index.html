Bottom line (verifiable against official sources)

  • Both units are in a building built in 1940, so they fall under the San Francisco Rent Ordinance (units built on or before June 13, 1979 have rent control and eviction protection). source
  • The current allowable annual increase under the SF Rent Ordinance is 1.6%, effective March 1, 2026 through February 28, 2027 (60% of the Bay Area CPI-U increase). source
  • Because local rent control (1.6%) is below the state threshold (5% + CPI), the California AB 1482 rent cap does not further limit these units. source
  • Maximum compliant increase on current base rent: Unit 1 = $63.92 (new rent $4,058.92); Unit 2 = $50.40 (new rent $3,200.40). Rent increases may not be rounded up. Unit 1 • Unit 2
  • One increase per 12 months; written notice required (30 days; 90 days if the increase exceeds 10% combined in 12 months; +5 days if mailed). source
  • Applying the same documented annual allowable increase to both units is the lowest-discrimination-risk approach. source
  • San Francisco tenants have access to free or low-cost legal help (e.g., Eviction Defense Collaborative, Rent Board counselor). Any increase should be able to withstand review by an SF tenant lawyer. source
