What was built

A fully autonomous, source-verified research pipeline and GitHub Pages dashboard that collects official NBA data, originates our own fair NBA lines, researches single-bet / parlay / player-prop strategies, measures correlations, passes every strategy through validation gates, removes luck via out-of-sample and permutation testing, and runs a leaderboard competition with rewards. Every table row carries a link to an official site; unconfirmable values are blank and flagged on a dedicated Flagged for Review page.

Most important findings

  1. In-sample success is not skill. Our prior-season rating model

(official 2025-26 advanced table, 82 GP per team) picked 0.780 of training-window winners but only 0.476 of the chronological holdout (n=21); holdout binomial p = 1.0000, Brier skill score -0.064 — official settled scores. It is classified NOT skill-based and receives no reward.

  1. A naive baseline beat the model. Picking the team with the better official 2025-26 record

was correct 0.710 of the time vs the model's 0.677 over 62 games.

  1. Team strength is highly stable within a season but only moderately across seasons.

2025-26 WIN% ~ NetRtg r = 0.971; the true out-of-sample 2026-27 WIN% ~ 2025-26 NetRtg r = 0.480 (BH q = 0.0145) — official source.

  1. Parlay legs are not independent. 2026-27 PTS ~ NetRtg r =

0.624 and PTS ~ PACE r = 0.400. Same-game parlay/SGP prices require a joint model that cannot be verified here, so they are left blank.

  1. Prop combos are data-limited. Leg correlations across 28 official team-leader baselines

are near zero and not significant, but game-level player logs are unavailable from free official NBA pages, so fair prop lines are blank and UNVERIFIED — official player table.

Decisions

  • No betting strategy is marked validated and no Division B reward is awarded. This is a

deliberate result of the validation gates, not a missing feature.

  • No market odds, vig, ROI, closing-line value or edge appears anywhere. The

NBA's own release says official betting data is distributed to licensed operators; the NBA.com games page exposes no odds values in retrieved content. Blank + flagged, per the no-hallucination rule.

  • Four out-of-sample research signals are ranked in Division A (100 / 60 / 30 / 10 pts).

These are predictive associations, not betting profits.

  • 17 irregularities including the 62/92 game coverage gap, /null player links on NBA.com,

unavailable official CDN JSON, and the platform's single-branch constraint are published on the Flagged for Review page and do not block the merge.
