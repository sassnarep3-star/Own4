Competition / Backtest & luck

Backtest and luck removal

Luck cannot be removed. It can be bounded, and this page shows the bounds used here.

How the backtest is run. Ratings are refitted before every season using only earlier seasons, so no forecast can see its own game. The holdout starts in 2017. The permutation tests use a frozen seed, so they reproduce.

The inference trap this page is built to avoid

  • With nineteen entries graded, the chance of at least one p < 0.05 result by luck alone is about 62%. That is why every p is corrected with Benjamini-Hochberg (gate G4).
  • A 55% edge needs roughly 2,300 graded decisions to be detected at 80% power. Most of the strategies here grade in the hundreds.
  • Splitting the holdout in half exposes rules that only work in one window. Two of the strongest-looking entries fail that test.
  • The only genuine cure is prospective replication (gate G12): a timestamped prediction, written before kickoff, that cannot be revised afterwards.
