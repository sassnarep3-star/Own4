Pre-registration. The rules, thresholds and the twelve gates were fixed before any holdout result was seen; the git history of nfl-own/engine/analysis.py shows the order. The model's two free parameters were selected on the dev window (forecast seasons 2002–2010) only, and that grid is published on the validation page.
