A leaderboard competition where points are paid for passing gates, never for a lucky hit rate. Fifty points per gate, paid only on a twelve-of-twelve finish, so the maximum on offer across 19 entries is 11,400 points.

Why the leaderboard exists at all

  • It makes a strategy prove itself before anyone acts on it, which is the opposite of how picks are usually sold.
  • It prices the cost of multiple testing: nineteen entries were graded, so a single significant result would be expected by chance (see the luck page).
  • It keeps the arithmetic honest: because no price exists in scope, entries are graded on hit rates against a stated break-even, never on invented profits.
