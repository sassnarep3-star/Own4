Q06 · How big must the sample be before a strategy can be believed? answered

PartContentSource

What we do not knowWhether any genuine edge exists at all in public data.official
The answerThe 300-decision gate G1 is a floor, not a sufficient sample.repo / archive
What would unlock itNothing - this is arithmetic.official

Q07 · Can luck be removed entirely? open

PartContentSource
What we knowNo. It can only be bounded: pre-registration, holdout, clustered intervals, placebo tests, FDR control and prospective replication.official
What we do not knowThe true edge of any strategy until prospective replication exists.official
The answerG12 (prospective replication) is the only gate that genuinely removes hindsight.repo / archive
What would unlock itRun the leaderboard forward with timestamped predictions.official

Status: open.
