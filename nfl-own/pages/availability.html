Injuries and depth: what the league actually publishes

The official injury report gives practice status and game status per player. It does not say who the backup is, and the league publishes no depth chart at all.

The 2nd/3rd/4th-string question cannot be answered from official sources. The club depth-chart URL returns a 404 for every club tried (proof: New England depth chart → 404), and the official roster page lists players, numbers, positions and roster status but no depth rank. So this site publishes unavailability by club and position, and never asserts that a named player is a backup. Suggestion S-02 is the only route to the real answer: licence a depth chart and snap counts.
