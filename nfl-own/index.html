What changed in this session

  • The official snapshot was re-read from nfl.com on 2026-11-13: the Week 10 Thursday game is now final (WAS 24, NYG 21), so the league has played 137 games, not 136.
  • Player props are not blocked. nfl.com publishes a full per-game log for every player, and the logs sum exactly to the published season totals. Three earlier NFL sessions recorded props as blocked; that was wrong (flag F-01).
  • Eleven team-stat columns were omitted rather than guessed, because the retrieved tables carried no readable header row. Four that could be proven by arithmetic are published.
  • Nineteen strategies were registered and nineteen were rejected. Two failed the split-half retest after passing their headline sample gate.
  • The archive's spread sign convention was established empirically, not assumed; an earlier draft of this session had it backwards and produced impossible 80% hit rates. That error is documented in the repo review.
