Price-snapshot store

One row per originated market per game: opening snapshot at origination, closing snapshot at settlement. Markets are originated in order: (1) side/spread, (2) total, (3) moneyline.

The NFL publishes no betting lines, odds or prices anywhere on nfl.com or operations.nfl.com (re-checked 2026-11-15). Every market_open / market_close cell is therefore blank and flagged, never invented. model_fair_line and diff columns are originated model outputs, labeled as such, and are not prices you can bet at.
