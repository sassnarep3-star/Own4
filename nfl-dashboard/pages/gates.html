Demotion gate DG-1 (live → paper)

A strategy marked LIVE is demoted to PAPER when any auto-firing condition triggers, evaluated weekly after results settle.

  • DG-1a: Brier skill vs baseline turns negative over the rolling 4-week window
  • DG-1b: realized CLV at executable price is negative for 4 consecutive weeks
  • DG-1c: maximum drawdown exceeds the pre-registered limit (flagged: no limit set yet)
  • DG-1d: any input source fails the drift alarm (missing field / row-count breach) for 2 consecutive captures
