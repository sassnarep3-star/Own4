Executive summary

One page: what we know, what we do not know, what we decided, and what must happen next. Every figure is source-linked or blank.

Known (verified from official sources)

  • All 32 teams' 2026 standings as of 2026-11-15, re-read cell-by-cell from nfl.com/standings; league identity ΣPF = ΣPA = 6,664 verified by the build script.
  • Week 10 schedule and scores: 14 games (10 final, 2 live, 1 halftime, 1 upcoming), 4 byes (CHI, DEN, PHI, TB), from nfl.com/schedules/2026/by-week/week-10.
  • Week 11 schedule: 13 upcoming games + 6 byes (ATL, CLE, GB, LAR, NE, SEA), from nfl.com/schedules/2026/by-week/week-11.
  • Week 10 injury report designations, published as rendered; team-misattribution irregularity flagged (F-02).
  • No betting price exists anywhere on nfl.com or operations.nfl.com; this absence is documented (F-01), not worked around.
  • Pre-registration discipline: 7 strategy definitions frozen before results; git commit hash is the registration.

Not known (blank, never inferred)

  • Market open/close lines, moneylines and prices (F-01).
  • Replacement depth rank and replacement name for every out starter (F-03): no official depth chart is published.
  • Designation deltas (Wednesday→Friday→kickoff): only one injury snapshot exists (F-04).
  • Per-designation play rate with confidence intervals (Q04): requires multiple seasons of designations joined to official inactives (n = 0 today).
  • Whether injury designations change outcomes (Q06): no archived injury reports exist.
  • Any strategy edge: 0 of 7 strategies qualify; 0 reward points paid.

Decided

  • Signal and staking are ranked in separate tables (no lucky staking run can mask bad signal).
  • Same-game parlays and prop combos are blocked at the joint-evidence gate (no validated joint probability, no void/push rules).
  • Correlations use officially sourced numbers only, with n published.
  • Injury enters as a surprise (delta), never a level — policy recorded, implementation pending (S-09).
  • Depth is never derived from jersey number, experience or salary.
  • No backtest numbers appear on the leaderboard; 0 strategies pass the gates.

What would change our mind

  • A licensed or official odds feed (unblocks G7, ROI/CLV grading, strategy qualification).
  • Timestamped Wed/Thu/Fri injury snapshots with retained hashes (unblocks Q03, Q15, surprise injury features).
  • A verified depth source for all 32 clubs (unblocks Q05, replacement-depth features).
  • Graded out-of-sample results against each frozen strategy definition (gates G1-G10).
  • Funded, written reward terms before any points are paid.
