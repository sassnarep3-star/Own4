7 · Method and limits

  • Local checks are exhaustive for the published tree (all pages, all links, all rows); external checks are a bounded, declared sample of 14 URLs out of 1,682 unique citations — not a full crawl.
  • Standings verification covers every published cell of six pages plus a label test on a seventh; other data domains (team stats, player leaders, game rows) were covered by earlier sessions' verifiers and were not re-derived here.
  • The nflverse archive is third-party; it is used only as a cross-check against official pages that were re-read directly, and it is labelled as third-party wherever cited.
  • No market price, odds, or betting line is treated as official anywhere in this report: the NFL publishes no betting lines.
