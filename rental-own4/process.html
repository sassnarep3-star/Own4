Unreasonable / unlawful “reasons” (do not use)

What fails — each is a red flag a tenant lawyer will spot instantly
“Reason”Why it failsSource
“The market rent down the street is higher” (continuing tenancy)Costa-Hawkins resets initial rent only on vacancy; mid-tenancy market comparison requires a comparable-unit petition, not unilateral notice.S9 ↗ S6 ↗
“A new roommate moved in”Expressly unlawful — an additional occupant is not an increase ground, even if the lease says so.S1 ↗
“I need more income / property taxes rose” (no petition)Generic financial need is not a ground; O&M/capital increases exist only by petition with proof.S6 ↗
“To make the tenant move out” / right after a habitability or organizing complaintRetaliation: unlawful; presumption within 180 days; civil liability and fee-shifting.S14 ↗
“Only Unit 1 — tenants there complained/have Section 8/are a family/are older” etc.Discriminatory treatment (source of income, familial status, disability, age, race, etc.) under FEHA/Unruh/FHA — liability regardless of amount.S12 ↗ S11 ↗ S13 ↗
“Rounding to a clean number”SF bars rounding up; $55.93 increase ≠ $56.00.S1 ↗
“Notice given late / by text message”Notice must be written, ≥30/90 days, personally delivered or mailed (+5 days); service defects invalidate the increase.S8 ↗ S1 ↗
“We waived the cap in the lease”Anti-waiver: rights under the AB 1482 regime and the Ordinance cannot be waived; waiver is void.S10 ↗

Reasonable notice package for the two units

  • Unit 1: 30-day notice, 1.6%, effective Feb 1, 2027 (keeps anniversary) — or 30 days from service for an effective date on/after Mar 1, 2026.
  • Unit 2: 30-day notice, 1.6%, effective June 1, 2027 — or the same for an earlier effective date in the 2026-27 window. If Option C (13.1%) is ever elected: 90-day notice, itemized banking years, license, rent history file ready for petition defense.
  • Attach no threats, no “or you must vacate” language, no references to protected characteristics. Keep tone neutral and businesslike.
