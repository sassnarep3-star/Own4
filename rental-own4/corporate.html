What this means for these two units

Recommended posture mirrors best individual-owner practice, hardened with corporate discipline: annual 1.6% on both units, license filed, notices templated and itemized, rent history compiled into a single audit file, and a written policy that both units are always increased on the same formula and date. That combination is indistinguishable from what a careful corporate landlord does, is trivial to explain to counsel, and leaves the 13.1% Unit 2 bank as a documented, deliberate choice rather than a surprise.
