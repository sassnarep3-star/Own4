Interpretation

  • “Reasonable and normal” in this market, for these units, is defined by law, not by vibes: 1.4–1.6% per year, once a year, CPI-formula, base-rent math. Anything above that requires a petition (capital, O&M, utilities) or a banked-increase disclosure.
  • National rent-price growth (~+2.8% reported; shelter +3.2% verified) exceeds the SF cap, so the cap — not the market — is the binding constraint, by legislative design.
  • Both current base rents ($3,995.00; $3,150.00) are plausible for Outer Sunset rent-controlled units of their size; confirming them against any official benchmark is impossible here and is flagged, not guessed.
  • Because the local cap is below inflation, the “economic” loss per year on Unit 2 alone is the difference between +1.6% (+$50.40) and +3.2% shelter inflation (≈ +$100.80) — an argument for, not a license to exceed, the law. Do not self-help.
