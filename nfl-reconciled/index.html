The three numbers this repository had wrong

1. The season is 148 games, not 136 and not 137

Every stored results feed says 136 or 137 games. The official standings settle it without anyone’s opinion: the 32 clubs’ published W+L+T sum to 296 club-games, so the league has played 148 games. No feed reaches that figure: each stops at the Thursday Week-10 final, and the remaining Week-10 finals are still on the official schedule page.

official ↗ · sum of W+L+T over all 32 clubs ÷ 2

2. The data they do hold is correct — and that is provable

Take the weeks-1-to-9 corpus (the 136-game log the brief names): it holds 6,246 points. The 12 official Week-10 finals add 578. Together that is 6,824, which is exactly the league’s own published total points scored — and the official Points-For total equals the official Points-Against total to the point. Zero points are unaccounted for, so the stored scores are not merely incomplete: they are consistent with every point the league credits.

6,246 + 578 = 6,824 = official 6,824 · official ↗

3. The availability capture that can be checked, passes

The repository holds 8 Week-10 availability captures with row counts from 98 to 347. Two of them hold 347 rows over 28 clubs, and both reproduce the live official table exactly where this session counted it: 8 of 8 club row counts and 18 of 18 cells. That is why 347 is the canonical figure — not because it is the largest.

What this page does not do

It does not merge, retire or edit a single workspace

Deleting 30 workspaces is the owner’s decision, not a session’s, and each one is somebody’s auditable work. What this page does instead is remove the ambiguity: for every fact that matters, it names the canonical value and the official page behind it.

The cost of leaving the duplicates in place is open item RR-01 on the decisions page, with the exact action that would retire them.
