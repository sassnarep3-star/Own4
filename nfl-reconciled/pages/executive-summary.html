Executive summary

One page for a reader who has five minutes. Every number below is the same number the other pages publish, computed by the same engines; the markdown twin is EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md.

The one thing to read

This repository had 31 NFL workspaces publishing 430 pages, at least fourteen of them presenting themselves as the place to start. They were each honest and they did not agree with each other. Nothing said which figure to believe. This workspace settles that: it states the canonical number for every contested fact, with the official page behind it, and leaves the rest to you.

No workspace was edited, moved or deleted. What was removed is the ambiguity, not anybody’s work.
