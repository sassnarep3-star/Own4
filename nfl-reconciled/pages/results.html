Results corpus against the league

A results feed is either complete or it is not, and the league’s own standings decide which. This page scores every stored corpus against two identities that need no modelling: games played (sum of every club’s W+L+T, ÷2) and total points (sum of the official PF column).

No feed matches all 32 clubs because every feed stops before the Week-10 Sunday games. The 10 clubs that do match are the ones whose Week-10 game had not been played when the feed was frozen.

The closure: the gap is exactly the Week-10 finals

Per-club shortfalls are not uniform noise — they are one missing game per club. Summing the deficit over the 22 clubs that are short gives 533 points. The 12 Week-10 fixtures the official schedule page prints as FINAL add 578 points. The two figures are identical, and all 12 scores match the per-club deficits club by club.
