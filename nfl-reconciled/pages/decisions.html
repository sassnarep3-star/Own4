RC-11 · Market prices

What the workspaces disagree about

Every workspace publishes blank market columns and blames a missing official price source; three of them disagree on whether a third-party price may be shown at all.

Why no official source decides it

The NFL publishes no betting price, so no official number exists to settle this. Whether a licensed or third-party price may be used is a rights decision, not a data decision.

What it blocks

ROI, closing-line value, rewards, gate G7, every leaderboard tier.

What would unblock it

One written owner verdict on acceptable price sources plus the provider's terms. Until then every price cell stays blank.

Evidence: nfl-availability-desk/data/questions.json · nfl-book/data/suggestions.json · nfl-verify/data/questions.json

RC-12 · Ordered depth rank (1st/2nd/3rd/4th string)

What the workspaces disagree about

Workspaces publish a 32-club depth register, a per-team replacement view and a matchup matrix, while others state no official ordered depth exists to store.

Why no official source decides it

The league publishes no league-wide ordered depth chart. Club pages exist but vary (one publishes an image, one prepends jersey numbers, some list several players in one slot), so no single official source settles a rank for all 32 clubs.

What it blocks

The backup-deficiency feature, the matchup matrix, and any 'replacement at depth d' claim.

What would unblock it

A club-by-club verification route, or an official snap-count feed, verified for all 32 clubs before any depth-dependent number is ranked.

Evidence: nfl-book/data/depth_register.json · nfl-book/data/matchup_matrix.json

RC-13 · Player props and parlays

What the workspaces disagree about

Workspaces publish prop and same-game-parlay structures with no prices; others publish correlations with n=0.

Why no official source decides it

No official price and no official per-game player log exist under the official-source rule, so neither a prop price nor a prop correlation can be computed from an official source.

What it blocks

Prop combos, same-game parlays, joint-evidence gate.

What would unblock it

Same verdict as RC-11, plus an official per-game player-stat source.

Evidence: nfl-book/data/correlations.json · nfl-book/data/joint_evidence.json

RC-14 · The 2026 kickoff / onside-kick regime priors

What the workspaces disagree about

Two workspaces publish different 2026 rule priors as model inputs.

Why no official source decides it

This session did not read the 2026 rulebook, so it does not assert what the 2026 kickoff or onside-kick rules are. Asserting either would be a guess.

What it blocks

Kickoff-adjacent totals and 4th-quarter win-probability curves.

What would unblock it

Someone must read the official 2026 rulebook section and freeze the rule text with a commit hash before it is used as a prior.

Evidence: nfl-book/data/model_outputs.json

RR-01 · 31 workspaces exist because no rule said where work must land

The position

This session has removed the ambiguity about which numbers are right, but not the duplication itself. 31 workspaces still publish 429 pages, and 12 of them run no CI gate. Every future session that reads the brief and refuses to overwrite a peer will add one more.

What would unblock it

Pick one canonical namespace and state it in COORDINATION.md. After that, a new session lands its work inside that namespace or writes to the owner instead of creating a sibling. Nothing needs deleting on day one: the duplicates become harmless once readers are sent to one place, which is what this page does.

Evidence: nfl-reconciled/data/derived/namespace_index.json, nfl-reconciled/data/derived/register_duplication.json
