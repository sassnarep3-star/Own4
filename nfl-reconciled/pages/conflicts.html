RC-01 · Games in the results corpus SETTLED

What the workspaces say

Five workspaces publish a season corpus: nfl-book 137 rows, nfl-command 137, nfl-ledger 137, nfl-decision 136, nfl-edge 136. The brief calls it 'the 136-game log'.

The answer

148

148 games have been played. Weeks 1-9 account for 136 of them and Week 10 for 12, and 136 + 12 = 148 is what the official standings force. '136' is therefore correct for weeks 1-9 only; '137' is the same corpus plus the Thursday Week-10 final; neither is the season. A feed whose file says 137 while its coverage note says 'through week 9' is mislabelled, not wrong.

Why that is the answer

Sum of every club's W+L+T = 296 club-games; 296 / 2 = 148 games played.

Official source read 2026-11-16: official ↗

Repository evidence: nfl-book/data/derived/results_corpus_reconciliation.json

What it affects: Every rating, backtest and leaderboard that says it used the full season.

RC-02 · Is the stored score data correct, not just incomplete? SETTLED

What the workspaces say

Five corpora hold 136-137 games and none states whether the games it does hold are right.

The answer

6824

The weeks 1-9 data is correct. The official Week-10 finals add exactly 578 points; the corpora contain 6,246 points; 6,246 + 578 = 6,824, which is the league's own published Points-For total, and Points-For equals Points-Against league-wide exactly. Zero points are unaccounted for, so no stored score is contradicted by the league's totals.

Why that is the answer

Sum of the official PF column = 6,824 = sum of the official PA column.
