Read this before the numbers

No price on this site is official. The NFL publishes no odds anywhere, which was checked again this session. Every spread, total and moneyline comes from one commercial book, syndicated through a public endpoint, archived with its URL and read time. It is traceable. It is not official, and it is not a market — it is one book.

Thirteen of fourteen week-10 games have not been played. Their "closing" number is the provider's current number and will keep moving. Only one game has a true open-to-close pair.

Nothing has ever been staked. Zero of fifteen strategies pass the signal gates, zero pass both tables, and no money has been at risk anywhere in this repository at any point.
