Replacement depth, observed rather than ranked

The requirement asks to track the depth of the replacement, not just the fact that one happened. Three official documents make that possible: the injury report says who is out, and the Game Book prints who actually started, who came off the bench, who did not play and who was inactive. Comparing two consecutive weeks for one club is what turns a name change into a measured replacement.

What "replacement starts: 1 of 2" means. N.Allegretti was not the week-9 starter at C and had 1 of 2 starts in the two graded games before taking the job, so the club filled the position from inside rather than from the top of a known order. That is a fact about who played. It is not a rank, and it is the only kind of depth fact an official document supports.
