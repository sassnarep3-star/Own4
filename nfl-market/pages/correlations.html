Correlations, officially sourced only

Only values that both sides of the correlation can be sourced for are computed. Each row names which side is official and which is third-party. A row where either side is missing is printed blank with the reason, never filled in. n is published on every row, always.

Most rows here are blank on purpose. Two of four correlations can be computed from values that are official on both sides. One cannot be computed at all yet because only one game has been played. One is cited from a prior session rather than recomputed, and says so. Printing a coefficient without its n is not allowed anywhere in this table.

Why the control row matters

MC-02 correlates wins with point differential. It must be strongly positive; if it were not, the standings transcription would be wrong. It is a data-integrity check wearing the clothes of a correlation, and publishing it is how a reader can see that the transcriptions are sound before trusting the rows that are actually interesting.
