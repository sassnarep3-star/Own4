The five findings that matter

1. The store works; the prices in it are one book's, not a market

All 84 rows reconcile: the spread sign agrees with the provider's own details string, both two-way pairs de-vig to exactly 1.0, every total is plausible, and every row carries its URL. Two real transcription errors were caught by those checks before publication — four flipped spreads, and four games attached to another game's prices by relying on list order. But the whole store is DraftKings syndicated through one endpoint. The NFL publishes no odds. Dispersion across books, which is where any retail edge is claimed to live, cannot be seen at all.

2. The results feed is genuinely official, and it is the only part that is

Sixteen official finals are transcribed, each with the page it was read from. One week-9 game renders "Hidden Score" on its own game page and was recovered from the official Game Book PDF. That asymmetry — official scores, third-party prices — is the single most important structural fact about this site, and it is why the flag page exists.

3. The split leaderboard answers a question that a single table cannot

Fifteen strategies. Five were profitable over the full sample. Zero can be distinguished from luck after correcting for fifteen tests, and zero pass both tables. That combination is the most common way a betting system fools its author, and the two-table layout makes it visible in one screen instead of buried across two papers.

4. Replacement depth is measurable after the game and not before

Two official Game Books for one club produced one confirmed replacement and one rotation change, with the incoming player's starts and appearances in the window. The replacement had one start in the two graded games and took the job in the second, which is the depth evidence. No rank is claimed anywhere, because the NFL publishes no depth chart; the depth-chart route returns a 404, re-read this session. This is the requirement most affected by a league publication decision, and it cannot be engineered around.

5. The demotion gate is the safest thing on the site

Gate DG-1 is one-directional: it can only remove a strategy, so applying it today to results that already existed introduces no lookahead. Under it, zero of fifteen stay live. That the answer was known in advance is the point — it is a specification with a testable history, not a promise.
