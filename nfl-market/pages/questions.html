Decisions, questions, and what would settle them

Read the five decisions first. Every decision has options, the evidence for each, and the cost of waiting; none of them requires code. Then read the questions: each one states what is known, what is not, and what would settle it. Suggestions are last, because most of them depend on a decision above them.

NFL market lab — price-snapshot store, verified results feed, origination order, split leaderboard, replacement depth, official-only correlations, and a demotion gate. Analytical research only; no wagering advice; nothing here has ever been staked.

If gambling is a problem: National Council on Problem Gambling, ncpgambling.org, 1-800-MY-RESET (1-800-697-3738).

No price on this site is an official league price. Scores, standings, injuries and game-day lineups come from the NFL; every spread, total and moneyline comes from one commercial book syndicated through a public endpoint.

Every table row carries a source link. Values that could not be confirmed from an official source are blank and listed on the Flagged for review page.
