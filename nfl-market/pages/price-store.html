Price-snapshot store

Every market we originate is recorded twice: once at the opening number the provider publishes, and once at its latest number. Both are stored with the exact endpoint URL they came from and the time they were read. The parser reads only the archive; it never fetches, so a row that is not in the archive cannot appear on the page.

Read the units before the numbers. For the side, the open/latest pair is the home handicap, and a positive move means the home side became more of an underdog. For the total it is the total, and a positive move means the number went up. For the moneyline the pair is the away price and the move is in percentage points of implied probability, not cents — the two are not the same unit, and mixing them is how a modest move gets described as enormous.
