The headline finding. On 4,055 out-of-sample games the correlation between (my line − market line) and the actual result against the market spread is r = 0.0072 with a 95% confidence interval of [-0.024, 0.038]. That interval contains zero. The originated lines are close to the market but not better than it, and no strategy built on them clears the validation gates. This is the result the system was built to produce: a reproducible "not yet" instead of an unverifiable "yes".

How a number gets onto this site

  1. Official NFL.com pages are fetched and read, then transcribed into data/official/*.json.
  2. The third-party game archive is spot-checked against those official pages before it is used (45 of 45 games matched; Week 5 could not be read and is flagged).
  3. Model parameters are written to data/model_params.json with a registration timestamp before the first run, so they cannot be tuned to the results.
  4. Every forecast is refitted using only games that finished earlier, so no future information can leak.
  5. Every strategy is scored, tested against a no-skill simulation, corrected for multiple comparisons, and then run through eight gates before it can be called reward-eligible.
  6. Anything that cannot be sourced is left blank and flagged.
