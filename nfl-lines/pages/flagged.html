IR-01HIGHOfficial dataNFL.com rendered Week 5 2026 scores as 'Hidden Score'
The official score page https://www.nfl.com/scores/2026/REG5 displayed every Week 5 2026 game with the literal text 'Hidden Score' and status 'Final'. No numeric value could be read, so no comparison against the archive was possible for that week.
Effect
Week 5 2026 is unverified against the primary source. It remains in the dataset on the strength of the 45 of 45 games that could be checked.

IR-02HIGHMarket dataNo official NFL source publishes betting lines
The NFL does not publish spreads, totals, moneylines or prop lines. Every market number on this site comes from a third-party archive pinned to a commit and recorded in data/provenance.json.
Effect
Every market line on the site is labelled third-party. No market figure may be presented as a verified fact.

IR-03HIGHInjuriesDepth-chart rank is not published in the official injury report
The official report gives Player / Position / Injury / Practice Status / Game Status. It never says whether a player is a starter, a 2nd, 3rd or 4th string replacement. Every depth-rank cell on this site is therefore blank.
Effect
The requirement to 'know deficiencies such as 2nd, 3rd or 4th string backups replacing starters' cannot be met from the official injury report alone.
needs reviewwww.nfl.com/injuries/
IR-04HIGHPlayer propsNo verifiable player-prop data or prop prices were available
Prop combos are specified as an engine (correlation-aware joint pricing, gates, staking) but no official or verifiable source for player prop lines or player game logs was reachable in this session.
Effect
Every player-prop value on the props page is blank. The team-level combo (favourite covers and over) is fully populated because both legs have recorded prices.

IR-08MEDIUMModel specificationLinear rest term produces a large bye-week effect
The fitted rest coefficient is 0.71 points of home margin per day of rest differential. For Week 10 PIT @ CIN, Pittsburgh's bye (14 days vs Cincinnati's 7) contributes 4.94 points, which is the main reason the model's line sits 7.06 points away from the market.
Effect
Any own line involving a team coming off a bye should be treated as provisional.

IR-09MEDIUMRepositoryEleven overlapping NFL namespaces exist in this repository
The repository now contains eleven directories whose names begin with nfl: nfl/, nfl-lab/, nfl-quant/, nfl-research/, nfl-decision/, nfl-decisions/, nfl-edge/, nfl-hub/, nfl-intel/, nfl-audit/ and this one, nfl-lines/.
