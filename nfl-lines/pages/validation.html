Validation gates

Eight pre-declared gates. All must pass before a strategy can be called reward-eligible.

Eight gates, declared before the results were seen. A strategy must pass all eight to be reward-eligible. The gates are deliberately strict: the point is not to find something that worked once, it is to avoid rewarding luck.

Why nothing is eligible

The binding constraints are G3 (significance after correcting for testing 15 strategies at once) and G4 (must beat the 95th percentile of a no-skill simulation at the same prices). Every strategy that made money did so by an amount well inside the range that chance alone produces at its sample size.
