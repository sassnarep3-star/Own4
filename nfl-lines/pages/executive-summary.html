What was built

An end-to-end line-origination and validation system for the NFL, run on 2026 Week 10 data. It originates its own spread, total, team totals, win probability and moneyline for every game; measures the disagreement against the market; turns that disagreement into bets across singles, parlays and a same-game combo; and then refuses to reward any of them until they pass eight pre-declared gates. The injury report is a frozen Week 10 snapshot, retrieved 2026-11-12 00:00 UTC; it is not a live feed and must be refreshed before use.

What the evidence says

  • The originated lines track the market but do not beat it. The correlation between my line and the actual margin is 0.3384; the market's is 0.4393. On totals, mine is 0.1942 against the market's 0.3135. Mean absolute error on the spread is 10.56 points against the market's 10.01.
  • The disagreement carries no information. If the model had skill, a bigger gap between my line and the closing line would produce a bigger cover margin. Measured: r = 0.0072, 95% CI [-0.0236, 0.038], p = 0.645059 over 4055 games. The interval contains zero.
  • The probabilities are honest but not sharp enough. Brier score 0.22652 against the market's 0.21059 and a naive baseline of 0.24729. The model is well calibrated (weighted reliability error 0.00088) — when it says 65%, teams win about 67% of the time — it simply does not know anything the market does not.
  • No strategy has demonstrated skill. 15 strategies were tested. The best by return on investment, unders in windy outdoor games, made +7.77% over 303 bets; the no-skill simulation puts 95% of luck-only outcomes between −11.08% and +10.82%. It sits inside that band, and its p-value rises from 0.17 to 0.44 once you correct for having tested 15 strategies.
  • Nothing is reward-eligible, and that is the intended output. The gates exist so that a strategy cannot be rewarded for being lucky.

The three gaps that matter most

  1. Quarterback and injury information is not in the model. Week 10 MIA @ IND: the market total is 38.5, mine is 46.68, an 8.18-point gap. The official injury page the same day reports Miami placing its quarterback on injured reserve with a first-time starter in line. The model does not know that. Source: nfl.com/injuries.
  2. Depth-chart rank is not published. The official report gives position and status, never whether a player is a starter or a 2nd, 3rd or 4th string replacement. Every depth-rank cell is blank by design.
  3. Only closing lines exist. Every edge here is measured against a price that is already final. If the model's advantage lives in an earlier number, the current evaluation cannot see it.
