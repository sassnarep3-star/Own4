Prop combos

Same-game and player-prop combinations, priced with measured correlation instead of assumed independence.

Player props: no verifiable data exists in the sources available to this session. The NFL publishes no player prop lines, and no verifiable archive of player game logs or prop prices was reachable. Every player-prop value on this page is therefore left blank and flagged. What is populated is the engine: the correlation-aware pricing method, the gates, and one fully measurable team-level combo, because both of its legs have recorded prices.

How the combo is priced

The naive way to price a same-game parlay is to multiply the legs together, which assumes the legs are independent. They are not. The pricer here instead estimates the joint probability from history, using only games that finished before the one being priced, and reports the difference. That difference is the correlation adjustment, measured rather than assumed.
