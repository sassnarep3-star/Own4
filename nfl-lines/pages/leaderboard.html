Nothing is reward-eligible. Zero of 15 strategies clear all eight gates. The leaderboard ranks them anyway, because the ranking shows how far each one is from qualifying and which gate is binding.
Why rank by z-score and not by return. Ranking by ROI would simply rank whoever got lucky on the smallest sample. The z-score measures how far the observed win rate sits from the break-even rate implied by the prices actually taken, in standard errors. It still cannot manufacture skill that is not there — it just stops the ranking from rewarding noise.

Reward policy

  • A strategy is reward-eligible only when it passes all eight gates.
  • Rewards are ranked on z-score against break-even, never on raw ROI.
  • The reward schedule itself must be written down and funded before the first strategy becomes eligible (suggestion SG-12), so the incentive cannot be retro-fitted to whoever happens to be leading.
  • Every submitted strategy must commit its parameters before its first run (SG-07), exactly as the house model does in data/model_params.json.
