Suggestions

Ranked improvements to the site and the system. Review, choose, then implement — in that order.

Nothing on this page is approved. Every suggestion is a proposal waiting for review. Each carries the evidence behind it and the test that would show it worked, so you can choose a path before anything is implemented.

SG-01P1ModelSuggestion
Feed the official injury report into the forecast, starting with one feature: whether the club's most-used QB earlier in the season is the named starter.
Why
The single largest own-line disagreement in Week 10 (MIA @ IND, 8.18 points on the total) sits on a game where the official page states Miami's QB is on injured reserve and a first-time starter is in line.
Evidence
www.nfl.com/injuries/ , www.nfl.com/news/dolphins-place-qb-malik-willis-groin-on-injured-reserve-kyle-mccord-in-line-for-first-start
CR-01 (edge vs result) confidence interval moves away from zero, measured only on games after the feature is added.proposed - not approvedregister
SG-02P1Data acquisitionSuggestion
Acquire an official depth-chart source so 2nd/3rd/4th-string replacements can be identified.
Why
The requirement 'know any deficiencies such as 2nd, 3rd or 4th string backups having to replace starters' cannot be met from the injury report alone - it has no depth rank.
Evidence
www.nfl.com/injuries/
At least 90% of Week 10 players on the official injury report have an official depth rank attached.proposed - not approvedregister
SG-03P1EvaluationSuggestion
Acquire timestamped opening and closing lines so every edge is measured against a price that actually existed when the bet could be placed.
Why
Right now the model can only be measured against a closing line it has no hope of beating, which makes the whole evaluation pessimistic but also unverifiable in the optimistic direction.
Evidence
No official source exists; this is a commercial purchase.
Every graded bet carries both an opening and a closing price with timestamps.proposed - not approvedregister
SG-04P2ModelSuggestion
Replace the linear rest term with a categorical short-week / normal / bye feature and re-register the parameters before the next run.
Why
A linear 0.71 points per day produces a 4.9-point bye effect, which is the main driver of the largest spread disagreement in Week 10.
Evidence
data/derived/own_lines_week10.json (PIT @ CIN)
Re-registered parameter file committed before the next run; the bye effect is reported as a fitted category, not a slope.proposed - not approvedregister
SG-05P2VerificationSuggestion
Extend the official score spot-check from 3 weeks of 2026 to one week per season across the whole evaluation window.
Why
The archive is trusted on 45 games. The other 4,010 are unverified against a primary source.
Evidence
www.nfl.com/scores/2026/REG9 pattern applies to every season and week
At least one full week per season, every season, re-read from NFL.com with a published match rate.proposed - not approvedregister
SG-06P2SiteSuggestion

Evidence

SG-07P2CompetitionSuggestion
Require every submitted strategy to declare its parameters in a file committed BEFORE its first run, and reject any strategy whose parameters were changed after results were seen.
Why
This is the only mechanism that separates a strategy from a curve fit. It is already applied to the house model (data/model_params.json carries a registration timestamp).
Evidence
data/model_params.json
Every leaderboard row links to a parameter file whose commit precedes its first result.proposed - not approvedregister
SG-08P3ModelSuggestion
Add a player-prop module, but publish no numbers until a verifiable prop price history exists.
Why
The engine for prop combinations is specified here, but the NFL publishes no prop lines and no verifiable prop archive was available to this session, so every prop cell is blank by design.
Evidence
No official source; flagged IR-08
A prop strategy appears on the leaderboard with a sourced price for every leg.proposed - not approvedregister
SG-09P3OperationsSuggestion
Automate the weekly pipeline: fetch the official schedule, injury report and standings, regenerate the lines, re-run the gates, and publish - without manual input.
Why
Today the official pages are read and transcribed by hand. That does not scale past one week and introduces transcription risk.
Evidence
www.nfl.com/injuries/ , www.nfl.com/standings/league/2026/REG , www.nfl.com/schedules/2026/REG10
A scheduled run produces the Week 11 page with no human step.proposed - not approvedregister
