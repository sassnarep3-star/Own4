How to read the injury band

The official injury report gives a game status (OUT / DOUBTFUL / QUESTIONABLE) but not whether the player is a starter or how many snaps they would take — the NFL publishes no depth chart (404 at nfl.com/depth-charts/). The band therefore applies disclosed multipliers (0.25 / 0.50 / 1.00 pts per OUT / Doubtful / Questionable) at low / central / high and shows the range. The published line is the no-adjustment line; a wide band is an honest statement that the input cannot support precision (flag F-02 / F-05).
