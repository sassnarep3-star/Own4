Correlations

Correlations are computed only from officially sourced numbers (the official standings) and n is published with each value. Player-prop and same-game-parlay correlations are blank because the NFL publishes no official per-game player log and no odds (C6, C8 flagged).

Method

Pearson r, 95% CI via Fisher z, two-sided p. All inputs from nfl.com/standings (read 2026-11-14). A blank r is never an estimated value: it means the input cannot be obtained from an official source, and the reason is stated in the row below the table.
