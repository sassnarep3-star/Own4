Suggestions & decisions

A decision board, not a to-do list. Each suggestion carries the context, the options considered, the evidence available, the cost, the risk, its prerequisites and a recommendation — so a path can be chosen before anything is implemented.

The recommended path

Work in this order: the first group contains the decisions that gate everything else.

  1. Step 1 - Gate decisionsS02 Establish the operating jurisdiction and whether derived lines may be published, S07 Adopt a written skill definition before any reward is paid, S01 Buy or license an archived NFL odds history (opening + closing prices)
  2. Step 2 - Data decisionsS06 Obtain an archived same-game-parlay (SGP) price for correlated prop combos, S09 Establish whether an official 2026 player-level box-score feed exists, S08 Review the third-party archive licence before redistributing derived values
  3. Step 3 - Depth decisionsS03 Obtain an official depth chart / snap-count feed so replacement depth can be tracked, S05 Adopt a disclosed injury-to-points conversion (published as a band, not a point), S04 Begin a weekly injury snapshot archive

S01Buy or license an archived NFL odds history (opening + closing prices) Market data DECIDE NOW
Context
The price snapshot store (opener/closer per market) is an empty shell: the NFL publishes no odds, so every market_line is blank and Gate G7 can never pass. Without a price history nothing can be backtested as a bet, no ROI is computable, and no reward can be paid as anything but research points.
Options
  • Buy a licensed archived odds feed (e.g. the NFL's official sports-data distributors) and record opener + closer per market
  • Continue with assumed -110 only, clearly labelled as a forecast, not a bet
  • Do not operate a betting function at all; keep this as pure research
Evidence
nfl.com/standings and nfl.com/games carry no odds values; price_snapshot_store is blank for opener/closer on every market.
Cost
High (licence fee) if a real feed; nil if research-only.
Risk
High: operating without a settled legal position (S02) could expose the project; paying for a feed before the legal question is settled is wasted spend.
Prerequisites
S02
Recommendation
Settle S02 first, then decide S01. Until a real price exists, keep every line labelled 'forecast, not a price'.
Closes
