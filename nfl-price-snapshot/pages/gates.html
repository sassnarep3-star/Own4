The demotion gate (G11)

A LIVE strategy is demoted back to PAPER when (a) its week-clustered 95% CI on ROI includes zero for N=2 consecutive grading weeks, or (b) it fails FDR at q=0.05 on re-grade. A demoted strategy may not be staked live until it re-qualifies on a fresh holdout. One bad week does not demote; two consecutive CI-including-zero weeks do. No strategy is live this snapshot, so G11 is not triggered; the rule is published before any strategy goes live.
