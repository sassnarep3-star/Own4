How points would be awarded

  • The pool is 1,000 research points per snapshot — research points, not money: no jurisdiction, licence or funding source has been established (questions Q02, Q15).
  • A strategy must clear every non-blocked gate. A blocked gate blocks the whole pool.
  • Ties break on the widest lower confidence bound, not the highest point estimate — the conservative choice that resists luck.
  • Provisional awards require a forward record: the strategy's next week of lines must be committed before kick-off and graded after (suggestion S15 / S01).
