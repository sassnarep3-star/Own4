Price snapshot store

One record per market we originate (side / total / moneyline, per game). For each market we store the snapshot timestamp and our originated fair line. The opener and closer are the market's opening and closing numbers. The NFL publishes no betting odds, so opener and closer are blank for every market and flagged (F-01); nothing is inferred or filled in.

This store is an empty shell until a price exists

Once a licensed opening/closing price archive is available (suggestion S01), this schema is ready to hold it. Until then, every row carries the source that establishes the blank, and the store is dated so the first real snapshot can be compared against it.
