Executive summary

Snapshot 2026-11-14. What was verified, what could not be, and what has to be decided next.

The bottom line in one paragraph

All 32 official 2026 standings rows were read directly from nfl.com (sum W = sum L = 136; sum PF = sum PA = 6,291). The Week 10 schedule (14 games, 1 completed, 4 byes) and the Week 10 injury report were transcribed from official pages. A transparent rating model, fitted only on games played before the slate, originates a side, a total and a moneyline for each of the 13 unplayed games. But the NFL publishes no betting odds, so every market_line is blank, Gate G7 is blocked, 0 strategies qualify and 0 of 1,000 reward points are awarded. Nothing here is an edge; everything is a forecast, not a price.
