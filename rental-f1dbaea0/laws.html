How the three layers interact (control hierarchy)

  1. Federal floor: no cap; discrimination and collusion bans apply everywhere.
  2. State overlay (AB 1482): 5%+CPI/10% cap + just cause — applies unless a stricter local regime governs the unit; local law that is more protective continues to apply (SF.gov).
  3. Local (SF Rent Ordinance) — strictest, controls here: 1.6% annual allowable (2026-27), banking, license prerequisite, 30/90-day +5 mailed notice, 17 just-cause grounds, petition-based passthroughs; administered by the SF Rent Board.
  4. Private ordering: leases may not waive tenant rights (AB 1482 waivers void; waiver of retaliation protections void).
