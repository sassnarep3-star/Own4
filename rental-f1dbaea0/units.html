Why the two units show different numbers. The difference is entirely explained by different skip histories under the statutory formula — Unit 2 simply banked more years — not by the occupants of either unit. That distinction is what makes a uniform-method, document-everything approach the right answer to the discrimination question. See discrimination.html.
