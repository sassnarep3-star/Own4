What these checks can and cannot prove

They prove the transcribed official values are mutually consistent, and that the game archive reproduces every club’s record and points exactly. They cannot catch a typo that is internally consistent, and they do not certify that an outgoing link still resolves to the same content today. That gap is flag F-10 and suggestion S-16.
