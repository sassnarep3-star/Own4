Validation and luck

Ten gates, frozen before any result was produced, plus four separate bounds on luck. A strategy qualifies only if all ten pass. None does.

The ten pre-registered gates and how each strategy fared
GateNamePre-registered ruleWhy it existsResult by strategySource

G2Beats a naive baselineown-line MAE < naive baseline MAE for both spread and totalA model that cannot beat 'assume a 0-point margin / league-average total' has no information in it.S1_spread pass, S2_total pass, S3_moneyline pass, S4_parlay passgates.json ↗

Thresholds are design choices, not official NFL values (flag F-11). Tier policy: Qualified = all ten pass and earns points; Bronze = 5-9; Rejected = fewer than 5. Only Qualified earns any of the 1,000-point pool.

How luck is bounded

BoundMethodWhat it rules outWhere it entersSource
Week-clustered bootstrapResample weeks, not bets, 4,000 times; report the 2.5th and 97.5th percentile ROIBets inside a week share injuries, weather and referee crews, so resampling individual bets understates uncertaintygate G5results.json ↗

Residual persistenceCorrelation of consecutive-season mean spread errorPersistent error is a bias to fix, not skill to selldiagnosticcorrelations.json ↗
Prospective replicationFreeze the rule, then grade games played after the freezeThe only test that removes researcher choicegate G8 (not yet possible)nfl.com
Luck cannot be removed. It can only be bounded, and the bounds can only fail a strategy or leave it unproven. Nothing in this repository certifies skill; the strongest honest statement is that every registered rule is statistically indistinguishable from chance after costs, and three are worse than chance.
