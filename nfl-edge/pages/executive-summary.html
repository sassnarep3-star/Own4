The three decisions this evidence supports

  1. Do not publish picks or prices. Our own lines are a model output; the market they would be compared with does not exist in this repository, and our model is worse than the closing line on accuracy anyway.
  2. Keep the leaderboard as a rejection machine. Its job is to stop a lucky backtest from becoming a paid strategy. Today it rejected everything, which is the correct output for public-data rules against efficient prices.
  3. Buy data or change the question. The two blocked requirements (props and starter/backup effects) are blocked by licensing, not by method. Either licence the data or redefine the product as a decision-support and data-coverage tool.
