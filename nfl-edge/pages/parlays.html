Strategies / Parlays

Parlays

Cross-game two-leg parlays built from the strongest single-bet legs each week. Same-game combinations are excluded on purpose: their legs are not independent, and this repository cannot price them without archived same-game prices.

Pricing rule

Two legs at −110 have a true payout of (1.9091² − 1) = 2.6458 units profit per unit staked. A push leg voids that leg rather than the ticket. No same-game correlation adjustment is applied because cross-game legs are independent by construction; where legs are correlated, the measured phi values are on the correlation page.

Why parlays lose more than singles. Each leg carries the same negative expectation, and multiplying them compounds it: −3.1% per leg becomes −15.7% per two-leg ticket. A parlay is not a way to amplify an edge that is not there.

n = 155 is far below the 300-bet floor, so even a positive result here could not have been certified. The gate table says so explicitly rather than leaving the reader to notice.
