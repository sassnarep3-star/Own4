How to read phi

phi is the correlation between two yes/no outcomes. Zero means the legs are independent, so multiplying their probabilities gives the right joint price. Positive means they co-move and multiplying understates the true joint price; negative means multiplying overstates it. Both values above are within 0.011 of zero, so for these two team-level pairs, multiplication is a defensible approximation on this sample.

A value near +0.40 means the model is consistently wrong in the same direction about a third of the time — that is a bias to fix, not an edge to sell. Genuine skill would show up as profits persisting, which the leaderboard does not find.
