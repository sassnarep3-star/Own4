Q1Does any strategy we run actually beat the closing line?ANSWEREDMeasured walk-forward on 2,383 holdout games, 2017-2025, frozen config. S1 spread: 1,540 bets, ROI -3.12%, bootstrap 95% CI [-7.84%, +1.54%], placebo p=0.41. S2 total: 1,187 bets, ROI -5.32%. S3 moneyline: 2,060 bets, ROI -8.45%. S4 2-leg cross-game parlay: 155 bets, ROI -15.74%.Whether a different, still-unwritten rule would beat it.No. Every registered strategy is negative after the vig, and none is statistically distinguishable from break-even except in the wrong direction (S3's upper bound is -1.70%).Nothing. This closes D4 in the negative.results.json ↗
Q2Do we have a market line to compare our own lines to, for 2026?ANSWEREDNo official NFL source publishes betting lines or prices - that is league-wide, not a gap in this collection. The only prices in this repository are a third-party nflverse closing-line archive inside the peer nfl-quant workspace, and it holds no 2026 prices. This session did not verify the NFL's commercial data-distribution arrangements from an official league source, so none is claimed here; the Sportradar/Genius Sports note that appears in this repository concerns the NBA, not the NFL.Whether a licensed price feed will ever be available to this project.No 2026 market price exists in scope. Every 2026 market and divergence cell is therefore blank.Blocks D3 and all 2026 profitability questions.nfl.com
Q3Is our spread model more accurate than the market?ANSWEREDHoldout mean absolute error, spread: ours 10.502 points, closing line 9.871 points, naive zero-margin 11.231 points. Total: ours 10.830, closing line 10.470, naive league-average 11.042.Whether a richer model (injuries, rest, weather) closes the 0.63-point gap.No. We beat the naive baseline but lose to the closing line on both markets. That is the normal, expected result and it is the honest one.Gate G4 fails for every strategy.results.json ↗
Q4Is the model's stated uncertainty honest?ANSWEREDNominal 90% interval coverage is 89.34% empirically (target 0.85-0.95, so this passes) but the mean residual is +0.651 points, i.e. the model systematically under-predicts the home team's margin (|bias| < 0.5 was the pre-registered bar).Whether the bias is a home-field constant error or a ratings error.Partly. The interval width is right; the centre is off by 0.65 points toward the away team. Gate G3 fails on the bias test only.A single-parameter fix (raise HFA by ~0.65) could be tested, but it must be a new pre-registered version, not a post-hoc tweak.results.json ↗
Q5Can luck be removed from the leaderboard?ANSWEREDLuck cannot be removed; it can only be bounded. We use four bounds: week-clustered bootstrap CIs, a matched-random placebo permutation test, a season-stability requirement, and a residual-persistence check.Residual persistence is +0.40 across consecutive seasons, which is weak evidence that the model's spread error partly repeats - i.e. a persistent bias, not a persistent edge.No strategy can be certified skill-based. The most defensible statement is: at efficient closing lines, a small public-data model is not distinguishable from chance after costs.Sets expectation for D2 and D4.correlations.json ↗
Q6What exactly are we allowed to call an official NFL source?ANSWEREDnfl.com pages (standings, schedules by week, injuries, team stats, player stats, game pages) and operations.nfl.com. Team websites are club-operated, not league-official. Betting lines, projected lines and SGP prices have no official league source.Whether the league will publish any machine-readable official API to the public. Its current stats pages are HTML-only.Only league-operated pages count. Everything else is labelled third-party or left blank.Constrains every other answer on this site.nfl.com
Q7How big a sample is enough before a leaderboard result means anything?ANSWEREDThe pre-registered floor is 300 graded bets (gate G1) plus a lower bootstrap bound above zero (G5) plus a placebo p below 0.05 (G6).Nothing fundamental; the exact thresholds are research design choices, not official values, and are labelled as such.300 bets is the floor, not a target. At -110 pricing you need about 52.4% winners to break even, and detecting a 2-point edge needs thousands of graded bets.Explains why 155 parlay bets cannot be judged at all.gates.json ↗
Q8Does the 2026 season give us enough games to originate lines responsibly?ANSWEREDEvery club has played exactly 9 games through Week 9 (verified: 136 games, 32 clubs, aggregate W-L-T and PF-PA match the official standings with 0 mismatches).Whether 9 games of same-season data is better than a shrunk historical prior.Nine games is thin. Our model therefore shrinks every rating toward the league mean with a 4-game prior and carries 35% of last season's rating into the early weeks.Justifies the shrinkage design; does not justify confidence.nfl.com
