Originated lines

Our own spread, total and fair moneyline for every Week 10 game, built only from the official team statistics on this site. These are model outputs, not prices, not predictions and not recommendations.

The model, in full

  • Home points = (home offence points/game + away defence points/game) / 2 + home-field / 2
  • Away points = (away offence points/game + home defence points/game) / 2 − home-field / 2
  • Spread = −(home points − away points); total = home + away
  • Moneyline = the fair (no-vig) price implied by a normal model of the margin with σ = 14.343 points, the holdout residual standard deviation
  • Home-field = 1.5 points, a stated assumption (flag F-04), not an official value

That is the entire model. It has three parameters and no proprietary inputs, which is exactly why it loses to the closing line by 0.63 points.
