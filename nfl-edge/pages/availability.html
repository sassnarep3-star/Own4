Availability / Injuries & backups

Injuries and backup depth

What the league actually discloses about availability, and an explicit statement of the part it does not disclose: who is a starter and who is a backup.

The requirement that cannot be met from official sources. You asked to track second-, third- and fourth-string players replacing starters. The NFL does not publish a depth chart on nfl.com — the team depth-chart URL returns a 404 — and it does not publish snap counts. The injury report gives practice and game status only. So this page reports who is unavailable and at which position, and never claims a player is a backup. Guessing would be the one thing that makes the rest of this site worthless.

What it would take to answer the backup question

RequirementStatusNoteValue in repoSource
Starter/backup designationBLOCKEDNot published by the league; the depth-chart URL 404sblanknfl.com
Snap counts / participationBLOCKEDLicensed product; not on nfl.comblanknfl.com
Who is unavailable, and whereAVAILABLEOfficial injury report, captured above275 playersnfl.com
Coaching-staff depth commentsAVAILABLE (unstructured)nfl.com news items do say things like 'expected to start again'; they are prose, not a dataset, and would need manual reading per gameprosenfl.com
