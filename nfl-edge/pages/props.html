Strategies / Player props

Player props

This page is deliberately empty of results. Player-prop strategies cannot be graded with the sources this project is allowed to use, and inventing a proxy would produce numbers that look like evidence and are not.

Blocked, with the reason named. Grading a prop requires two things: official player-game outcomes, and the price the market actually offered. nfl.com publishes season-to-date player leaderboards only (no per-game logs in machine-readable form), and no prop prices exist anywhere in this repository. Without both, a "prop strategy" is a guess about a guess.

What would make this page real

  1. An authorised per-game player-stat feed (the league publishes aggregates; per-game data is licensed).
  2. An archived prop price with a timestamp, so the price being tested is the price that existed.
  3. A pre-registered gate version that adds a dependence correction for same-game combos.

A peer workspace in this repository reported a QB-over + WR1-over joint lift of 1.45x. It was measured against a rolling-mean proxy line, not a sportsbook, and that workspace's own flags record the gate as failed. It is not imported here (see Q10).
