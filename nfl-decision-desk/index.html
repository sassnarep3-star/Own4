What this site decided, in one paragraph

The 2026 corpus is the frozen third-party archive: 136 settled games (weeks 1–9 complete, week 10 absent) plus 136 scheduled. That is the brief's "136-game log". This session then read the official week-10 page at 2026-11-15T23:24:27Z and found 9 finals, eight of which postdate the archive. Adding those nine results makes all 32 clubs match the official standings cell for cell, with zero unexplained deltas — that is the strongest verification this repo can produce without 136 individual game-page reads.

The honest result. Walk-forward, the moneyline model scores a Brier of 0.2525 against a base-rate benchmark of 0.2496 and a vig-free market benchmark of 0.2147. The paired week-cluster interval on (market − model) is [-0.07532, -0.00660], entirely below zero: the market is better. No amount of stake sizing fixes that, which is why signal and staking are ranked separately and why 0 of 1,000 reward units have been awarded.

1. Two 2026 games put the "home" team on another continent, and the model still gives it home field. SF@LA was played at the Melbourne Cricket Ground and NE@DET at FC Bayern Munich Stadium, per the official game centres. A constant home-field term is unsupported for both. Published as defect FX-01 rather than silently patched.2 gamespublished defectnfl.com game centre OFFICIAL NFL
2. The brief's injury-report counts do not reproduce, and that is the point of timestamps. At 2026-11-15T23:24:27Z the official page carried 347 rows across all 28 Week-10 clubs, of which 171 (49.3%) carry a game designation — not "6 of 254". The three Lions players flagged as blank now read Out. Both facts are only visible because the capture carries a clock.171/347 designatedresolvednfl.com injuries OFFICIAL NFL
