Walk-forward discipline

For week w the model is fitted only on weeks < w. Weeks 1–2 are not scored because there is no honest history before them; they are skipped and counted, never imputed. Shrinkage is chosen on an inner walk-forward inside the training slice, so the hyper-parameter never sees the games it is scored on. The logistic link scale is fitted the same way.

Fitting an ablation on that would measure noise and call it a personnel effect.
