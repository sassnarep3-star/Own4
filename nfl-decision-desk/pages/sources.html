What was NOT read, and is therefore not claimed

The 136 official game pages, the 136 Game Book PDFs, club depth-chart pages, inactive lists, snap counts, and the NFL rulebook section on the declared onside kick were all not read this session. No value on this site depends on them. Where one of those would be needed, the row is blank and the feed is named.

Environment note

Direct outbound HTTP from this sandbox is restricted to a small allowlist (github.com, codeload, api.github.com, npm, PyPI). Official nfl.com pages were reached through the session's page-reading tool, and each one listed above was actually returned and read. raw.githubusercontent.com was not reachable, so no archive was re-downloaded; the vendored copies already in the repository were used and their structure was verified.
