Prices, own lines, and the residual

Origination in the order the brief requires — side, then total, then moneyline — with the fair line, the market line and the difference kept as three separate columns, and the residual against the market published for every game.

DEC-1, decided before any backtest was published. The NFL publishes no betting prices, so no price on this site is official. Closing prices for 2011–2025 exist in a third-party archive; opening prices exist only for 2021. Consequently: (1) 2026 origination rows carry a fair line and a blank market column with a reason; (2) backtests are split into PRICE-VERIFIED (2021) and CLOSE-ONLY (2011–2025) strata; (3) 2026 is excluded from any price backtest because its opening prices do not exist.
