Computed only from traceable numbers, with n published on every value and a named blocker on every blank. An automatic robustness guard has been added, because one of these coefficients turned out to be an artefact.

The guard that earned its place. CR-04 was computed as r = −0.426 between rest differential and home margin — a plausible "tired teams lose" story. The guard detected that 74 of 120 games share a single rest value and that 14 games in two tiny tail cells carry the whole coefficient. It is published as NOT ROBUST, with the group means beside it, instead of as a finding.

Warnings that apply to the whole table
