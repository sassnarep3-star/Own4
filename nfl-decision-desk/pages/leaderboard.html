Controls ran on this build, and they matter

A coin flip, an always-home rule and a climatology base rate are ranked in the same table as the model. The model does not beat them by anything the interval can distinguish — and the market benchmark beats all of them. That comparison is the point.

Rewards

reward units are escrowed until a strategy clears G1-G4 AND holds LIVE status. 0 of 1000 awarded. 0 of 1000 units awarded. A strategy qualifies only by clearing all four statistical gates and holding a live status, and nothing clears the coverage floor at this sample.
