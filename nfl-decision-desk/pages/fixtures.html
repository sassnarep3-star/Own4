FX-01 — PUBLISHED DEFECT
two 2026 regular-season games are officially international: SF at LA (Melbourne Cricket Ground) and NE at DET (FC Bayern Munich Stadium). In both, the club listed as HOME is not playing at home.
the walk-forward model adds a constant home-field term to EVERY game. For these games that term is unsupported. The defect is published rather than silently corrected, because correcting it changes published numbers and must be a pre-registered change, not an edit.
