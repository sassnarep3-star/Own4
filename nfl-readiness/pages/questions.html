Questions, information and answers

A decision cannot be made on information that does not exist, so each question is split into what the evidence establishes, what is missing, and the specific artefact that would resolve it. 20 questions. 20 answers are blank, because none is established by the evidence in hand.

The blank answers are the deliverable. Writing a plausible answer here would be the most damaging thing this page could do: it would enter the model as a fact and never be challenged again. A blank with a named discovery task is worth more than a confident guess.
