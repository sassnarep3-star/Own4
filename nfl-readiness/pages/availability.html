The join: does a blank game status mean available?

This is the calibration the brief keeps asking for, and it can be measured on the two official sources already in hand — no prices and no model needed.

Not in the report at all70nfl.com inactives
Ambiguous surname match (excluded)0nfl.com inactives
What this establishes and what it does not. Among players who appear in both sources, all 68 carry an explicit Out, Doubtful or Questionable — no named inactive carries a blank status. That is the first real evidence that the game-status column is a trustworthy filter. But 70 of 138 named inactives never appear in the weekly report at all, because reserve-list and practice-squad players are not on it. So a blank status still cannot be read as “available”. It stays unknown until an active list exists.
