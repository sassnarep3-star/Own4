Methodology & verification protocol

1. Data provenance

  • Only official league sources: nfl.com/standings, nfl.com/scores, nfl.com/injuries (+ game pages). Retrieval date 2026-11-12. Every table row in this site carries one.
  • Transcription is verbatim; derived columns are computed (engine/analyze.py) and labeled as derived; literature constants are labeled UNVERIFIED (F-01/F-02).
  • Consistency gate before publication: W=L, PF=PA, NET=PF−PA, PCT recomputed — all must hold or the build fails.

2. Originated-lines model (v1)

  • Team strength: season PF/PA per game → expected score for a matchup = mean(own OFF/g, opponent DEF/g).
  • Fair margin = home exp − away exp; fair total = Σ; P(home) = Normal(margin, σ=13.45, UNVERIFIED); ML from P(home); no explicit HFA term (embedded); neutral site handled implicitly (W10-02).
  • Pythagorean share uses exponent 2.37 (UNVERIFIED). Luck Δ = actual − Pythagorean wins.
  • Pre-registered 2026-11-12 before Week 10 concludes; parameters committed in git before any result is known to the model.

3. Correlation & multiple-testing discipline

  • Pearson r with two-tailed Student-t p (df=30); Benjamini–Hochberg FDR across all reported tests (reported together, q=0.05).
  • Dependence for parlay/prop pricing is measured, never assumed; independence rejected at |r|>0.10 or p<0.05 (G9).
  • Prop-pair correlations are intentionally BLANK — no official game-log feed; inventing them would violate the no-hallucination rule.

4. Luck removal & validation gates

  • Walk-forward chronology only; holdout locked; minimum n=300 graded games per strategy/market; BH-FDR; permutation test on headline metric (z≥+2.0 required); CLV vs closing line when market data is licensed; calibration ECE ≤ 0.10; beat pre-registered baselines (home-always, favorite-always, naive flat).
  • Leaderboard: gates G1–G10 mandatory; rewards virtual until Q-05/06/07/11 close; honest zeros published (current status).

5. Verification boundaries

  • This build verifies: transcription consistency, internal recomputation, link existence, per-row source presence. It does NOT verify: external truth beyond the cited pages, market prices, injury replacement mapping (impossible without depth charts), or predictive power (needs SUG-05).
  • Sources are dynamic; standings/reports change. Re-fetch before any operational use; the commit history is the audit trail.
