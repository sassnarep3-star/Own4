Governance, gates and pre-registration

This is the part of the project that does not need any missing data. Gates, prohibitions and publication rules are written before results exist, so the record shows the rules were fixed in advance and not adjusted afterwards.

Rules that bind every strategy

ruleas frozen
leaderboard_rule
  • signal_vs_staking — signal metrics rank a strategy; staking metrics are displayed beside them and cannot promote it; ROI never appears as a ranking key
  • ranked_on — benchmark-relative proper score (Brier skill) with a game-clustered lower bound and a multiplicity adjustment across trials
  • forbidden —
    • season-long ROI as a ranking
    • win rate as a ranking
    • any backtest figure on a leaderboard page
    • rewards paid on assumed prices
  • reward_rule — 0 of 1,000 reward units are available until a strategy clears qualification AND verified prices exist for its graded bets
divisions
    • name — singles
    • eligibility — one market per game, forecast recorded pre-kickoff, graded on official result
    • name — game parlay
    • eligibility — blocked: joint probability not validated (see joint_evidence_gate)
    • name — same-game parlay
    • eligibility — blocked: joint probability not validated, legs share game-level dependence
    • name — prop combo
    • eligibility — blocked: no official prop prices, no operator void/push rules in the repo
joint_evidence_gate
  • rule — multiplying marginal probabilities is invalid under dependence; a combo may be ranked only when BOTH a directly validated joint probability and the operator's void/push rules are stored for its leg set
  • current_state — 0 of 4 combo divisions eligible; 0 joint estimates with n exist in the repo; 0 operator rule sets stored
control_baselines
  • random_picks — computable today: a uniform pick over the two sides of each market, graded on official results
  • favorites_only — not computable today: 'favorite' is defined by a price, and no official price source exists
  • market_consensus — not computable today: one third-party archive is a capture, not a consensus
  • rule — every leaderboard run must show the random baseline beside it, and must state which baselines are unavailable and why
price_data_policy
  • id — DEC-P1
  • status — OPEN - owner decision required
  • decision_needed — what counts as a market price for the purpose of a residual, a CLV measure and a reward
  • options —
    • SUG-01 A
    • SUG-01 B
    • SUG-01 C
  • interim_rule — no backtest that quotes a market number may be published on this namespace's pages until the decision is recorded; a model-only residual (fair line vs official final margin) is allowed and is labelled as not a price residual
depth_rule
  • rule — depth rank is never inferred from jersey number, seniority, salary, or an image read by eye; a rank may only be stored when a source publishes it as a dated, structured value
  • slot_rule — a depth slot is a set of players, not a starter; multiple players sharing a rank, sharing one slot, or leaving a slot empty are all published as such
  • open_source_question — Q-05 (no authorized structured rank source exists today)
  • note_on_this_session — no player in this namespace is described as a starter, a backup, or a rank - the capture publishes designations only, because that is all the official report publishes
correlation_policy
  • inputs — officially sourced numbers only (scores, standings, report counts); third-party captures are excluded from every r published here
  • reporting — n, r, and the derivation script path on the same row; an r without n is not published
  • lookahead — a correlation between two variables measured from the same single week is labelled 'descriptive, n=1 week' and may not be used as a model input
splits
  • ideal — train / validation / final-holdout seasons
  • available — one season of official results (2026) + 2025 standings as a pre-season prior
  • rule — until a second full season is stored, every published comparison is chronological-by-week within one season, and no 'holdout season' claim may be made
publication_contract
  • every_table_row —
    • value
    • source_url
    • read_utc or read_window
    • verification_state in {verified-official, derived-here, peer-cited, blank-flagged}
  • blank_rule — a cell with no official value is printed blank with a flag id; it is never filled by inference, never filled by a third-party value labelled as official, and never filled by a prior week's number
  • model_output_row —
    • p (probability)
    • the input vector that produced it
    • model_version
    • as_of
    • recorded_before_kickoff (bool)
  • market_columns — fair line / market line / difference are three separate columns; the market and difference columns stay null while DEC-P1 is open

Demotion gate (live &rarr; paper)

  1. any LIVE strategy returns to PAPER automatically on any of: (1) the 20-game rolling Brier-skill lower bound falls at or below 0; (2) a data-integrity failure in its inputs for 2 consecutive weeks; (3) its pre-registered refute condition fires; (4) its source page changes schema and the parser is not re-verified within 7 days
  2. the strategy keeps its history, loses its board rank, and its reward accrual stops at the moment of demotion, not at the moment of review
0 strategies are LIVE, so the gate has nothing to demote; it is published so that the first promotion cannot be its own exception

a rule's registration is the git commit that freezes it; the hash is published with the rule, and no figure computed after the hash may change the rule The hash itself is recorded in data/prereg.json and re-checked against git history by engine/preregister.py --check, which fails the build when a frozen rule has been edited after its registration commit.
