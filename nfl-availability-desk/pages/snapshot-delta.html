What a delta changes in practice

  • The 11 changed designations sit in 2 clubs (LAC 10, IND 1) — the two Monday-night fixtures. A Sunday capture of a Monday game is not late: it is a different fact.
  • Designation coverage in this capture: 171/347 (49.3%). Coverage is a property of the day, so it is stored with the read window, never as a table-wide statistic.
  • 0 rows were added or removed between the two renders that tie, so the difference between those two is a clock difference, not a coverage failure — while the 21-row and 71-row and 240-row gaps against the older captures are coverage failures. That is the difference between a good capture and a complete one.
