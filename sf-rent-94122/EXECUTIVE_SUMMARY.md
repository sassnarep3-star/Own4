## 2. Six Critical Legal Rules for This 1940 Building in ZIP 94122

1. **Binding Local Cap (`1.6%` through Feb. 28, 2027):** Under [SF Rent Board Form 571](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/571_Allowable_Annual_Increases_26-27_1.pdf) and [SF Admin. Code § 37.3(a)(1)](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco), the annual allowable increase for effective dates **March 1, 2026 – February 28, 2027** is **1.6%** of base rent only, with **zero upward rounding**. Statewide AB 1482 (`8.8%` in SF) does **not** apply ([SF AB 1482 Guide](https://www.sf.gov/reports--california-tenant-protection-act-2019-ab-1482)).
2. **Mandatory Housing Inventory License (`portal.sfrb.org`):** Under [SF Admin. Code § 37.15 / Ch. 37A](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory), no annual or banked rent increase may take effect unless the owner has filed the annual Housing Inventory and holds an active **Rent Increase License** on the effective date.
3. **Unit 1 Occupant Transition Trap:** Because original occupant `Tenant A1` continues to live in Unit 1, neither Costa-Hawkins ([Cal. Civ. Code § 1954.53(d)(2)–(3)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1954.53.)) nor [SF Rent Board Rules § 6.14](https://www.sf.gov/information--rent-increases-under-section-614-and-costa-hawkins) allows a market reset, and [Rules § 6.13](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco) prohibits charging extra rent for adding `Tenant A4`. Furthermore, if the April 1, 2026 agreement is a 12-month fixed-term lease, [Cal. Civ. Code § 827(a)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=827.) bars any unilateral increase prior to **April 1, 2027** (when the not-yet-published 2027–2028 SF rate will govern).
4. **Unit 2 Ledger & Banking Rules (`Topic 503`):** Because the brief supplies only the June 1, 2019 lease-start rent (`$3,150/mo`) and not interim 2019–2026 history, Unit 2's current lawful base rent and collectible banked balance are left strictly **blank** until ledger reconciliation. Under [SF Rent Board Topic 503](https://www.sf.gov/information--banked-rent-increases), banking requires ≥24 full months without an increase and simple non-compounded addition; any combined increase `>10%` requires **90 days personal / 95 days CA mailed notice** ([Civ. Code § 827(b)(3)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=827.); [CCP § 1013](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=1013.&lawCode=CCP)), and mailing a 95-day notice after **November 25, 2026** pushes the effective date past February 28, 2027 into the unpublished 2027–2028 rate period ([SF Multiple-Year Guide](https://www.sf.gov/information--annual-rent-increase-multiple-years)).
5. **Corporate vs. Individual Owners:** Corporate REITs ([SEC Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1687229/000168722926000016/invh-20251231.htm); disqualified from AB 1482 single-family exemptions under [Civ. Code § 37.10C](https://www.sf.gov/information--algorithmic-devices-set-rent-are-prohibited-san-francisco), `$1,000`/unit/month penalty; tenant-org standing effective Oct. 6, 2025 per [Ord. 169-25](https://www.sf.gov/news-update-to-algorithmic-devices-law)). Representative behavioral rates are left blank/flagged.
