Executive Summary & Compliance Action Plan

Concise synthesis of governing caps, occupancy-turnover locks, banking cliffs, fair-housing rules, and pre-service verification steps.

1. Property Context & Privacy Architecture

This study evaluates lawful and standard rental increases as of November 6, 2026 for two tenancies in an owner-reported 1940 residential building in San Francisco's Outer Sunset (ZIP 94122) analyzed under the San Francisco Rent Ordinance (SF Admin. Code Ch. 37 ↗; parcel APN, total unit count, and exemption status are unverified from Assessor/PIM records and flagged on Flagged for Review). All personal tenant details and initials supplied in the brief are strictly excluded from sf-rent-94122/ (HTML, JSON, Markdown, and verifier source code) and replaced with neutral placeholders (Tenant A1–A4 in Unit 1; Tenant B1–B2 in Unit 2), while keeping all dates and dollar amounts exact.

2. Headline Findings for Unit 1 & Unit 2

  • Governing Annual Cap (March 1, 2026 – February 28, 2027): For covered SF Rent Ordinance units, exactly 1.6% of base monthly rent (SF Rent Board Announcement ↗). Rounding up to the nearest dollar is unlawful. California's statewide AB 1482 rate (8.8% in SF) does not apply where the stricter local SF Rent Ordinance controls (Civ. Code § 1947.12(d)(7) ↗).
  • Mandatory Licensing Pre-Condition: No annual or banked increase on a covered unit is enforceable unless the owner has reported the unit to the SF Rent Board Housing Inventory and holds an active Rent Increase License on the effective date (SF Admin. Code § 37.15 / Ch. 37A ↗).
  • Unit 1 ($3,995.00/mo · Feb. 1, 2025 initial lease; Apr. 1, 2026 continuation with Tenant A1 & Tenant A4): Because original occupant Tenant A1 still resides in Unit 1, vacancy decontrol to market rent is barred (Civ. Code § 1954.53(d)(2)–(3) ↗; Rules § 6.14 ↗), and charging extra rent for replacement co-occupant Tenant A4 is barred (Rules § 6.13 ↗). If the April 1, 2026 instrument is a 12-month fixed-term lease through March 31, 2027, unilateral increases are barred until April 1, 2027 (Civ. Code § 827(a) ↗), when the 2027–2028 SF rate will govern. Only if Unit 1 is currently month-to-month at $3,995.00 can a 1.6% (+$63.92/mo → $4,058.92/mo) increase take effect on or before February 28, 2027.
  • Unit 2 ($3,150.00/mo reported at June 1, 2019 lease start · Tenant B1 & Tenant B2): Because interim 2019–2026 rent history was not supplied, Unit 2's current lawful base rent and any collectible banked balance are left strictly blank/unverified until ledger reconciliation under Topic 503 ↗ (which requires ≥24 full months without an increase and simple non-compounded addition). Conditional on $3,150.00 still being the current base rent, a single-year 1.6% (+$50.40/mo → $3,200.40/mo) adjustment on 30-day personal / 35-day CA mailed notice preserves any valid prior banked years and avoids >10% 90/95-day notice complications (note: mailing a 95-day notice after Nov. 25, 2026 pushes effective dates past Feb. 28, 2027 into the unpublished 2027–28 rate period).
  • Raising One Unit vs. Both Units & Counsel Review Boundary: Apply a neutral per-unit eligibility policy—adjusting each unit at its first lawful month-to-month contract/anniversary eligibility date after independent compliance checks (42 U.S.C. § 3604 ↗; Gov. Code § 12955 ↗; SF § 37.10B ↗). No public checklist guarantees avoiding complaints or passing legal review without case-specific attorney inspection of physical leases and ledgers.
