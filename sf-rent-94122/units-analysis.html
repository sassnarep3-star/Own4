Unit 2 Banking Options & The Nov. 25, 2026 / March 1, 2027 Rate-Year Cliff

Under SF Rent Board Banking Rules ↗, skipped annual increases bank only after 24 full months without an increase and are summed additively without compounding. Crucially, if a landlord imposes a banked increase on a new anniversary date after fewer than 12 months have elapsed since the most recent anniversary, the unbanked year is forfeited.
