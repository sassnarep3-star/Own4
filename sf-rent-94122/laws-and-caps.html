Multi-Jurisdictional Legal Matrix (Local SF · California · Federal)

For a 1940 multi-unit residential building in San Francisco ZIP 94122, local, state, and federal statutes stack together: the strictest tenant-protective cap governs price increases (SF Rent Ordinance 1.6% ↗), California state law governs written notice delivery and occupancy-turnover limits, and all three jurisdictions enforce strict anti-retaliation and fair-housing mandates.
