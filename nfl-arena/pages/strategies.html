Removing luck from the equation

  • Skill = performance that variance alone cannot explain, at the strategy's sample size, after correcting for the number of strategies tried.
  • Every ranked list carries: n, ROI, win rate, break-even rate, Wilson 95% interval, required-n for 80% power, raw p, BH-adjusted p, max drawdown.
  • A strategy whose Wilson interval contains break-even is reported as 'not distinguishable from luck' regardless of point estimate.
  • ROI-only leaderboards are prohibited.

Formulas used by the pipeline:

  • Wilson 95% interval: center = (p + z^2/2n)/(1+z^2/n), half = z*sqrt(p(1-p)/n + z^2/4n^2)/(1+z^2/n), z=1.96
  • Required n for 80% power at observed edge: n ≈ (1.96+0.84)^2 * p(1-p) / (p - p_break_even)^2 (normal approximation)
  • Multiple testing: Benjamini-Hochberg at q=0.05 across the whole registered family

Computed columns for every registered strategy are published on the Leaderboard page and in nfl-arena/data/leaderboard_metrics.json.

Selectively skill-based, by construction

  • Singles carry the evidence: one grade per bet, CLV measurable, luck deflated by Wilson + BH-FDR.
  • Cross-game parlays are allowed only from legs that individually pass the gates; cross-game independence is defensible (see Correlation), same-game independence is not.
  • Same-game / player-prop combos are held behind the FAM-3 gate until player data exists — a deliberately selective exclusion, not an oversight.
  • Survivor rule: a strategy that loses its edge against closing lines is rejected even if its ROI vs opening lines is positive — the skill definition is anchored to the efficient number.
