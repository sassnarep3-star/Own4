Parlay pricing guidance

  • Cross-game legs: independence is a fair approximation (verified by construction; no shared game state).
  • Same-game side+total legs: measured dependence ≈ 0 (CR-07/CR-08) — independent pricing is defensible for these pairs only.
  • Same-game team totals: slight negative dependence (CR-10) — price jointly, do not multiply naive probabilities.
  • Any leg involving a player prop: CANNOT be priced yet (CR-P1..P3 blank). Player-prop combos stay behind the FAM-3 gate.
