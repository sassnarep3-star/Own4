Competition rules

Entry: Any session or user may register a strategy by opening a registered entry: id, family, signal definition, stake rule, gate set version. Entries are pre-registered before grading.

Scoring: Primary: BH-FDR-adjusted significance at the sample floor. Secondary (published, never hidden): ROI, Wilson interval, CLV, max drawdown, seasons stable.

Reward rule: 1,000 points to the single strategy that passes ALL gates at the sample floor in a season; 0 otherwise. No partial credit, no discretionary bonuses. (non-monetary: Non-monetary. A cash prize on an unvalidated model turns research into promotion.)

Tiers: Qualified: All gates pass -> eligible for the reward decision · Bronze (no reward): 5-9 gates pass -> listed, zero points, no claim of edge · Rejected: <5 gates pass -> listed as rejected, zero points

Anti-gaming:

  • Strategy ids are immutable after first graded bet; changes create a new id.
  • Multiple-testing is handled by BH-FDR across ALL registered strategies of the season, not per author.
  • Any strategy whose edge disappears against closing lines is rejected regardless of ROI vs opening lines.
  • Leaderboard publishes the luck columns (Wilson, required-n, BH) next to ROI so variance cannot masquerade as skill.

0 of 15 registered strategies (nfl-lines walk-forward set) are reward-eligible; all BH-adjusted p-values exceed 0.05; confirmation windows below the 300-bet floor. The competition is, by design, currently a rejection machine.
