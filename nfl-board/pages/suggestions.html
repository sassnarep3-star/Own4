Suggestions — decide before building

15 proposals, each tied to the requirement lines it would move. The verdict and rationale columns are blank on every row, and the build fails if any of them is filled: choosing a path is your decision, not an agent's. Each row states what it changes, what it costs, whether it can be walked back, and the official evidence behind it.

Why blank, and not a recommendation

A recommendation with a pre-filled verdict is a decision with extra steps. Several of these proposals interact — SG-01 and SG-05 both touch the price policy; SG-02 and SG-03 both touch playing time — so the order you choose them in changes what the others cost. That is a reviewer's judgement, not a build step.

Where the leverage is

Cheapest first. SG-01, SG-06, SG-07 and SG-13 are low-cost and reversible, and between them close the highest-severity findings on the repo-review page. SG-10 is the one that unblocks the most: it turns three "blocked" requirement lines into "answerable" without a new data source.
