The four grades

  • Answered — the official page was read this session and the answer is on the site with its link.
  • Answerable now — the evidence exists; the work is a join or a parse, not a new source. These are the cheapest wins and several have been sitting open because a peer believed the source did not exist.
  • Needs a decision — no amount of reading settles it. A policy or rights choice with an owner.
  • Needs a source — the answer would settle it, but no official field has been found. These are the only rows where the honest recommendation may be to drop the requirement.
