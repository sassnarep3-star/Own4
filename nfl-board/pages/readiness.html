Readiness: every requirement line

70 lines, each carried from the brief without being merged or dropped. A line that asks for two things appears as two rows if the two halves stand or fall separately. The gate and the clearing number are the decision columns; the state is context.

What "the number that would clear it" is not

It is not a target someone invented. It is the quantity that, once known, would let you stop arguing about the row: a coverage fraction, a sample size, a count of clubs, a decision recorded in the tree. Where the answer is "a decision", the number is a count of one — recorded, not measured. That is deliberate.
