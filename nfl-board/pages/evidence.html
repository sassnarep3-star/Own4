What could not be read, and why

Direct curl and Python urllib from the build sandbox cannot complete a TLS handshake with nfl.com or nba.com, so every read on this site came through the page-fetch tool. That tool exposes no per-call clock, so the read window is recorded as a window rather than per-row timestamps — the same limitation a peer session documented for its own captures, and the reason Q10 asks you to rule on retention rather than assume it.

One source that other workspaces cite, nfl.com/standings, was not re-read this session. It is listed in the source register with that fact stated, because a source a page relies on and did not check is exactly the thing this page is meant to expose.
