The NFL decision board

Every instruction in the brief is a row. Each row carries three things a reviewer can act on: whether an artefact exists, which gate stops it, and the number that would clear it. Nothing on this site is a promise about a betting edge. The honest headline is that the results machinery is sound, the availability data is better than the site admits, and no price can be official because the league publishes none.

The one finding that matters most. Two peer datasets state that the "who actually started" side of the availability graph cannot be sourced, and leave it blank. That is no longer true. This session read the official Week 10 inactives article (12 games, both clubs each) and the official Game Book link published on every game centre. Both are dated, both are official, and one Game Book is already parsed elsewhere in this repository with zero join collisions against the injury report. Three requirement lines move from blocked to answerable the moment that is acted on — see SG-10.

The gate that blocks the most lines

Of the 70 requirement lines, 17 are stopped by G-NONE — nothing blocks it. That is the highest-leverage thing to fix: it is not a data collection problem and not a modelling problem, it is a decision.

What G-NONE means, and what would clear it

No official price source exists, because the NFL publishes no lines. Five requirement lines ask for a residual, a vig decomposition, a closing line value or a leaderboard stage that escrows one. None of them can be built from an official number, because there is no official number to build from. What would clear the group is a written policy that says which sources are permitted, on what usage basis, and which of the resulting numbers may be republished. A provisional policy already exists in the tree and is flagged for your review; until you accept or reverse it, every one of these lines stays blocked on a decision rather than on data.

Blocked lines: RQ-18, RQ-20, RQ-27, RQ-28, RQ-35, RQ-39, RQ-42, RQ-58

Why this board exists

The repository holds dozens of NFL research workspaces built by earlier sessions. Several publish overlapping requirement, question and suggestion registers, and several state a blocker that has since stopped being true. This board does one job: it puts the requirement, the blocker and the clearing number on one line so a decision can be taken without reading every namespace. It links to the analysis namespaces instead of copying their tables — the fragmentation itself is a question for you (Q18, SG-11).

How to read a row

State is the least useful column. Gate tells you what kind of obstacle it is — a missing official source, a rights decision, a sample that is too small, or nothing at all. The number that would clear it is the only column that tells you when to come back.

Not started is 5 lines: RQ-11 (Do not model depth from jersey order; model it from playing time), RQ-54 (Use snap counts as the starter proxy when the depth chart is unavailable), RQ-56 (Stadium roof/surface and NWS hourly wind/precipitation overlay), RQ-64 (Archive weekly injury reports for past seasons), RQ-66 (2026 kickoff and declared onside-kick regime priors filter). None of them is blocked by research; they are blocked by nobody having started.
