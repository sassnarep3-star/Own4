## Validation standard

Model lines and derived correlations are **not official NBA facts**; each needs an input record, timestamp, formula, uncertainty and predeclared holdout. Promoting a singles, parlay or prop-combo strategy requires authorized point-in-time inputs, operator quotes/settlement, joint-probability treatment of dependence, multiple-testing control and prospective replication. Luck cannot be removed from outcomes; repeatability and proper uncertainty limits reduce selection bias. Blank official market values are **unverified**, not zero. This audit neither computes a new correlation nor awards leaderboard points.
