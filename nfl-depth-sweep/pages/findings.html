Findings

Every number below is computed at build time from data/route_l.json and data/route_c.json; the page generator fails if the data and the prose disagree.

1 · The league route is closed (answers G-02's first half)

All 32 league per-team depth paths (nfl.com/teams/<slug>/depth-chart/) returned the league's own “404 – Flag on the Play.” body on 2026-11-17, and so did the league-wide nfl.com/depth-charts/. There is no depth chart to parse on nfl.com itself. Source of every row: nfl.com/teams/ (the league's own club index).

2 · The club route is open for all 32 clubs (answers G-02's second half)

Every club publishes /team/depth-chart on the host the league itself links as its “View Full Site”. 32/32 present · 31 machine-readable HTML tables · 1 image-only (Houston — the known limitation, re-confirmed). Total transcribed slots across the 31 table clubs: 901 off/def/special-team rows.

6 · Self-labels differ club by club

12 pages self-label unofficial; Indianapolis is labelled unofficial only in its dated release news; Seattle's page renders no heading at all; Arizona's heading says “Official Depth Chart” — the only club claiming official; New England states explicitly that the chart “reflects the opinions of the Patriots.com writers and don't necessarily reflect those of the organization”. No club page is a league-official statement of depth.

7 · What this sweep does not decide

  • Whether the deficiency feature may be built (SG-C) — still an owner verdict, left blank.
  • Whether any printed depth matches who actually played — that needs Game Book participation (covered elsewhere: nfl-collector, RC-07).
  • Houston's rows — image-only, so blank here; needs an authorized structured source, not transcription by eye (the inherited limitation).
  • Staleness of any specific row against the transactions log — only flagged where a page contradicts itself (see Flagged), never asserted.
