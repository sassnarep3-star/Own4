Answer, in two lines. The league route is closed: all 32 per-team depth paths on nfl.com — and the league-wide /depth-charts/ — return the league's own 404 body. The club route is open: all 32 clubs publish a depth page at /team/depth-chart, 31 of them as machine-readable tables. Houston is the single exception (chart is an image).
But the route is not yet a starter source. 15 rank-1 cells across 9 clubs hold two or more players, and 5 cells across 5 clubs are printed empty — column-1 would fabricate starters. Only 5 of 32 pages print an as-of date; 27 print none, so freshness is unverifiable for those. 12 pages self-label unofficial, one explicitly says it reflects only its writers' opinions. Depth must be modelled as groups, with capture timestamps — exactly as the brief requires — and the SG-C verdict on building the deficiency feature remains an owner decision this sweep does not take.
