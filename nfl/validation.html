Validation gates & leaderboard

Proposed gates, not empirically validated cutoffs. Numerical thresholds should be preregistered using a prospective power analysis and data-cost constraints, not tuned after inspecting the test set. There are no NFL entries eligible today.

GateRequirement before qualificationSource / rationale
V1 · provenanceDocument source, license, extraction time, game/player identifiers, revisions, and source-to-feature lineage. Exclude any field whose availability time is unknown.NFL Stats; dataset rights must be separately confirmed.
V2 · leakage controlChronological train/validation/test splits. Every feature must be reconstructible as known before the forecast cutoff. Freeze model and thresholds before holdout.Scikit-learn time-series cross-validation guidance (method reference, not NFL evidence).
V3 · benchmarkBeat a predeclared, simple baseline on proper scoring rules (e.g., Brier/log loss for probabilities); report calibration and sample size, not accuracy alone.Scikit-learn calibration guidance.
V4 · multiple testingRegister all strategy/threshold variants before evaluation; control family-wise false positives (e.g., Holm) and disclose the full test universe.NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook.
V5 · uncertaintyUse game/week-clustered uncertainty intervals; show season and week robustness; do not treat correlated observations as independent.NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook.
V6 · price/outcome integrityFor any betting-return claim, retain contemporaneous offered price, market/house, limits, settlement rules, timestamp and voids. Report ROI, units, drawdown and uncertainty net of fees.NFL source does not provide sportsbook price archives; values currently unavailable.
V7 · parlays / combosForecast joint outcomes directly or validate dependence; apply actual payout and correlation repricing. No independence assumption by default.Own4 dependence protocol; no NFL coefficients currently measured.
V8 · prospective replicationLock model, features, thresholds, and timestamped forecast log; evaluate on a later untouched period before “validated” status.Proposed governance; no NFL prospective trial conducted.
V9 · reward eligibilityPublic, reproducible scoring; equal information and timing; minimum sample/power; audit trail and tie policy. Rewards remain inactive without funding, terms and legal review.Not an NFL rule or existing program; no reward pool verified.

Leaderboard statuses

  • Research: hypothesis only.
  • Experimental: data and code reproducible, but gates incomplete.
  • Provisional: all retrospective gates passed, prospective replication pending.
  • Validated: all gates passed, including prospective replication and lawful data use.
  • Disqualified: leakage, unreproducible inputs, or failed preregistered criteria.
