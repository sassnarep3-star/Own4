Suggested path, with the gates that still matter

This page converts the evidence already recorded in the repository into an ordered recommendation. It does not alter any peer register, purchase data, claim a model edge, or record a reviewer verdict. Recommendations stay proposals unless a decision is explicitly registered in the owning governance file.

No reviewer decision recorded here

Recommendation at a glance: keep the project in research/shadow mode; do not publish market residuals, ROI, or paid rewards without price-source rights and settlement definitions; validate a singles baseline prospectively; keep injury/depth features out of trusted models until point-in-time availability and actual participation can be joined. Do not promote parlays or prop combos before directly measured joint probabilities and operator rules are available. These are conservative workflow recommendations, not claims that these data are impossible to obtain.

Decision list

Set the price-data policy before showing a market edge

What is known from this repository: the NFL Book price ledger holds originated fair-line snapshots; market-price/residual cells are blank under its stated evidence policy. The Decision Console records probes of NFL betting/odds routes, but that is not a survey of every licensed provider. This portal has not independently re-fetched those pages.

Suggestion: until there is a documented license and timestamped source, publish our fair line and inputs only; keep market line, residual, CLV and price-based ROI explicitly unavailable. If licensed odds are obtained, retain provenance, market/selection, timestamp, price format, operator and settlement/void rules with each record, and keep raw terms-restricted material private.

Path A — license a feed: proceed only after terms, coverage, history, timestamps and settlement fields are documented.
Path B — no feed yet (recommended default): preserve blank market values; run skill evaluation with proper scores, not price-based rewards.

Gate to clear: source authorization documented; open/close observations complete for the registered markets; settlement and void rules represented; independent price-integrity checks pass.

Review: price ledger · price-policy options · NFL Terms of Use · NFL odds page (repository probe record)

Keep singles as a shadow baseline; hold combinations behind a joint-evidence gate

What is known: the repository has pre-registration and leaderboard gates, and the NFL schedule/results pages provide game-level event context. A combination's probability cannot be justified by multiplying marginal probabilities unless the relevant dependence and settlement rules have been directly tested.

Suggestion: evaluate the frozen singles baseline first on prospective, chronological data. Publish proper scores against declared controls with game/season-cluster uncertainty. Do not rank same-game parlays or prop combos until the directly estimated joint probability beats a pre-registered threshold on a locked holdout and the relevant operator push/void rules are recorded.

Gate to clear: preregistered model and trial count; chronological final holdout untouched; benchmark-relative proper score with uncertainty; joint calibration and operator-specific settlement rules before a combo enters its own leaderboard division.

Review: strategy definitions · two-stage leaderboard · joint-evidence gate · NFL schedule · NFL scores

Make availability point-in-time evidence, then test the surprise—not the label level

What is known: official injury reports show reported designations at capture time; these are not themselves proof of who started or played. The existing registers document dated captures and open gaps around historical snapshots, inactive evidence and actual participation.

Suggestion: retain immutable, timestamped official report snapshots and deltas; join them only to dated official inactive/game evidence. Measure designation-to-play rates with sample sizes and uncertainty before feature use. Treat changes relative to the previous published report as candidates for a surprise signal; do not use an injury label as a standalone fixed penalty.

Gate to clear: repeatable multi-day snapshots with hashes and conflict flags; official game-day evidence joined by player/game; season-separated calibration with uncertainty; a chronological ablation showing incremental value over the same baseline.

Review: availability graph · ablation plan · dated evidence and deltas · NFL injury report · NFL transactions

Do not trust replacement deficiency until source coverage and slot semantics are solved

What is known: the repository's 32-club route sweep records a club-published page per team, with a machine-readable table for 31 and an image presentation for Houston; it also records shared and empty slots, dates missing from many pages, and differences in how clubs describe their charts. The 32-club sweep is evidence about source availability, not evidence that a chart rank equals playing time.

Suggestion: preserve raw text and slot groups; never infer depth from jersey number, experience, salary, or a sole rank-1 cell. Keep the Texans' unavailable structured values blank. Version any depth/availability evidence against official transactions. Build a playing-time feature only if an authorized, consistently available official snap source is verified across the full target set; otherwise leave the feature out.

Gate to clear: source route verified club by club; dated refresh and raw hash; roster/IR and transaction conflicts resolved; actual participation source confirmed; group/empty slot semantics preserved; no inference used to fill missing values.

Review: route findings · depth flags · matchup matrix · NFL team directory · NFL transactions

Make one review surface authoritative without deleting the research trail

What is known: several NFL workspaces contain overlapping requirement, question, source and suggestion registers. The Decision Console includes a repository census and a review queue. This is a repository organization issue, not an NFL fact.

Suggestion: use this NFL Decision Hub as a navigation entry point and designate one register as authoritative for recorded reviewer decisions. Keep older dashboards as linked evidence archives; do not mass-move or erase them. The selected owner should own the canonical verdict register, and every proposal should identify its evidence, gate, owner, status and reversal condition.

Gate to clear: an explicit canonical-register designation; duplicate proposals mapped to it without silently changing their status; old pages retained and linked; build/link checks pass.

Review: workspace census · existing review queue · NFL.com (project context only)

Defer weather, venue and regime features until their joins are reproducible

Suggestion: keep stadium roof/surface, hourly forecast, onside-kick regime and venue features as proposed datasets until each input is tied to published schedule/game facts with timestamps, coverage checks and a locked evaluation. An officially published forecast is not the weather at kickoff; actual roof state is not inferred from the forecast.

Gate to clear: official fixture/venue source per game; NWS forecast grid and forecast issue time captured at the model's decision time; roof state independently sourced; target definition and historical coverage documented; ablation on chronological holdouts.

Review: fixture context · venue register · National Weather Service · NFL schedules
