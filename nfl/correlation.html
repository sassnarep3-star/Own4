Correlation & joint-risk plan

Measured NFL correlation values: none. No values are filled in, because no NFL sample was analyzed in this repository. Correlation is dataset-, season-, selection- and conditioning-dependent; there is no universal “NFL parlay correlation” value.

Parlay-leg dependenceCompare direct joint hit-rate / calibrated joint probability against product of marginals; confidence intervals clustered by game-week.Cannot assess without paired, timestamped observations and offered prices.

Controls

  1. Define each event precisely, including overtime, pushes, voids and stat corrections from official rules/settlement terms.
  2. Use only observations available at the same forecast timestamp; prevent same-game leakage.
  3. Report sample size, uncertainty interval, season splits, multiplicity adjustment and sensitivity to extreme outcomes.
  4. Do not label association as causal or as a betting edge. Establish calibration and payout comparison separately.

Official definitions and raw stat availability are not themselves proof of these statistical quantities. See NFL receiving stats and NFL rules for source context only.
