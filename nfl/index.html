NFL Line Origination Lab

A source-led plan for developing and validating NFL game, player-prop, parlay and same-game combo signals.

Evidence boundary: This repository's existing empirical work is NBA, not NFL. This NFL section is a research specification only: no NFL dataset, fitted model, market line, validated edge, reward pool, or measured correlation is asserted. Missing values stay blank rather than being inferred.

Executive snapshot

Use official NFL statistics and rules to define outcomes; use timestamped, licensed point-in-time inputs for predictions. The first deliverable should be probability forecasts, not bet recommendations. Evaluate out-of-sample and compare with a documented baseline. A strategy is not leaderboard-eligible until it clears every predeclared gate in Validation.

Current measured NFL results: none. Current measured NFL dependence/correlation coefficients: none.
