Leaderboard & rewards

Competition rules are a proposal; there are no participants, rankings, prizes, or funded reward pool.

Proposed ranking

Freeze each forecast and candidate set before kickoff. For a common set of settled binary outcomes, rank gate-passing models by improvement in mean log loss versus a frozen baseline; clip probabilities by a preregistered epsilon. Report Brier score, reliability, game-clustered confidence intervals, coverage and net returns separately. Rank comparable markets separately; abstention cannot silently change the evaluation universe. Require a preregistered sample-size and precision/power plan before seeing the holdout. Apply a prespecified multiple-comparison procedure to promotion decisions. No gate pass means no eligible rank.

Potential reward policy: a fixed, externally funded pool may be split among eligible top ranks after a delayed audit. Funding, eligibility, ties, disputes, legal compliance and operator identity must be established first. This site makes no promise of compensation.
