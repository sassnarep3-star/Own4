Estimation protocol

For binary settled legs calculate φ = (p11 − p1p2) / √[p1(1−p1)p2(1−p2)] on the same sample, excluding voids under a predeclared policy. Report sample size, event definition, missingness and game-clustered bootstrap intervals; the statistic is undefined when a marginal is constant. Continuous residuals can use Pearson r, fitted only on training data. Stratify by era and position, shrink sparse estimates and validate joint probabilities on later games.

For two non-void legs, p11 = p1p2 + φ√[p1(1−p1)p2(1−p2)]; enforce probability bounds. Pairwise correlations alone do not determine a three-leg joint distribution. Simulate the complete joint outcome and settlement, including pushes and voids. With decimal payout d and no push, expected net return per unit is p·d − 1. Do not multiply leg probabilities unless independence is justified out of sample.
