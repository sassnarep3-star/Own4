Price-snapshot store

The contract for opening and closing numbers, and the honest count of what it holds.

A store that is empty and says why is a foundation. A store filled with an invented -110 is a fabricated dataset. This store defines the contract — one row per game, per market, per capture point, each requiring a value, a timestamp, an official URL and a hash, append-only, and refusing any assumed price — and then holds 39 slots with 0 filled.
