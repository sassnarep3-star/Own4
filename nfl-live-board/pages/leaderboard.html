Leaderboard — proper score, then escrow

Signal decides the ranking; staking cannot rescue a bad signal; multiplicity and clustering are printed beside every value.

Two stages, and only the first can rank. Stage 1 is a proper score: the Brier Skill Score against a benchmark the forecaster could compute before kickoff, with a 90% interval bootstrapped over weeks rather than games, and Holm-Bonferroni across all 5 registered strategies. Stage 2 is CLV/ROI, held in escrow, and it is blank because no closing price exists. A lucky staking run therefore cannot lift a strategy that failed stage 1 — which is exactly what the brief demands.

Result: 0 of 5 strategies qualify. Points awarded: 0 of 1000. The best point estimate is S1's +0.1253, and its week-clustered lower bound is above zero — but its unadjusted permutation p of 0.0333 becomes 0.1465 after the multiplicity adjustment for 5 trials, and that does not clear 0.05. Reporting the unadjusted value alone would be the error this rule exists to prevent.
