Executive summary

What is proven, what is not, and the three decisions that would unblock the most.

What is proven

  • The results feed is verified. 149 finals, reconciled to the official standings: 192 of 192 checks pass and the games-played identity is exact. Two independently maintained feeds agreed on all 137 games they shared.
  • A line was originated in the required order for all 13 Week-11 fixtures — side, then total, then a moneyline derived from the side — with p and its inputs stored for every forecast.
  • The competition is gated. Five strategies were pre-registered before scoring. 0 qualify. Reward points: 0 of 1000.
  • The luck channels are named and closed where possible. An assumed price, raw win rate, game-level independence, multiplicity and result-chasing are all removed. The control floor is computed: luck alone reached 0.0351.
  • Availability was re-verified independently. 96 rows across 8 clubs, 96 agreements, 0 disagreements. 25 reserve-list moves transcribed.

What is not proven, and will not be claimed

  • No edge. The best strategy's Brier skill is +0.1253 with a week-clustered interval of [0.0143, 0.2384], but its Holm-adjusted p across 5 registered strategies is 0.1465. The unadjusted p of 0.0333 would have looked like a result; the adjustment is why it is not one.
  • No market comparison. The market line and the difference are blank for all 13 fixtures. No official page publishes a price. A model that cannot be compared with a line cannot be said to beat one.
  • No replacement depth. No official depth chart and no official snap-count table were found. The 2nd/3rd/4th-string requirement and the matchup matrix are blocked on that, not on effort.
  • No availability ablation. One dated snapshot cannot be compared with a model that never saw it.
