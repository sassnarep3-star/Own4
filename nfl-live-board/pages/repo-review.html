A line-by-line audit of the repository's NFL namespaces, with its own limits stated.

What the audit checked, and what it did not. It enumerated every NFL namespace in the repository, scanned every page and every table row for a source link, classified every external host, looked for runtime references to a local or preview host, resolved every internal link this workspace publishes, and cross-checked the two independent results feeds game by game.
