Origination — side, then total, then moneyline

Thirteen Week-11 fixtures, priced by a frozen baseline, with fair, market and difference kept apart.

The order the brief demands. Stage 1 is the side, stage 2 is the total, stage 3 is the moneyline — and the moneyline is derived from the side, p(home) = Φ(fair margin / residual SD), never fitted separately, so the three stages cannot disagree. The model is a frozen shrunk points-for/points-against baseline with five stated parameters; it is a transparent benchmark, not a claimed edge.

The estimator is published twice, as the brief asks

Stage 1's side and stage 2's total are independent estimators of different quantities, and the moneyline is a transform of the side rather than a third estimate. The gap between the fair total and the total implied by the fitted margin is carried in the raw artefact (data/origination_week11.json) so the two can be compared before either is collapsed into one number.
