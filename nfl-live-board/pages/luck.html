Removal of luck

What was removed, what could not be, and the floor that luck alone produced at this sample size.

The question this panel answers: how good does a strategy look on this sample if it has no skill at all? Two luck-only strategies are scored in every run. The better of their upper bounds is the floor. A point estimate below it is indistinguishable from variance, whatever its p-value.
