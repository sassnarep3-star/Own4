What this page is

One workspace that answers the owner's brief as a single decision surface. It originates its own NFL lines in the required order (side, then total, then moneyline), scores strategies on a proper score with the luck taken out, and states for every requirement whether it is met, partly met, or blocked — and what number would clear the block. It does not restate the 36 peer workspaces in this repository; it links them and audits them.

The headline, in one sentence. The results feed is now verified against the official standings game for game (149 games, 192/192 checks, games-played identity exact), the model's side forecasts show a positive but not yet significant proper-score edge (Brier skill +0.1253, week-clustered interval 0.0143 to 0.2384, Holm-adjusted p 0.1465 across 5 registered strategies), no strategy qualifies, and 0 of 1000 reward points are awarded — because the two things that would make a strategy provable, a market price and a time series of availability, do not exist yet.
