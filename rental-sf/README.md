# San Francisco rent increase review

A privacy-preserving informational guide for the two supplied, anonymized rental-unit histories. Open [`index.html`](index.html) for the executive summary, unit-specific arithmetic, source-linked rule tables, process checklist, and flagged items.

## Boundaries

- Tenant names/initials and other personal information are intentionally excluded. Supplied dates and rents are preserved as requested.
- Tenancy facts were supplied by the user and are not independently verified. The arithmetic is illustrative only; no unit-specific legal rent is determined.
- Current official city annual allowable rate used in the guide: 1.6% for effective dates March 1, 2026–February 28, 2027.
- The guide is informational, not legal advice, a formal coverage determination, a rent notice, or a guarantee of attorney approval.
- Source links in the site point to official government pages. Values not established from official sources are left blank or explicitly flagged.

## Repo review disclosure

This repository's existing primary project is NBA analytics, unrelated to San Francisco rental law. The new material is isolated in `rental-sf/` and linked from the existing dashboard. Existing NBA pages/data were not reinterpreted as rental evidence. The site makes no claim that the rental facts were verified from the repository.
