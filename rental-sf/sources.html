Research limits

What these sources cannot prove

Government pages establish the legal framework and public rate tables. They do not prove the private rent paid, the last increase date, tenant identity/continuity, lease term, unit exemption, owner entity, or current license for an address not supplied. Those facts require private records or a unit-specific Rent Board/attorney review. The January 2025 referral listing is dated; contact providers to confirm current intake and availability.

No official owner-size study documenting typical corporate-versus-individual increase practices in San Francisco was identified in the material reviewed. That is a research-scope limitation, not a claim that no such research exists anywhere.
