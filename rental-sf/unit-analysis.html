Unit A: roommate change

Do not treat a roster update as a rent reset.

The owner-reported facts say at least one original occupant remains. San Francisco’s additional-occupant rule bars a rent increase solely because another occupant is added; the 6.14/Costa-Hawkins market-rate pathways generally require the last original occupant to leave and other conditions to be met. Verify the lease and whether each person is a co-tenant or subtenant before drawing a conclusion. Ordinance §37.3(a)(11) ↗ Roommate replacement ↗ Market-reset guidance ↗

Assess each unit on its own record

There is no obligation to raise both rents for symmetry. A neutral policy can result in different outcomes where lawful base rents, last increase dates, occupancy histories, or coverage differ. Applying the same dollar increase to both is not a substitute for correct, non-discriminatory calculations.
