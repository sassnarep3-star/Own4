A percentage is not a reason by itself; the legal pathway controlsAs of November 6, 2026

03 / Lawful grounds

Match every dollar to a recognized path.

For a locally controlled unit, general costs, market demand, or a new roommate are not free-standing authority to add an arbitrary surcharge. Identify the legal category, calculate it under the City rules, and document the supporting records.

Prudent explanation

Explain the actual legal category—do not embellish.

For an annual increase

A straightforward explanation is that the proposed amount is the annual adjustment published by the San Francisco Rent Board for the stated effective date. State the exact dollar and percentage calculation on the lawful base rent. The annual allowance is not an individualized invoice for repairs or insurance.

For banked or petition amounts

Name the banked years and percent, or the specific approved petition/passthrough and its attached calculation. Do not call an unverified estimate “banked,” or describe an ordinary expense as a certified improvement without the City’s decision.

✓

Recommended decision rule

For each unit, independently confirm (1) coverage and lawful base rent, (2) the last effective increase and full history, (3) the applicable path, (4) license and effective-date rate, (5) a compliant itemized notice, and (6) neutral, non-retaliatory treatment. If any input is unknown, pause rather than round up or assume.
