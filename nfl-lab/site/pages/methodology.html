Line definitions

ObjectDefinitionSource
team_a_spreadHalf-integer handicap minimizing absolute distance of modeled cover probability to 0.5; tie chooses the smaller threshold.2026 NFL Rulebook ↗
totalHalf-integer threshold minimizing absolute distance of modeled over probability to 0.5; tie chooses the smaller threshold.2026 NFL Rulebook ↗
moneylineOwn win-share probability P(win)+0.5*P(tie); fair decimal odds=1/p. This is a project draw-half convention, NOT a verified sportsbook settlement rule.2026 NFL Rulebook ↗
cross_game_parlayTwo eligible favorite outright-win legs in the same season-week, sorted by official game ID and paired deterministically. Leg p=P(outright win); a tied game is a lost leg for this research event, not a claimed sportsbook settlement. Joint p=p1*p2+rho*sqrt(p1*(1-p1)*p2*(1-p2)), clipped to Frechet bounds. Rho uses only earlier weeks.2026 NFL Rulebook ↗
same_game_comboSpread/total joint probability is summed over the same Poisson score grid, never multiplied as if independent. Model-implied dependence is not an empirical sportsbook correlation.2026 NFL Rulebook ↗
player_propHalf-integer line nearest the empirical median of observed 2026 regular-season values. Descriptive only; no availability or market-edge claim.2026 NFL Rulebook ↗
player_comboSame-player receptions and receiving-yard thresholds are evaluated on paired observed games. Cross-player same-game dependence is left blank without paired observations.2026 NFL Rulebook ↗
