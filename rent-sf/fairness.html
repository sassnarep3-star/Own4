A conservative recommendation, not a market survey

Consider the same annual-only policy for both units, up to 1.6% during the stated period, without assuming any banked increases. Offer more notice than the minimum and consider a smaller increase or none. Document neutral reasons and check each unit’s individual eligibility; do not use protected traits, complaints, or roommate replacement as a reason.
