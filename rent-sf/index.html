A clearer view of rent increases

A practical, privacy-conscious guide to evaluating two reported rent-controlled units in the Outer Sunset.

ANNUAL ALLOWABLE1.6%

Mar 2026 – Feb 2027

NOTICE AT ≤10%30 days

Plus mailing time, if applicable

RECOMMENDED LENSAnnual only

No assumed banked increases

Decision brief

Use the local annual allowance as a ceiling for a cautious annual-only option, not a market-rate target. Eligibility, lawful rent and notice must be confirmed independently for each unit. A lower increase or no increase is also possible.
