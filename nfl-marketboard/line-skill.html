Line-making skill

Are our numbers closer to the official result than the market's?
This is the cleanest signal measure we have, because it involves no betting at all. Take the official result, and ask how far our originated number was from it versus how far the market number was. If we were making better lines, our error would be smaller. It is not.

Delta is market MAE minus our MAE, so positive would mean we are closer. Both are negative. The total gap (-0.755 points) clears conventional significance at p = 0.046462 — in the wrong direction. With 105 graded games this is a stable measurement of the current model, not a sampling artefact, and it is the strongest single reason no strategy is close to qualifying.
