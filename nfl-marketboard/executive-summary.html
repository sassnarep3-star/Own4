Decision

Nothing here supports betting anything yet. The model loses to the market on both markets it originates, the sample is at 46% of the pre-registered floor, and after correcting for the eight strategies we tested, the best q-value is 0.2893. The single most useful next step is not more data — it is fix the model before adding more data, because collecting more games would only make this negative result more precise.
