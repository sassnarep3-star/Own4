Promotion & demotion

The rules that send a live strategy back to paper.

Demotion: live back to paper

A demotion gate is what stops a strategy from being kept alive by explanation. Six triggers are evaluated on every build against every strategy; any one firing sends a live strategy back to paper. They are applied automatically, not retrospectively.
