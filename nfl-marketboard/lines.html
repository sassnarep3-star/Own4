Originated lines

Side, then total, then moneyline — fair, market and difference in separate columns.
Origination runs in one order and the third number depends on the first two. The side comes from the margin model; the total comes from the same model's scoring rates; the moneyline is then derived by pushing the fair spread through a normal distribution whose width is scaled by the fair total. That is the order the brief asks for, and it is enforced in code rather than by convention. Fair, market and difference are three separate columns on every row.

Method

E_away_ptsL + off[away] + def[home] - HFA/2nfl.com standings ↗

Each formula is pre-registered verbatim in data/model_card.json; the link is to the official feed the formula consumes.
