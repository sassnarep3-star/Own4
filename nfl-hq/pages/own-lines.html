Price and edge are blank on purpose. The NFL publishes no betting lines. The market spread column is third-party (nflverse/nfldata) and is labelled as such; it is a comparison value, not a price we can obtain. The method, the pre-registered parameters and the walk-forward discipline behind these numbers are documented in the peer workspace: nfl-lines ↗.

How a line is produced

A weighted recency model of scoring margin and total, refitted before every kickoff on strictly earlier games only, with a ridge penalty and a floor on the residual standard deviation. The parameters were written to a registered file before the first walk-forward run, so the out-of-sample claim cannot have been tuned into existence. Full description and the reproduction commands: nfl-lines method ↗.
