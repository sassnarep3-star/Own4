How the reward works

A strategy earns a reward when it clears all eight pre-registered gates, which include a positive confirmation-period ROI, a p-value that survives Benjamini-Hochberg control across the whole strategy family, and an edge larger than the bootstrap luck band. The prize, the payment trigger and the tie-break rule have not been set, and that is recorded as Q03 and Q04. Drawdown and pushes are handled as described in the peer workspace: nfl-lines ↗.

Nothing is awarded this season. Zero strategies clear the gates. The board is published anyway, because a leaderboard with no winners is the honest result and it shows exactly how far each strategy is from qualifying.
