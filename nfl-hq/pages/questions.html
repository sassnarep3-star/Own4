Open questions

Nothing on this site has been implemented on the strength of an assumption. These are the questions that have to be answered before a line is bet, a strategy is promoted or a suggestion is built. Each one states what is already known, what is not, who can answer it and whether it blocks a decision.

Q01What is the executable price we will actually get, and from which source?ROI, break-even rate and every reward tier depend on the price. The NFL publishes no betting lines.We have closing lines and prices for every game in the third-party archive, verified against NFL.com final scores for Week 9.We have no verified executable price for any future game, no evidence of what price our own lines would attract, and no second archive to cross-check the first.Sportsbook market operator, or a second licensed price feed.Blockingsee
Q02Which market does Own4 compete in, and which markets are out of scope?The strategy families, the correlation work and the leaderboard all depend on the market being fixed.The research covers spread, total, moneyline, same-game parlays and a player-prop proxy.No owner has stated which market Own4 is trying to be better than, or what a realistic price advantage is worth in points.Project owner.Blockingsee
Q03What bankroll, unit size and maximum exposure govern the competition?Drawdown limits and the reward rule cannot be set without a risk budget, and maximum drawdown is already reported per strategy.Every strategy is measured in flat 1-unit stakes, which is the standard neutral unit.No bankroll, no unit size, no per-strategy or per-correlated-cluster exposure cap, and no drawdown tolerance.Project owner.Blockingsee
Q04What exactly is the reward, and what has to happen for it to be paid?The leaderboard has to be a competition with a prize, not a report.The leaderboard ranks by z-score against break-even and publishes reward eligibility per strategy.No reward amount, no payment trigger, no tie-break rule and no payment schedule.Project owner.Blockingsee
Q05What sample size and holdout split define a strategy as qualified?Every gate threshold is a choice; a threshold picked after seeing results is not a gate.The gates are pre-registered with a timestamp written before the first backtest run, and each strategy has a development and a confirmation period.No owner has confirmed that the pre-registered thresholds are the thresholds they want, and the minimum sample floor has not been ratified.Project owner, with a reviewer who did not write the model.Blockingsee
Q06At what measured correlation does a parlay stop being a sum of its legs?Parlay pricing and the dependence gate both turn on it.13 correlations are measured with confidence intervals, including same-game dependence, and parlay legs are priced with the measured dependence rather than assuming independence.No declared threshold at which a leg combination is refused, and no rule for cross-game clusters that share a quarterback, a venue or a weather cell.Project owner, with the model author.Blockingsee
Q07Are player props in scope, and if so whose prop lines will we price against?Player-prop combinations are one of the three families the request names.A prop-combo framework and a proxy of prop outcomes exist and are measured.No official player-prop line exists anywhere, no prop variance is verifiable, and the proxy is not a price.Project owner, and a prop-price data provider.Blockingsee
Q08What is the depth-chart source of truth for a second, third or fourth string backup?This is the only way to answer the request about backup starters replacing starters, and it is the single largest gap in the tracker.The official game book, published after each game, prints the depth code for each substitute, and two worked examples are proved from it.NFL.com publishes no pre-game depth chart, no depth rank and no reason for a replacement. There is no official pre-kickoff source at all.A licensed pre-game depth chart provider, or the club's own published depth chart, plus a weekly game-book transcription task.Blockingsee
Q09How should a starting quarterback change be priced, given the model has no quarterback feature?NFL.com published at least six quarterback changes for Week 10 and the model cannot see any of them.The quarterback rows are published for every team on the report, and the official news headlines are captured with their NFL.com links.No feature, no weight and no test exist for a quarterback change, so its effect is entirely outside the model.Model author and project owner.Blockingsee
Q10How often must the official injury report be re-read, and who owns the diff?The report is rewritten every day. A stale snapshot is a wrong lineup.The diff between the 2026-11-12 and 2026-11-13 reports is published, showing exactly what changes in a day.No schedule, no automated fetch job and no owner for the diff.Project owner and whoever will run the fetch job.Blockingsee
Q11Who signs off on a suggestion before it is implemented, and what is the record of that decision?The request asks for suggestions that a person reviews and chooses from. That requires a named decision owner.Each suggestion on the board has a decision status field and a recommendation.No decision owner is named, no review cadence is set, and no decision log records what was chosen and why.Project owner.Blockingsee
Q12What is the promotion path from research to live, and where is the hard stop?A strategy that passes its gates is still not a strategy anyone should stake.The gates, the confirmation period and the reward rule already exist.No promotion path, no live-staking criteria, no kill switch and no definition of what happens when a live strategy starts losing.Project owner.Blockingsee
Q13How are pushes and ties scored in the leaderboard?Pushes occurred in 107 games at the spread and 36 at the total across the sample, so the rule matters.Pushes are excluded from the win rate and are counted and published as their own column.No statement of whether a pushed bet earns or loses its stake, and no agreed treatment of a tie game.Project owner.Non-blockingsee
Q14Do we benchmark against closing lines or opening lines, and is the archive's line the benchmark?Closing lines are the hardest price to beat and therefore the correct benchmark for an edge claim.The archive stores spread lines and both prices for every game, and the strategy rules use the market line in the comparison.It is not established that the archive's line is a true closing line, and there is no second source to confirm it.Price-data provider and project owner.Blockingsee
Q15What official source governs weather and roof, if any is used?One strategy relies on wind at 15 mph or above, and the roof and surface fields come from the third-party archive.The wind, roof and surface values are present for every archived game and the wind strategy is measured.No official NFL weather source exists, and future-game weather cannot be verified from any official source.Official league or stadium weather feed, if one exists.Non-blockingsee
Q16What is the full official evidence chain for a player-prop price and a prop-pair correlation?Prop combinations are a named family and one of the two most blocked areas of the research.The framework, the proxy and the dependence control are described and measured.No official prop line, no prop variance and no prop-pair correlation is verifiable, so the combo family is priced against a proxy rather than a market.Prop-price data provider.Blockingsee
Q17How many historical games should be re-read from NFL.com before the archive is trusted wholesale?The archive supplies 4,055 out-of-sample forecasts. Right now 15 games were re-read in this pass.15 of 15 completed Week 9 games match exactly, and the previous session re-read 45 games across three weeks.Only 60 of 4,055 forecast games have been re-read from official pages. The earlier seasons rest on the archive alone.Project owner, to decide whether a full re-read is worth the effort.Blockingsee
Q18Which namespace is canonical, and what happens to the other NFL workspaces?The repository carried 16 overlapping NFL namespaces before this integration, each with its own copy of the questions, suggestions and flags.This site is the canonical entry point and every other namespace is linked and described on the namespace map.No decision has been taken to retire, archive or merge the duplicates, and several peer pages carry a hard-coded namespace count that is now stale.Project owner.Blockingsee
Q19Should the daily official-data read run in continuous integration, and where does it store the raw capture?The official injury report changes every day; a manual re-read will fail.Every raw capture used here is committed under this namespace with a SHA-256 and a retrieval timestamp, and the diff is published.No scheduled workflow exists to re-read, diff and publish the daily update.Whoever will run the workflow.Blockingsee
Q20What is the acceptance criterion for a strategy that is 'selectively skill based'?The request asks for strategies that are skill based rather than lucky, and the current rule is a gate list rather than a definition.Luck is measured three ways: a bootstrap band around break-even, a permutation test against random selection and false-discovery control across the strategy family.There is no agreed definition of what fraction of a strategy's result may come from luck and still count as skill, and no threshold on the bootstrap band has been ratified.Project owner with a reviewer independent of the model author.Blockingsee

How to use this register

A suggestion on the Suggestions board is ready for implementation only when every question it depends on is marked answered. The Decision status column on the suggestions board shows exactly which questions each one waits on, so a reviewer can choose a path without first reading everything else.
