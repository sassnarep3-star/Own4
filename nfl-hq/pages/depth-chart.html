Depth chart and replacements

The request asks for second, third and fourth string backups replacing starters. This page separates what an official source can prove from what it cannot, and publishes every piece of official evidence that exists.

The honest answer. NFL.com publishes no pre-game depth chart, no depth rank and no reason for a replacement. Every depth-rank cell in this repository is therefore published blank and flagged. The only official depth evidence is the substitution code printed in the official game book after the game, which proves what happened but cannot set a pre-kickoff line.

Worked examples from the official game books

The NFL game book, published after each game, lists both starting lineups and every substitution with a depth code such as 3QB. Comparing the same position across consecutive weeks proves who actually started in place of whom, without a depth chart and without inference. Two examples are proved in the peer workspace.

What would close the gap

Nothing available today closes it. A pre-kickoff depth signal needs either a licensed pre-game depth chart or a full season of official game books transcribed into a substitution history, from which a pre-kickoff model could be built. Both are costed on the Suggestions board as S05 and S13, and the decision is recorded as Q08.
