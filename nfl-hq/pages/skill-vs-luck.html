Skill versus luck

How luck is removed from the comparison, and what remains when it is. Five independent controls are applied to every strategy: a walk-forward walk with no look-ahead, a development and a confirmation period, false-discovery control across the strategy family, a bootstrap luck band around break-even, and a permutation test against random selection.

Read the confirmation column before anything else. Several strategies carry a positive development ROI and a negative confirmation ROI. That is the signature of luck rather than skill, and it is the reason the leaderboard awards nothing. The definition of how much luck a strategy may contain and still count as skill has not been ratified, and that is recorded as Q20.

The walk-forward discipline

The model is refitted before every kickoff on strictly earlier games only, with a capped training window, and the strategy thresholds are read from a file registered with a timestamp written before the first backtest run. The look-ahead test result, the calibration and the permutation tests are published in the peer workspace: nfl-lines ↗.

Where the research is still exposed

The model has no quarterback feature and no depth-chart feature, and the rest-differential term is large at 0.71 points per day, which drives the single biggest model-versus-market disagreement in the sample. Those are the first two things a reviewer should look at, and both are on the Flagged for Review page.
