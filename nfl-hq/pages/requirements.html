What "Partial" means here

One requirement is partial: the injury and depth-chart tracker. The injury half is complete and verified. The depth half is blocked by the absence of an official depth chart, and that is a source limitation rather than an unfinished task. The exact missing input, the sources that could close it and the cost of closing it are recorded as Q08 and S05.

Questions and suggestions that must be answered first

No line can be bet, no strategy can be promoted and no suggestion can be implemented until the open questions are answered and a reviewer has chosen from the suggestions board. Both are deliberately left in a reviewable, un-actioned state.
