What is verified, and what is not

Verified against an official source

32 league standings rows, 32 official team-stat rows, the complete Week 10 schedule with 14 game URLs and 4 bye teams, 321 official injury rows across 14 games, 15 Week 9 final scores re-read from NFL.com, and 24 NFL.com news headlines published on the official injury page.

Not verifiable from an official source

Betting prices and market lines (the NFL publishes none), depth-chart ranks, player-prop lines, player-prop variances and prop-pair correlations, the reason a player appeared or disappeared from the daily report, and the historical coverage of the third-party archive. Each of these is published as a blank cell and listed on the Flagged for Review page.

Known weaknesses in the research itself

The model has no quarterback or depth-chart feature, so it cannot see the six quarterback changes that NFL.com itself published for Week 10. The rest-differential term is large. Only the most recent completed week was re-read from official pages in this pass; the archive's earlier seasons have not been re-read line by line from NFL.com.
