Each suggestion states the problem, the options with their trade-offs, a recommendation, the effort and impact, and the decision status. Nothing on this board has been implemented unless the status says so.

S08Add a liquidity and line-movement gate before any strategy can be promoted.The archive records the spread, both prices and the total with both prices. Option B: gate on price availability and on movement between open and close. Option C: full order-book modelling.Option B is recommended and is cheap, because the archive already holds the fields.MediumHighRecommendQ01, Q14see

Option B: pre-register everything, including additions, with a dated amendment. Option C: hold-out a permanent untouched test set.Option B is implemented for the model and its strategy thresholds. How to use this board

Pick a suggestion, read the questions it waits on, and record the choice. Two suggestions are already implemented because they were part of building this site: S01, the consolidation into one canonical site, and S07, ranking the leaderboard by z-score against break-even. Every other suggestion waits on a decision from the project owner, recorded as Q11.
