Strategy research / Validation gates

Validation gates

The gates every strategy must clear, written down and pre-registered before the backtest was run, with the measured result for each strategy. The gate rule text is fixed; it is not a set of conclusions.

Validation gates &#183; 8 pre-registered gates
IDGate as writtenWhat it is forSource
G1Sample size: at least 250 graded betsThe floor on how much evidence a strategy needs before it can be judged at all. A strategy with fewer graded bets has not been tested, it has merely been observed.rule text ↗
G2Confirmation period (2022-2026) return on investment is positiveThe gate that separates skill from luck: the period that was never used to build the strategy has to agree in sign with the period that was.rule text ↗
G3Statistically significant after Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction across all strategiesMultiple-testing control across the whole strategy family, so that testing fifteen rules does not manufacture a winner by chance.rule text ↗
G4Observed ROI lies above the 97.5th percentile of the no-skill null distributionEffect size against the null: how large an edge pure chance produces on a sample this size.rule text ↗
G5Development period (2011-2021) return on investment is positiveThe mirror of G2 on the development period, so that a strategy cannot pass by being lucky in one half of the sample only.rule text ↗
G6Profitable in at least 60% of individual seasonsConsistency across seasons, which is where a strategy that worked for one hot run fails.rule text ↗
G7Maximum drawdown no worse than 25 units at 1 unit per betRisk: a strategy that is right on average but pays for its wins with long losing runs is not usable.rule text ↗
G8Every bet was priced with a price recorded in the source archiveSource compliance: a bet priced at a guessed price is not a result, so every price has to come from the recorded archive.rule text ↗
