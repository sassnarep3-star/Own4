How to read the calibration table

If the model were calibrated, the actual win rate in each bin would sit on the predicted rate. Model Brier 0.22652 against a market Brier of 0.21059 and a base-rate Brier of 0.24729; model log loss 0.64394 against 0.6086. Lower Brier score and lower log loss are better. The market row is the benchmark built from the third-party closing moneylines with the overround removed two-way.

Correlation is not causation

A measured correlation between two team statistics says the two move together across 4055 games. It does not say that one causes the other, and it does not transfer to a future season automatically. Every value above is computed from the third-party game archive; the official NFL pages publish no correlation. The decision threshold that turns a correlation into a refused parlay leg is recorded as Q06.
