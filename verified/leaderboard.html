Competition / Leaderboard

Leaderboard and rewards

The competition is designed to reward demonstrated, replicated skill — not a hot week. A strategy earns a place only by clearing all ten gates; until then its reward is exactly zero, however good its record looks.

Scoring rules

Eligibility: all ten gates must pass on the same locked snapshot. Ranking among eligible strategies is by out-of-sample Brier score for probability markets and by MAE for line markets, with the sample size and the FDR-adjusted p-value published next to each rank. A strategy that fails a gate scores nothing — there is no partial credit for a good-looking record, because partial credit is how luck gets paid.

Standings: no qualified entries

Zero of eight strategies qualify, so the reward pool is unpaid. This is the correct output of the design, not a failure of it: the sample is 35 graded games against a 300-game floor, and the one nominal signal does not survive multiple-testing control.

What a qualifying submission would look like

It would need a locked, timestamped snapshot; at least 300 graded games; a price from an official or licensed source so expected value is computable; a measured dependence adjustment for every combination; FDR survival across every test in the run; and the same sign of edge in three separate time blocks. None of those conditions is met by anything published so far in this repository.
