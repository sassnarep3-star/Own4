Why these three families

The three strategy families
FamilyWhat it isWhy it is hardSource
SinglesOne market, one game.The only family where a signal can be measured cleanly: one Bernoulli draw per game, no joint distribution to estimate.Gates ↗
ParlaysTwo or more markets multiplied into one ticket.Profitable only if the joint probability is priced with measured dependence; pricing legs as independent inflates every long shot.Gates ↗
Player-prop combosTwo or more player markets in the same game.The entire edge lives in the correlation between the players' outcomes, and that correlation cannot be measured without per-game logs.Gates ↗

What would make a strategy eligible

A strategy needs, in order: an official or licensed price to compare against; a sample of at least 300 graded games; a probabilistic edge that survives multiple-testing control; and the same sign of edge in three separate time blocks. Reaching 300 graded games at this sample's coverage would require roughly nine weeks of the season, so no shortcut exists.
