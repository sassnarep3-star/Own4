Validation gates

Ten gates, written to verification/data/gates.json before the analysis was run. Two of them are checks on the research itself (source compliance, reproducibility); seven are statistical; one is market access. A strategy is eligible only if all ten pass.

Scoreboard: 4 pass, 5 fail, 1 blocked. The failures are not close calls. The sample floor is missed by 265 games, the winner model loses to the free home baseline, and the single nominal result fails multiple-testing control once both strategy tests are counted.

Calibration of the originated win probability

A probability is only useful if games predicted at 60% happen about 60% of the time. With 35 graded games the buckets are small, and the middle bucket is visibly noisy — which is exactly the point of the sample floor.
