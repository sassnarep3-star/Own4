Availability-blind and price-blind. These are team-strength lines. They do not know who is injured (the official injury report was not transcribed, flag F-INJURY) and they are not compared with any market, so no edge, stake or expected return is claimed. Every price column is blank by design.

Parlays — why the legs cannot be multiplied

Inside a single game the legs are not independent. Two measurable dependencies matter for a winner-plus-total parlay, and both are reported with a confidence interval on the Correlations page. On the graded sample the observed joint hit rate of the originated winner and the originated over was 0.2571 against an independence product of 0.2939 — a gap of -0.0367 on 35 games. The gap is descriptive, not significant, and it is the reason gate G6 forbids independence pricing.

Player props — reference points only

Official season averages exist for the leading player of each team on the slate. Per-game distributions do not: no official game logs are available under the official-source rule, so variance, hit rate around a line and teammate-to-teammate correlation cannot be measured. Rather than invent them, the probability and edge columns are blank and strategies S6 and S7 are blocked (flag F-PROPS).
