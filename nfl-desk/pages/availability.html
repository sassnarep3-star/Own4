Availability & depth · 2026-11-19

Who is out, and whether a replacement can be confirmed

Two different facts are often confused here: who is listed as out, and who actually replaced them. The first is published and countable. The second needs inactive lists and snap counts, and this repository has neither.

4. The link that is missing

To say "the starter was replaced by a third-string tackle" you need three joins that do not exist here: an expected starter (dated depth chart), an actual starter (Game Book), and a replacement's playing time (snap count). The desk publishes the listed designations because they are official and countable, and refuses the replacement claim because it is not.
