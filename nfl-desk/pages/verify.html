What this desk checks before it publishes, and what it does not

A green check here means the pages are internally consistent and every row carries a source. It does not mean the numbers are true, and it does not mean any other workspace agrees.

What is not checked

This verifier does not fetch the official pages at build time, so it cannot detect that a source has changed since the read date. It does not re-verify peer workspaces' claims. It does not check that a figure is economically meaningful — only that it is sourced and reproducible.

Research only, not wagering advice. A value that could not be confirmed from an official source is blank and flagged.
