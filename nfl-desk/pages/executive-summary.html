Executive summary · 2026-11-19

The NFL programme in one page

Written for a reader with two minutes. Every number below is a count produced by a script in this folder and every figure is linked to the file it came from.

1. What exists

42 NFL workspaces sit beside this one. Between them they hold price stores, results feeds, availability captures, depth sweeps, strategy registers, 43 flagged pages and three competing requirement registers. 40 of them ship a verifier; none compares one workspace with another, which is why the same fact can carry four values and nothing fails.

3. What the evidence does not support

Nothing in this repository demonstrates a profitable strategy, and no price is officially sourced, so no ROI claim can be computed from verified numbers. The most honest published result is a walk-forward backtest of 35 graded games that does not beat a home-team baseline (0.571 against 0.600, one-sided p 0.70) while a totals model clears a permutation test but fails the sample floor and multiplicity control.
