Validation gates — executed in code

Thresholds are project policy (not mandated by any official source) and were fixed in research/config.py before the holdout was first opened. Source links point to the official definitions/processes each gate relies on.

IDGateTypeRuleSource
G1Temporal integrity (no look-ahead)executed testEvery feature for a game uses only earlier games of the same season (shift-by-one). Perturbation test: adding 40 points to a game's own result must leave that game's features unchanged (team test) and adding 30 points to a player's box score must leave that player-game's features unchanged (player test), while later games change.SRC-13
G2Sample sizepolicyHoldout must contain at least 1,000 settled bets (singles/props) or 300 parlays. Underpowered results cannot earn the top tier.project policy — no official source applies; SRC-13
G3Beats the market proxypolicyMean absolute error of the strategy's line must be lower than the market-proxy line (50/50 season-to-date/recency blend) on all holdout rows, one-sided p < 0.05 with date-clustered standard errors.project policy — no official source applies; SRC-13
G4Statistical significance after multiple testingpolicyHit rate must exceed the 52.38% break-even implied by an ASSUMED −110 price (1/1.9091) with Holm-adjusted one-sided p < 0.05 across ALL registered trials (27 single/prop trials). Parlay types are tested on ROI > 0 with Holm across the parlay types that could be built. Standard errors are clustered by game date.project policy — no official source applies; SRC-13
G5Repeatability (luck removal)policyHit rate > 50% in at least 3 of the 4 holdout seasons, > 50% in the dev sample at the same threshold, and > 50% in both alternating-week halves of the holdout.project policy — no official source applies; SRC-13
G6Threshold robustnesspolicyHit rate > 50% at all three pre-registered edge thresholds (top 50%, 25%, 10% of dev |edge|), so the result does not hinge on one cut-off.project policy — no official source applies; SRC-13
G7No directional biaspolicyShare of Over/Home sides must stay within 35%–65%, and the hit rate must beat a directional null (each season's own over-rate) with p < 0.05 — a strategy cannot win by just always leaning one way.project policy — no official source applies; SRC-13
G8Parlay correlation screenpolicyAverage pairwise correlation between leg outcomes must satisfy |ρ| ≤ 0.10, because the ASSUMED independent-leg price would otherwise be mispriced (sportsbooks reprice correlated legs).project policy — no official source applies; SRC-13
G9Parlay size cappolicyAt most 3 legs per parlay.project policy — no official source applies; SRC-13
G10Availability checkpolicy + official processProps are voided if the player does not play (as books do). For live use, the official NBA Injury Report must be checked: status due 5 p.m. local the day before a game; 1 p.m. local on the second night of a back-to-back; updated continually on game day. This check is NOT automated in the backtest (historical reports not collected).SRC-08
G11Placebo calibration (system)executed testRandom-side strategies placed on identical bet sets must pass the significance gate no more often than 5% of the time (observed: 0 of 2,700).project policy — no official source applies; SRC-13
G12Prospective replicationNOT RUNStrategy must replicate on games played AFTER the code and thresholds were frozen. Not possible with the available dataset (ends 2023-24); this is why the highest tier is labeled "Provisional".project policy — no official source applies; SRC-13
