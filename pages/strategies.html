Why these are hypotheses

NBA Stats supplies definitions and public team/player statistics, and NBA Official describes injury-report timing and requirements. Neither source establishes predictive advantage or makes wagering recommendations. Price, lineup, historical outcome and grading data must be assembled and independently tested before any candidate can be ranked as successful.
