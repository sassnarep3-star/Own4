Methodology & sources

How lines are made, how strategies are judged, where every number comes from.

Model

  • Rating: R = (Σ margin + k·R₂₅)/(n + k), k=41, n = games played; R₂₅ = official 2025-26 +/- per game.
  • Margin = R_home − R_away + H, H = 1.725 (GP-weighted mean 2025-26 home +/-).
  • Total = O_h + D_a + O_a + D_h − 2·L with O = points for, D = points against per game (same blend), L = league mean O.
  • Probabilities: Φ(margin/σ), σ = expanding-window residual RMSE (needs ≥30 earlier games). Same-game parlays: bivariate normal with the residual margin–total correlation.
  • Own line = model margin/total rounded to 0.5; fair price = no-vig price of the model probability.
  • Limitations: no opponent adjustment of current-season margins, no injuries/rest/travel, tiny sample.

Luck-removal protocol

  • Gates and strategy catalogue committed before evaluation (commit 0b97f52); walk-forward with strict date cut-off; proper scoring rules; paired bootstrap (10,000 reps, seed 20261102); Bonferroni across 6 tests; min n=300; half-sample stability; market gate for any real-money use.
