🏆 Leaderboard — singles, player props, parlays & combos

How to read this. Every entry is a strategy that originates its own line, evaluated once on 4,144 frozen holdout games (2020-21 → 2023-24). "Hit rate" is the share of bets where the outcome landed on the strategy's side of the market proxy line. Break-even at an assumed −110 price is 52.38%. Rewards are recognition tiers/points only (no cash). Tier rules: Provisional Gold = passes every gate; Silver = significant after Holm correction but fails only sample-size (G2) and/or directional-bias (G7); Bronze = beats break-even at raw p<0.05 but does not survive multiple-testing correction; Control = calibration check, never ranked. Points = tier base (100/50/10) + lower-bound edge in percentage points. “Provisional” because gate G12 (prospective replication) has not been run. All edges are versus a proxy, not sportsbook prices — see Research and Flagged for Review.
