Executive summary

State of the NFL origination system, every claim source-linked. Companion markdown: EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md.

Bottom line. The system now records, for every market it originates, an opening and a closing number with provenance and settlement, and a verified results feed that makes backtests and ROI computable at all. On the evidence available today, no strategy has yet cleared the luck barrier (best BH-adjusted p = 0.44), so the leaderboard ranks skill signals honestly and holds all rewards. One signal under continued paper tracking: unders in outdoor games with wind ≥ 15 mph (n=303, hit 55.4%, z=1.43) — not yet distinguishable from luck.
