Singles/props overall1 of 27 registered trials pass every gate (P3 top 50%); 13 more are Silver; the rest fail on significance or repeatability. Nothing is called validated: gate G12 (prospective replication) has not been run.SRC-13 SRC-15 ⚠
Player propsAll four prop strategies beat the market proxy (MAE p<0.05) and survive Holm correction at their dev-selected threshold (e.g. P4 PRA top 10%: 60.6% on 4,573 bets). But they lean Over 68%–76% of the time, so most fail gate G7 (directional bias); only P3 (assists) at top 50% passes everything. Likely reason: the proxy ignores minutes information, which is what these strategies exploit — a real market prices minutes.SRC-13 SRC-15 ⚠
Game totalsT1 (pace form + league scoring drift) reaches 56.9% at top 25% (Silver) but leans Under 65%; T2 (rest flags alone) has no skill (hit 51.9%, IC +0.007). Rest does not move totals: back-to-back vs total-residual correlation is about zero (holdout -0.008 home / +0.010 away).SRC-13 SRC-15 ⚠
Point spreadsM2 (rest differential) at top 10%: 59.3% on only 636 bets (Silver — underpowered, G2). Back-to-back effects are small but real: home back-to-back vs home-margin residual -0.061, away back-to-back +0.059 (holdout).SRC-13 SRC-15 ⚠
Calibration controlsT3 and M1 only re-weight the recency term already inside the proxy. They would pass the gates (e.g. M1 top 50% 56.6%), which shows the proxy’s 0.5 recency weight is arbitrary rather than revealing any market inefficiency. They are excluded from ranking.SRC-13 SRC-15 ⚠
Why not a naive baseline?Iteration 1 settled against a plain season-to-date line: simply betting the direction of recency form hit 60.7% on totals and 57.6% on player points. That is not credible as skill — the naive baseline lags in-season trends. The proxy was strengthened (flagged: the holdout was viewed before the redesign).SRC-13 SRC-15 ⚠
Parlays & combos5 parlay types pass every gate (XG2, XG3, SGP2, Teammates: two player-points legs, same team, Opponents: one player-points leg per team); their ROI is inherited from leg edges versus the proxy at assumed −110 prices, so it is not an achievable sportsbook return. Same-player combos (PTS+REB, PTS+AST, REB+AST, PRA) show ρ≈0.15–0.19 and are blocked by G8.SRC-13 SRC-15 ⚠
Dependence structureTeammates’ top-2 scorers’ points residuals are uncorrelated (+0.014); opposing top scorers are mildly positive (+0.092); a top scorer’s points vs the game total is +0.236. Within a player: points↔rebounds +0.251, points↔assists +0.138, rebounds↔assists +0.175.SRC-13 SRC-15 ⚠

Method

StepDefinitionSource
Market proxy (stand-in for a sportsbook)Season-to-date mean + 0.5 × (recency EWMA − season mean), shifted by a constant so 50% of dev outcomes land over. No sportsbook prices are used anywhere.SRC-13
Own lineProxy + Σ βᵢ·(feature − dev mean). β from OLS on dev only, then frozen. Features: pace form, league drift, back-to-back flags, rest differential (teams); minutes trend, team back-to-back, home, game pace (players).SRC-13
Bet ruleBet the side of the adjustment (Over/Home if adjustment > 0) when |adjustment| is in the top 50% / 25% / 10% of the dev distribution. All three thresholds are registered trials.SRC-13
SettlementOutcome vs proxy line; pushes void. Break-even 52.38% at an ASSUMED −110 price (decimal 1.9091).SRC-13
Standard errorsClustered by game date (props within a night are not independent).SRC-13
Multiple testingHolm-Bonferroni across all 27 registered single/prop trials (parlays: across built parlay types).SRC-13
Luck removalDirectional-null test, season-by-season and alternating-week repeatability, threshold robustness, placebo strategies, clustered errors, frozen holdout.SRC-13
