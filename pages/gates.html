Validation gates and the demotion gate

A strategy must pass the validation gates G1–G8 before it can be promoted or rewarded. The demotion gates DG-1..DG-4 send a live strategy back to paper when their trigger fires. Thresholds not fixed by an external standard are marked project-defined and appear on the suggestions board for owner choice.
