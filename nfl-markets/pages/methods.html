Methods & verification

Origination model (v1, frozen)

Elo with margin-of-victory multiplier for sides; EWMA OFF/DEF point ratings for totals; fair ML from the fair spread via a frozen normal CDF. Calibration (slope 0.03777, HFA 2.566 pts, σ 13.967) is fitted on 1999-2010 and frozen; origination is walk-forward from 2011 with a day-before-kickoff cutoff — no same-day or future information can enter any fair line, and every snapshot row records its state size (state_games_used). No market number enters the model anywhere.

Snapshot conventions

  • SPREAD SIGN: home-margin convention everywhere (positive = home favored) — verified empirically (sources page).
  • ORIGINATION ORDER: side → total → moneyline, enforced by market_seq.
  • ORIGINATION TIME: gameday − 1 day, recorded per row (model_captured_at).
  • Diff columns: diff_open_line = fair − open, diff_close_line = fair − close — separate columns, never merged.
  • Moneyline diff: fair probability − vig-included implied probability of the archived close (conservative).

Settlement & staking assumptions (all flagged)

  • Settlement uses the verified results feed only (32/32 reconciled).
  • Singles priced at assumed standard −110 (no official odds archive exists); 2-leg parlays at 2.646×; pushes void.
  • ML strategies are signal-only: no opening ML odds archive exists anywhere.
  • Entry price = opening number where archived (2013-2021), else the archived market line (labelled).

Verification protocol

  • Every table row on this site carries a source link (official where it exists; labelled third-party otherwise).
  • 2026 standings recomputed from the feed match the official capture 32/32, 0 mismatches.
  • Live spot checks 2026-11-14: official game-page URL pattern resolves; NFC East records 4/4 match.
  • Line conventions verified by outcome-based tests (7+ favorites' win rates; settlement agreement 99.8%).
  • Build-time verifier: every row must keep its source link or the build fails (engine/verify.py).
