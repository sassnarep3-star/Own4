What this site will not do

It does not print a return on investment, a bankroll, or a price that no official page publishes. No official NFL page carries a betting line, so every market column is blank, every price is our own, and the leaderboard is a proper-score leaderboard with an explicit statement that it cannot rank strategies for money. The staking stage is empty by rule, not by omission. See Leaderboard and Findings.
