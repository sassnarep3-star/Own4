Each strategy, in both directions

A one-sided change test is a marketing device: it lists only the evidence that would confirm its author. Every row above carries a promote line and a demote line, and the four controls are marked as controls so they can never be mistaken for candidates.

S1 Model favourite (singles, shadow) — what would change our mind

Definition. Bet the club the rating model makes the favourite. Decision uses the model's own probability only; no price input.

Registered: PREREGISTRATION.md §7

Change test. Right now: a lower bound of -0.0148 on Brier skill means the model has NOT been shown to beat a coin flip with confidence, while a model-free rule scores within 2.6 points of it. If 184 more graded games keep the lower bound below 0, this strategy is dead and should be withdrawn rather than tuned.

Source: official results

S2 Model favourite, high confidence (singles, shadow) — what would change our mind

Definition. S1 restricted to fixtures where the model's own probability is at least 0.65.

Registered: PREREGISTRATION.md §7 (threshold 0.65 frozen)

Change test. The 0.60-0.70 bin currently observes 41.7% home wins against 64.4% predicted — one bin, 12 games. If that inversion survives 50 more games, the 0.65 threshold is selecting the model's worst probabilities, not its best, and S2 must be withdrawn.

Source: official results

S3 Better-record rule (CONTROL, not a candidate) — what would change our mind

Definition. Pick the club with the better wins-minus-losses record computed from games before the cutoff. Zero model content.

Registered: PREREGISTRATION.md §7

Change test. This control is the reason the model's headline accuracy is not treated as evidence: they differ by 2.6 points with an interval of [-3.7, +8.5]. If this control ever beats the model on a larger sample, the ratings are not earning their complexity.

Source: official results

S4 Fade of S1 (registered falsification test) — what would change our mind

Definition. Bet the opposite of S1. If the model were noise, this would score about the same.

Registered: PREREGISTRATION.md §6.5

Change test. 32.2% accuracy when inverted, against 67.8% for the original, is the shape you want: the original is not symmetric noise.

Source: official results

C1 Coin flip (control baseline) — what would change our mind

Definition. A fair coin per fixture, 2 000 repeated runs.

Registered: PREREGISTRATION.md §7

Change test. Nothing: it is the null the whole page is measured against.

Source: official results

P1 Parlays, same-game parlays, prop combos — what would change our mind

Definition. Not defined on purpose: a combination cannot be ranked without a validated joint probability and the operator's void/push rules.

Registered: blocked by G6

Change test. CR-03 shows a same-game scoring dependence of r = 0.053 (n = 296, interval includes 0) — too weak to justify independence and too imprecise to justify dependence. Multiplying marginal probabilities stays forbidden until a joint number exists.

Why parlays, same-game parlays and prop combinations are listed but not defined

A combination has no respectable probability without a joint distribution over the exact legs, and the operator's void and push rules. Gate G6 requires both before a combination may be scored; CR-03 gives a scoring dependence that is neither distinguishable from zero nor usable, so the gate cannot open. Defining these strategies anyway would mean publishing a number with no derivation, which is the one thing this namespace refuses to do.
