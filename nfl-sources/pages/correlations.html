How to read CR-04, the one that matters

Rest difference correlates with home margin at r = 0.3727 over 132 fixtures, which is the strongest relationship in this set and the only one whose interval excludes zero. It is also fragile in a specific way: 83 of those fixtures have zero rest difference because both clubs played in the same week, so the coefficient is carried by the minority where the rest weeks actually differ. Among those the correlation rises to 0.5686 on 49 fixtures.

Leave-one-week-out drops one week of fixtures and recomputes; the coefficient stays inside [0.329, 0.426], so no single week carries it. The split by rest edge is the honest caveat: read alone, the 'home club rested more' cut says rest is worth about seven and a half points, and the 'away club rested more' cut says it is worth about minus four and a half. Rest is therefore reported and not shipped as a rating parameter.

What a correlation is not

None of these are causal, and none are corrected for multiplicity: nine were registered and five could be computed, so a p-value of 0.068 read as a discovery would be reading noise. The rest result is the exception only because its interval excludes zero and it survives leave-one-week-out.
