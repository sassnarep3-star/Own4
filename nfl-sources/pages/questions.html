How to find the missing information

  • For prices and closing lines (Q01, Q08): the only route is a licensed feed with a written right to redistribute. Ask the operator for a historical archive with timestamps, not a live screen; the residual columns on Week 11 fill in the day that archive arrives.
  • For depth, snap counts and inactives (Q03, Q09): NFL publishes snap counts on the game book PDFs, which are the official object the brief already names. The register needs one dated read per club per week, not a scrape of a depth chart.
  • For injury status history (Q06, Q12): a Wednesday/Thursday/Friday capture with a hash per capture. The first one is cheap; the value is in the second and third, because a level cannot be scored and a delta can.
  • For stadium roof and surface (Q10): the venue's own published stadium guide is the authority; the NFL game centre does not print it, so a fourth-party aggregation would be a citation we cannot defend.
  • For anything a peer namespace already holds (Q04, Q11): read it from that namespace rather than re-deriving it, and cite the namespace as the source.
