Removal of luck

The panel the brief asks for, built out of what actually exists.
  • Control baselines on the same run: coin flip 0.2500 Brier, home-rate prior 0.2609, record-only rule 0.6522 accuracy. A strategy is only interesting relative to these, never in absolute terms.
  • Fade test: inverting S1 scores 0.3217; inverting S2 scores 0.2951. A symmetric-noise strategy would score near 50% both ways.
  • Placebo label shuffle: p = 0.0005 (S1) and p = 0.0010 (S3).
  • Week-cluster intervals, not fixture-level ones: a fixture-level interval would be roughly half as wide and would wrongly exclude zero.
  • Break-even price: S1's own picks have a mean fair American price of -272.6, which implies a break-even hit rate of 73.2%; it delivered 67.8%.
  • What is still luck: across 115 fixtures the model finished 3 games ahead of the rule that ignores it — 25 fixtures decided the whole comparison. Gate G1 wants 300 graded games before a gap that small is allowed to count, so the verdict is "not separable", not "slightly better".
