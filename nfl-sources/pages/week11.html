Week 11: our own line, originated side → total → moneyline

13 fixtures. Side first, then total, then moneyline, in that order, twice — before and after week 10. The market columns are blank because no official page carries one.

Six columns per fixture are blank on purpose: no official NFL page publishes a market price, so the market line, the market total, the market moneyline and the three residuals do not exist. Instead of importing a number from a site we cannot cite, we publish our own line twice and the movement between the two.

Why two snapshots. open is the model fitted through week 9 — the line we would have posted before week 10 was played. update is the model fitted through week 10. The difference between the columns is the effect of one week of official results on our own opinion, before any opponent has published theirs. When a price source exists, the residual columns fill in and the same table becomes a market comparison without changing shape.
