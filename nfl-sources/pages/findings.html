The one-paragraph version

Our own ratings beat a coin flip by 12% on the Brier score over 116 graded fixtures, and beat the bookmaker's own home-field prior by 16%. That sounds like a result until the interval is computed on weeks rather than fixtures: it spans zero. A rule that only looks at win-loss records lands 2.6 accuracy points behind the model, well inside noise. The one relationship strong enough to use is rest, and we have not modelled it. Prices do not exist on any official page, so the entire money question is unanswerable by construction — and saying that plainly is the finding, not a gap in the work.
