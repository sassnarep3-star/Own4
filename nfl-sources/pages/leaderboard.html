Leaderboard

A wrong leaderboard is worse than none. Stage one ranks strategies by proper score only; stage two is disabled until a price exists.

Two stages, and only stage one exists. Stage one is a proper-score qualification: a strategy must clear G1 (300 graded fixtures) and G2 (a week-cluster lower bound above zero on Brier skill) before it is looked at for money. Stage two is a prospective escrow of closing-line value and return, which needs a published price and therefore cannot run at all. A leaderboard that shows a return before stage one is complete is ranking luck.

Why there is no ROI column anywhere on this site

A return requires a price. No official NFL page publishes one, and this namespace will not take a price from a site it cannot cite as authoritative. The arithmetic that would matter is published instead: at −110 a strategy needs 52.4% to break even, at −120 it needs 54.5%, and our own fair price for S1's picks averages -272.6, which needs 73.2%. S1's observed accuracy is 67.8%. Those four numbers are the entire money question that can be answered honestly today, and they answer it: not yet.
