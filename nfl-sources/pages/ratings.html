What these numbers are, exactly

Each club gets one attacking number and one defensive number, fitted by ridge regression on the official scores of every game before the cutoff, with a shared home-field term. The reported quantity is the net rating: offence above average plus defence above average, where a positive defensive number means the club concedes fewer points than average. The split between the two components is identified only by the penalty, so the level shift from that penalty is reported explicitly rather than hidden.

The sign trap, fixed and recorded. An earlier version of this table subtracted the defence term, which made a good defence look bad by about twice its true margin. Catching it took a targeted sign check rather than a visual read of the output. It is flagged in Flagged as FL-11 because a silent sign error in a published rating is exactly the kind of defect that survives review by looking plausible.
