Executive summary

The whole result in one page: what was measured, what it can support, and the four things it deliberately does not claim.

Bottom line. A simple, opponent-aware point rating does forecast NFL winners better than every control it was pre-registered against, and the result survives a deliberate attempt to destroy it. That is a forecast-skill result. It is not a betting result: the repository holds no price, so no money metric exists and no reward is awarded.

1 · What was measured, and how

Rule text, sample window, hyper-parameter grid, metrics and gates were committed and hash-locked before the numbers were computed (prereg/PREREG-CD.md, SHA-256 77de26eda552edb923cdbae8d090e57bdabfd56adbe8d5b8809b489d2aa0b4e2). The build refuses to run if that file changes. Protocol: expanding-window walk-forward, week 4 to week 10, each week forecast only from earlier weeks; λ chosen on weeks 4–7 and frozen; weeks 8–10 held out from every choice.

5 · Totals are a tie, and are published as a tie

Total MAE 10.776 against a training-window mean of 10.790: an improvement of 0.014 points with a week-cluster 95% interval of [-0.1144, +0.0891]. The interval straddles zero, so neither estimator wins. Both stay visible, as the brief asks.

6 · What the brief asked for that is not here, and why

  • ROI / CLV / staking. No official price source. Zero points awarded.
  • Parlay and prop-combo ranking. Needs a validated joint probability and the operator's void rules. Neither exists.
  • Injury ablations. Needs point-in-time availability over time; one week is held.
  • Depth from playing time. No snap-count source exists in the repository.

Each of those is a question with a named route to an answer on the Questions page, and a decision path on the Suggestions page.
