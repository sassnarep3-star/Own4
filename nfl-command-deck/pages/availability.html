Limits of this evidence — stated, not softened

  • One capture (2026-11-18T23:49:17Z). The Week 11 report is a series that runs Wednesday to kickoff; a single read cannot show a designation moving.
  • The report's game status may be blank mid-week even for a player later ruled out; a blank is published as blank, not as 'available'.
  • No inactive list and no snap count are held, so 'the backup replaced the starter' cannot be confirmed from these sources. The chart's next name is what the club publishes, not evidence of who played.
  • The depth snapshot (2026-11-17) and the report capture (2026-11-18) are one day apart; a roster move in that window would not appear in both.
