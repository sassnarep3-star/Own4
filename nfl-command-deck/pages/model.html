Why the holdout matters here

The holdout skill (+0.2397) is higher than the full-window skill (+0.1362), which is the opposite of what leakage or overfitting would produce, and is consistent with the model being a real, if modest, signal. It is also only 42 forecasts: the interval [+0.1107, +0.3274] is the honest statement, not the point estimate.

Correlations

The brief asks for correlations from officially sourced numbers with n. The only official numbers held are final scores; the correlations computable from them (margin vs total, home points vs away points) are already published with n=137 in nfl-book/data/correlations.json, and re-deriving them here would duplicate a peer number rather than add evidence. Parlay and prop-combo correlations require prices and player-level official lines, which do not exist in this repository. Nothing is estimated; the section is empty by rule.
