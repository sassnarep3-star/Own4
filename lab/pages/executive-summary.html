Executive summary

As of 2026-11-02 18:09 UTC. Every figure is computed by model/evaluate.py and shown with its source.

What this project did

  • Transcribed every official 2026-27 final to date (92 games, Oct 20 – Nov 1) and the official 2025-26 and 2026-27 standings and team tables from nba.com, with a source link on every row. 11 of 11 consistency checks pass (game-level sums reproduce the official team table for all 30 teams on games, wins, losses and +/-).
  • Built an own-line model (BLEND-41): team rating = current-season margin shrunk toward last season's with weight k=41 (declared before testing), plus a home-court term of +1.73 points from 2025-26 home games. It produces a spread, total, win probability and fair price for each game — not copied from any sportsbook.
  • Pre-registered seven validation gates and a strategy catalogue before running any evaluation, then scored every strategy walk-forward (a game is predicted only from strictly earlier games).

Leaderboard verdict

No strategy is validated and no reward is paid. Pre-registered requirement: ≥300 out-of-sample games (we have 92) and a bootstrap CI above zero (none). The best-scoring entries are Provisional (point estimate beats baseline; monitored, reward 0). Player-prop strategies and market-edge singles are Blocked: no official bulk player logs and no official odds source were available, so nothing was inferred or filled in. Parlay: the same-game correlation haircut is tiny (rho ≈ -0.07, CI about ±0.2) and does not improve Brier score.

How luck was removed

  • Walk-forward only (no look-ahead; asserted in code); parameters declared a priori; nested check tunes k on early games and tests on later ones (chosen k=10: test MAE 12.77 vs 12.98 for k=41 — immaterial).
  • Proper scoring rules (MAE, Brier), paired bootstrap CIs with Bonferroni correction, minimum-sample gate, half-sample stability gate. ROI and win/loss records are not used because they are dominated by luck at this sample size and no official odds exist.
  • Rewards go only to strategies that clear sample-size and significance gates; otherwise the 1,000 virtual units stay unallocated.
