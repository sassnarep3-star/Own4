NFL line origination — what we can verify, and what we cannot

Own lines for the 13 unplayed Week 10 games, ten pre-registered validation gates, measured correlations for singles, parlays and prop combos, injury and depth tracking, and an honest scoreboard. Snapshot 2026-11-13: every number below was read from an official page on that date or computed from published code.

Headline: nothing here is an edge

Our rating model beats a naive baseline and loses to the closing line (10.913 vs 9.848 margin MAE). No strategy survives Benjamini–Hochberg control, no strategy has a confidence interval above zero, and the pre-registered placebos behave exactly as they should — they lose. 0 of 1,000 reward points are awarded. The NFL publishes no odds, so no price, stake, edge or return is claimed anywhere on this site.

What this release is, and is not

Verified against an official source

32 standings rows, the 14-game Week 10 slate with kick-off times, networks and byes, the one completed Week 10 result (WAS 24–21 NYG), 95 injury designations and 8 lineup changes named in official NFL.com reporting. All 160 recomputed 2026 record cells match nfl.com.

Not verified — published as blank or blocked

Any betting price or return, any depth-chart string, any per-game player log, any historical injury archive, the archive's licence terms, and the date within the week at which the third-party line was captured. Each is listed on the Flagged for review page with its source rather than filled in.
