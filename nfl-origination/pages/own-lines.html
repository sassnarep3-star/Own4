How to read the injury band

The official injury report gives a game status — OUT, DOUBTFUL or QUESTIONABLE — and nothing else. It does not say whether the player is a starter, because no official depth chart exists. The band therefore applies a disclosed assumption at three multipliers (×0.5, ×1.0, ×2.0) and shows the range. The published line is the no-adjustment line. A wide band is an honest statement that the injury input cannot support precision — see flag F-05.

Method

  • Rating: shrunk points-scored-per-game minus shrunk points-allowed-per-game, with 8 games of shrinkage toward the league mean, so a 2-game sample cannot dominate.
  • Home field: 2.063 points, measured on 1359 prior-season games (prior seasons only, never the season being predicted).
  • Fitted on: every completed regular-season game before every completed REG game before 2026-11-12 (7103 games).
  • Win probability: normal CDF of expected margin over a sigma of 13.5, derived from the closing line's measured margin MAE rather than assumed.
  • Total: the sum of the two clubs' pace estimates (offence plus defence over two). It is published because it is ours, but it carries no usable signal yet — see suggestion S20.
