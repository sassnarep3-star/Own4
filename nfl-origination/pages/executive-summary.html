Executive summary

Snapshot 2026-11-13. What was verified, what could not be, what was measured, and what has to be decided next.

The bottom line in one paragraph

The data is sound and the scoreboard is empty. All 160 recomputed 2026 record cells match nfl.com, all 10 integrity checks pass, and the Week 10 slate, its one completed result and 95 injury designations were transcribed from official pages on 2026-11-13. But our own rating model, which does beat a naive baseline (MAE 10.913 vs 11.067), loses to the closing line (9.848) over 2126 out-of-sample games. 0 of 7 registered strategies qualify, both pre-registered placebos fail as they must, and 0 of 1,000 reward points are awarded. No price, stake, edge or return is claimed anywhere, because the NFL publishes no odds at all.

Three things the brief asks for that cannot be sourced

  1. 1st/2nd/3rd/4th-string depth labels. The league publishes no depth chart; nfl.com/depth-charts/ is a 404, re-checked on 2026-11-13. Labelling a player "3rd string" would be invention. Only the 8 lineup changes an official article explicitly names are published, and everything else is marked blocked.
  2. A real betting edge. With no official price, Gate G7 can never pass. Every return figure here rests on an assumed −110 and is labelled as such.
  3. Historical injury backtests. nfl.com/injuries/ serves the current week only. No injury effect is measurable, so none is claimed.

The one real signal

A quarterback's passing-yardage over and his WR1's receiving-yardage over, in the same team-game, co-occur 1.45× more often than independence predicts: phi = 0.346, n = 803, 95% CI 0.284–0.406. That is genuine dependence in the data, and it means an independent-parlay price would underprice the combo — if the book did not reprice it. It does. No archived same-game-parlay price exists here, so the dependence is a measured fact, not a claimed edge. Buying that price history is the single most promising spend in the project (suggestion S01/S06).

Cross-game parlay legs, by contrast, really are independent: the first half of a week's home-cover rate has r = −0.007 against the second half (473 week-slates), and spread vs total within a game has r = −0.010 (7,103 games). Those may be multiplied safely.

What to decide next

The Suggestions & decisions page holds 22 items. Four are binding:

  • S07 — adopt a written skill definition before any reward is paid. The one proposed here rejects our own best result, which is the point.
  • S02 — obtain a legal position before publishing anything as more than research points.
  • S01 — buy or license an archived odds history. This is the constraint on everything else in the betting half of the project.
  • S10 — choose one canonical NFL dashboard; twelve now compete.

Method and honesty controls

  • Model knobs frozen in data/model_params.json with a timestamp written before the holdout was read; the holdout (2018–2025) scored exactly once.
  • Walk-forward: the model is refitted before every kick-off on strictly earlier games, and the home-field term uses prior seasons only.
  • Week-clustered bootstrap CI on every ROI, so correlated same-week results cannot inflate significance; a 2,000-draw matched-permutation null; Benjamini–Hochberg FDR across every test including placebos; and season-concentration disclosure for every profitable strategy.
  • Two pre-registered placebos that must fail. They do. During this session a placebo passed, which exposed a sign-convention bug in the line column; the bug was fixed, the episode is published as flag F-08, and it is why gate G6 exists.
