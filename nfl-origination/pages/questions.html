Q08What sample size is needed before a 55% hit rate is distinguishable from luck? known blocks: Nothing — answered. It is the reason G2 exists.
Answer
At -110 the break-even hit rate is 110/210 = 52.38%. Detecting 55% against that null needs roughly n = 1,700 bets for 80% power at alpha 0.05. Every strategy tested here falls short except the two benchmarks.
Known
The arithmetic is reproducible: z = (0.55-0.5238)/sqrt(0.5238*0.4762/n). ↗ stats/

Method

Q18Is the archive's line column a price or an expectation? partial blocks: Any precise claim about closing-line value.
Answer
An expectation: spread_line is the expected home margin, positive when the home team is favoured. Measured, not assumed: reading it that way gives MAE 9.83 and r = +0.43 against actual margin; the opposite reading gives MAE 14.31 and r = -0.43, which no real market line can produce.
Known
The check (IC-04) compares both readings explicitly and fails the build if the wrong one wins. ↗ github.com/nflverse/nfldata
Not known
Whether the archive's line is the CLOSE or a snapshot earlier in the week.
Why it blocks
Any precise claim about closing-line value.
