Correlations

A parlay's fair price needs the joint distribution, not the product of its legs. So the dependence has to be measured, not assumed — and where it cannot be measured, left blank.

Method

Pearson correlation for continuous pairs, phi for binary prop legs, 95% CI via Fisher z, and Benjamini–Hochberg control across every test at once. A blank r is never an estimated value: it means the input cannot be obtained from an official source, and the reason is stated in the row below the table.

What each result means

C1 — Closing spread vs closing total, same game

Near zero, so spread and total angles on the same game are close to independent and may be combined; that is a dependence fact, not an edge.

C2 — Home cover vs over, same game (2-leg SGP)

Near zero over the whole archive, but this pool mixes every game. The player-prop pairs (C5) are the ones that show real dependence.

C3 — Team points-for vs points-against, same season

Weakly negative: a club that scores more tends to concede slightly less, which is the opposite of the naive 'good offence means bad defence' story. Used to justify rating offence and defence separately.

C4 — Rest differential vs home margin

Statistically detectable (n=7,103) but economically tiny: r=0.035 explains about 0.1%% of margin variance. This is why rest is NOT given its own weight in the model.

C5 — QB passing yards over proxy line vs WR1 receiving yards over proxy line

Real positive dependence: the legs hit together more often than independence predicts, so an SGP priced as if the legs were independent would underprice this combo. Whether that survives the book's repricing is C8, which is blank.

C6 — Prop legs within the same team-game (second pair)

Blank. The archived prop file exposes one QB/WR pair only; no second pair can be computed without new data.

C7 — Home-cover rate in the first half of a week vs the second half

Near zero, which supports treating legs from different games as independent when multiplying them.

C8 — Same-game parlay legs vs the sportsbook's repriced SGP price

Blank. No archived same-game-parlay price exists anywhere in this repository, and the NFL publishes no prices at all. The correlation (C5) is measured; the price it must be compared against is not obtainable.

C9 — Injury designation vs next-game scoring margin

Blank. nfl.com/injuries/ serves only the current week and publishes no historical archive, so an injury effect cannot be estimated at all.

Trading rules this implies

  • Cross-game legs may be multiplied. The dependence between games is indistinguishable from zero (C1, C2, C7).
  • Same-game player-prop legs may not. QB passing yards and WR1 receiving yards are positively dependent (C5), so multiplying them understates the joint probability by about 45%.
  • That is not an edge. Books reprice correlated same-game legs, and the repriced price (C8) is not obtainable here. The dependence is a measurement; the edge would be a comparison against a price, and there is no price.
