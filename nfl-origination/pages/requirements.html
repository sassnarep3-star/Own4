Requirements checklist

Every line of the brief, mapped to what exists on this site and what does not. A requirement is only marked MET when the artefact exists and its evidence is published here. Nothing is marked MET because it was attempted.

How to read the status column

MET the artefact exists and is evidenced on a page linked in the row. PARTIAL something real exists but a named gap remains. BLOCKED the requirement cannot be met without a source that does not exist — for example the depth-chart requirement, because the league publishes no depth chart.
