Read this one first

F-08 is a mistake this project made and fixed. An earlier version of the pipeline read the archive's line column backwards, which made the model appear to beat the closing line by 3.4 points of MAE and produced a strategy with +47% ROI. What exposed it was a pre-registered placebo passing — a strategy that cannot possibly work showing a "profit". The convention was then measured against both readings and the bug fixed. It is recorded here rather than deleted, because it is the clearest evidence on this site that the gates work.
