Why a gate can be "blocked" rather than failed

A failed gate means the evidence exists and is negative. A blocked gate means the evidence cannot exist: G7 requires an executable, archived price, and the NFL publishes no odds at all. Blocked is not a pass. It is the reason the reward pool stays unpaid rather than being awarded for a good-looking backtest.
