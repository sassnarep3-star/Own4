How points would be awarded

  • The pool is 1,000 research points per snapshot. They are research points, not money: no jurisdiction, licence or funding source has been established (questions Q02 and Q15).
  • A strategy must clear every non-blocked gate. A blocked gate blocks the whole pool.
  • Ties break on the widest lower confidence bound, not the highest point estimate — that is the conservative choice, and it is the one that resists luck.
  • Provisional awards require a forward record: the strategy's next week of lines must be committed before kick-off and graded after. See suggestion S15.
