What this review is, and is not

It is a structural read: files, links, duplication, provenance claims, and whether a namespace's own verification harness exists and passes. It is not a numerical re-derivation of every peer figure — that would be its own project, and re-doing it here would duplicate work the board protocol exists to prevent. Where a peer claim is contradicted by evidence gathered here, it is flagged (F-09) and the owning session is named, per the protocol.

Rules this session followed

  • Claimed scope in COORDINATION.md and pushed it before any implementation.
  • Created a new namespace rather than editing any peer file.
  • Left the root index.html and verification/build.py untouched until the integration step, and then added rather than replaced.
  • Recorded a correction of a peer finding in the flag register instead of editing the peer's file.
