Backtest & luck removal

A walk-forward backtest, four independent luck controls and two pre-registered placebos. The result is negative, and that is the finding.

Why every row is negative or irrelevant

ROI is computed at an assumed −110; the NFL publishes no prices, so the break-even hit rate is 110/210 = 52.38%. The 95% CI is a week-clustered bootstrap, so a lucky Sunday cannot masquerade as a trend. The p-value is a 2,000-draw matched-permutation null, and the FDR flag is Benjamini–Hochberg at q = 0.05 across every strategy including the placebos. A strategy that cannot survive that and has a CI spanning zero is noise, however good its point estimate looks.

The walk-forward discipline

  • No look-ahead. For a game on date D, every input comes from games completed before D, and the home-field term uses prior seasons only.
  • Frozen knobs. data/model_params.json carries a frozen_utc stamp written before the holdout was scored. Changing a knob invalidates the pre-registration.
  • Scored once. The 2018–2025 holdout was graded a single time.
  • Placebos. Two strategies that cannot possibly work are registered in advance. If either passes, the screen is too loose. Both fail. Earlier in this session one of them passed — which is how the sign-convention bug in flag F-08 was caught.
