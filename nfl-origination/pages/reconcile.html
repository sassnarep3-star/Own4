Why a third-party archive is involved at all

The NFL publishes no downloadable game log. The archive at nflverse/nfldata ↗ mirrors official box scores and is used only as a third-party input, labelled as third-party wherever it appears. Its 2026 slice plus the one officially-read Thursday result reproduces the official standings on every cell, which is the check that licenses its use here. Its licence terms were not reviewed — flag F-07.
