Why the official venue column is empty

Requirement R-23 asks for the venue of every fixture transcribed from the official game centre. That requires one fetch per fixture: 150 for the played season and 13 for Week 11. It is mechanical work, not analysis, and it is suggestion SG-07. Until it is approved, 163 of 163 venues are blank rather than guessed. A third-party source that already holds them is shown on the third-party candidate page.
