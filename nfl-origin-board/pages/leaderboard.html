Leaderboard

Four divisions, two controls, zero rows - because zero strategies have a graded sample.

The leaderboard is empty, and that is its correct state. Ranking needs 300 prospective graded games; the site has zero prospective graded games. An empty board publishes no number that could be mistaken for a track record.

What is enforced regardless. Ranking input = benchmark-relative Brier skill score with a game-cluster bootstrap lower bound and a Bonferroni multiplicity adjustment for the number of trials. Win rate and realised ROI are never ranking inputs (rejections R-38, R-65). Rewards are payable only from the prospective escrow sample, never from a backtest (R-09, R-59).
