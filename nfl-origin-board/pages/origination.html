Origination & residual

Side, then total, then moneyline - with the residual columns waiting on a price source.

R-03 fixes the origination order: the side first, then the total, then the moneyline. R-04 asks for three columns — our fair line, the market line, and the difference. R-18 asks for the residual to be published on a schedule. The order is frozen and published; the columns exist and are empty, because there is no market line to difference against.

No market number appears anywhere on this site. nfl.com publishes schedule, scores, standings and injuries. It does not publish betting prices. Until a price source is chosen under a read licence (suggestion SG-01), the market line, the residual, closing line value, the vig-free decomposition and ROI are all blank. They are not estimated, back-filled or proxied.

What a price policy must settle first (R-20)

  • Which vendor, and under which licence.
  • Whether raw prices may be stored, or only derived residuals.
  • What counts as the closing price, and at what timestamp.
  • How vig is removed, and whose consensus is the benchmark.

The repository already contains a candidate historical price source, shown unverified on the third-party page. It covers 150 of 150 fixtures in this season with a spread, a total and a moneyline. Deciding whether to accept it is the single highest-leverage open choice.
