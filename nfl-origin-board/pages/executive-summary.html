Executive summary

The state of the NFL line-origination programme as of the 2026-11-16 official capture.

What this session established

  • A verified results corpus. 150 fixtures of the 2026 season were read from the official schedule pages, week by week. 148 have official final scores. Recomputed into per-club records, they match the official standings page exactly: 0 mismatches across all 32 clubs on W, L, T, points for and points against.
  • The 136-game corpus is now real. Weeks 1-9 contain exactly 136 games. They were cross-checked against the nflverse archive already committed in this repository: 136 of 136 games have identical scores, 0 discrepancies.
  • A current availability capture. The official injury report was read in full on 2026-11-16: 347 rows across 28 clubs, 171 of them carrying a game status (70 Out, 90 Questionable, 11 Doubtful). The 4 clubs missing are the 4 clubs on bye in Week 10.
  • Two defects from earlier in the project are closed. The three Lions game-status designations that were blank (Kerby Joseph, Cade Mays, D.J. Reed) are present in this capture as Out; and the Week 10 injury tables have been re-captured at current counts.
  • Twenty-two announced-starter and roster events are recorded from dated official nfl.com articles, including Drew Lock, Kyle McCord and the Lamar Jackson injured-reserve sequence. No replacement is inferred anywhere: where the league has not named a starter, the column is blank.
  • Depth evidence is now bounded rather than misreported. The Nov. 17 sweep found club pages for 32/32 teams (31 HTML tables; Houston image-only), but only 5/32 charts print an as-of date. This establishes page-route coverage only, not actual starters or playing time; those fields stay blank until official Game Books and snap counts are joined.

The one decision that matters most

The repository already contains the data that would clear three gates at once. nfl-lines/data/raw/nfldata_games.csv is the nflverse games archive: 7,548 games from 1999 to 2026. It holds a venue for 150 of 150 of this session's fixtures, a roof for 146, and spread, total and moneyline for 150. It is not an official source, so nothing from it has been merged into the official tables here — but its 2026 scores agree with the official pages on every one of the 136 games both sources cover. Accepting it as a flagged third-party source would clear the venue, roof/surface and historical-price gates immediately. That is suggestion SG-01 and flag RF-15.

What is deliberately not published

  • No market line, no residual, no closing line value, no ROI. No licensed or official price feed is connected, and the brief requires the price policy to be decided before any backtest is published.
  • The 2026-11-17 sweep found club depth pages for 32/32 teams (31 HTML tables; HOU image-only), but only 5/32 charts print an as-of date. These are not verified game-day starters or snap shares; no official snap-count join is connected, so no replacement-deficiency value is published.
  • No leaderboard rows. Ranking needs 300 prospective graded games; the site has zero, and an empty leaderboard is the honest state.
  • No parlay or same-game-parlay price anywhere on the site. Multiplying marginals is invalid under dependence and the joint-evidence gate is closed.
  • No value filled in from judgement. Where an official source does not state it, the cell is blank and carries the gate that blocks it.
