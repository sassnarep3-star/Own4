How to read the split-half row (C-4). It correlates each club's net points per game in Weeks 1-5 with the same club's net points per game in Weeks 6-10, across 32 clubs with a chronological split and no shuffling. A low value means one half of a season tells you little about the next, which is exactly the quantity any strategy must beat rather than assume.

What is missing

  • Correlations involving price, closing line value or ROI: blocked by G-PRICE, and none is estimated.
  • Correlations involving availability or depth: blocked by G-DEPTH and G-INACTIVE.
  • Strategy-level luck separation: blocked by G-SAMPLE at n ≥ 300 graded games.
