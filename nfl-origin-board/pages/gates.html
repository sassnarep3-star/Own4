Gates & pre-registration

What a strategy must pass, what sends it back to paper, and what was frozen before it saw data.

Pre-registration is the anti-luck mechanism that costs nothing. A strategy is registered by committing its frozen definition before it sees the evaluation window; the registration is the commit. Backtest numbers may never appear on a leaderboard.

Ranking and demotion rules

  • Ranking input = benchmark-relative Brier skill score with a game-cluster bootstrap lower bound and a Bonferroni multiplicity adjustment for the number of trials. Win rate and realised ROI are never ranking inputs (rejections R-38, R-65).
  • Rewards are payable only from the prospective escrow sample, never from a backtest (R-09, R-59).
  • Demotion: a live strategy returns to paper when its game-cluster lower bound on benchmark-relative Brier skill falls below zero over a rolling window, or when it fails a control baseline on the same sample (R-08).
  • Registration: the commit hash of the commit that freezes the definition IS the registration; the definition is frozen before the strategy sees the evaluation window.
  • Drift alarm: row counts and required fields are pinned; a missing field fails the build rather than publishing a blank (R-63).
  • Registration: Published after the merge commit exists; the governance page prints the hash emitted by verify.py at build time.
