Do-not-do list (all unlawful here or high-risk)

  • Rent increase above allowable without a certified Board petition — void and tenant-recoverable. SF.gov — petitions
  • Increase served while the rent-increase license is suspended/expired (Housing Inventory non-compliance). SF.gov — Housing Inventory FAQ
  • Any increase within 180 days after a tenant’s tenantability complaint — retaliation per Civ. Code §1942.5. Civ. Code §1942.5
  • Surcharge because a roommate (e.g., a new occupant) moved in — expressly unlawful. SF.gov — rent increases
  • “Market adjustment” dressed as a capital improvement without petition and documentation.
  • Compounding banked percentages, prorating, or rounding up — prohibited by SF rules. SF.gov — banked increases
  • Serving Unit 2 with a 13.1% notice before the counselor resolves the 12-vs-24-month banking rule (flag F1) — or with less than 90 days’ notice because “it’s only $412”.
  • Any selection of which unit gets an increase based on who lives there — see discrimination.html.
