Rules & fairness

One unit or both?

There is no rule here requiring both units to receive an increase at the same time. A consistent annual-only percentage for eligible units is a cautious policy choice, not a safe harbor. Different timing, base rents, lease terms or a documented decision to defer can justify different outcomes, but must be assessed individually. Do not rely on protected characteristics, complaints or other retaliatory reasons; a neutral policy may still merit legal review for discriminatory effects.

Privately document the eligibility and objective basis for each decision. Do not publish tenant records. A local attorney should review the full facts before service. California Civil Rights Department · housing ↗ · HUD · Fair Housing Act ↗
