03 / IMPLEMENTATION

Notice & resources

A checklist and sample language for review—not a completed or legally sufficient notice.

Pre-notice checklist

StepWhat to confirmOfficial source
1 · Verify eligibilityPrivately check coverage and exemptions, complete lease and amendments, current lawful base rent, prior notices and dates, and whether a fixed-term agreement permits the change.SF Rent Board · rental laws ↗ · SF Rent Board · rent increases ↗
2 · LicenseComplete Housing Inventory reporting and confirm a current rent increase license is on file before the proposed annual or banked increase takes effect.SF Rent Board · housing inventory ↗
3 · Select a dateAt least 12 months since the last annual increase or tenancy start as applicable; use the rate in effect on the actual effective date. No retroactive increase is proposed.SF Rent Board · rent increases ↗
4 · Written noticeThe city describes 30 days for a cumulative increase of 10% or less in the preceding year, 90 days for more than 10%, plus 5 days if mailed. Verify service and exact timing with counsel.SF Rent Board · rent increases ↗ · SF Rent Board · banking ↗
5 · ContentState the dollar and percentage increase and effective date. Identify the annual and any banked portions; do not add banked amounts without substantiation. Confirm any other statutory, lease, subsidy or service requirements with counsel.SF Rent Board · notice rules ↗ · SF Rent Board · banking ↗
6 · Keep recordsRetain the calculation, applicable rate, license, service evidence and neutral decision basis privately. Make no claim that legal counsel or tenants have approved the increase.SF Rent Board · housing inventory ↗ · California Civil Rights Department · housing ↗

DRAFT FOR COUNSEL ONLY

Plain-language notice outline

Dear [tenant placeholder],

Subject to a valid written notice and applicable law, the proposed annual rent adjustment is [percentage], or [monthly dollar amount], changing the monthly base rent from [current lawful base rent] to [new lawful base rent], effective [verified date]. The annual portion is [annual amount]; the banked portion, if any, is [verified amount or none].

This adjustment is proposed under the San Francisco Rent Ordinance. Please contact [property manager placeholder] with questions. You may contact the San Francisco Rent Board or seek independent tenant advice.

Not for service as written. Names, addresses, delivery method, effective date, legal notices, and eligibility must be checked privately by counsel.
