Player props and prop combos

Twelve originated prop lines anchored on official season averages, and the measured dependence that reprices every two-leg combo.

The anchor is official; the volatility is not. Each player's mean is copied from the official game card. The probability around it uses a pooled within-player residual standard deviation from partner-sourced game logs, because the four players on tonight's slate have only 4-6 logged games each (flags F05, F06, F07).

Why 0.26 matters. Points and rebounds correlate at r = 0.268 on 1,951 within-player residuals (95% CI 0.226 to 0.309, BH q ≈ 0). That is the single most useful number on this page for pricing: a points-plus-rebounds combo for a heavy-minutes starter is systematically more likely than the product of its legs, by roughly 0.03 to 0.05 in absolute probability.
What would change these numbers, and what would not. Player-specific variance, minutes projections and opponent defensive context would all change them. A different book's line would not: these are model outputs, not quotes, and no official market price is available to compare against.

Formulas

line      = round(mean * 2) / 2                      mean = official season average
P(over)   = 1 - Phi((line - mean) / sd)               sd = pooled within-player residual sd
P(A and B)= bivariate_normal(z_a, z_b; rho)           z = probit(1 - P(over))

Combo dependence uses the pooled within-player correlation for the pair, not a player-specific estimate, because per-player samples are too small. Flagged as F05/F07.
