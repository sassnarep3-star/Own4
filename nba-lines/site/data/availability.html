How availability enters the model

The ratings on the singles page are fitted on completed games and are not adjusted for tonight's availability. The count of players listed Out per team is shown beside each line as context, and the adjustment is left undone rather than guessed. That is why the lines carry a note instead of a silent bump: an un-modelled injury is a known limitation, an invented adjustment is a fabricated number.
