The four that matter most

F01 — no prices, therefore no edge

Every line on this site is a model output. A model output becomes an edge only when compared with a price somebody will accept the other side of. There is no official price archive and no licensed feed, so no expected value, no ROI and no closure-line value is claimed anywhere. This is the single largest limitation of the project and no amount of modelling removes it.

F02 — 96 games

Ninety-six graded predictions cannot distinguish a skill of 0.05 from zero with any confidence. The permutation p-value shows the observed skill is unlikely under pure chance, but the bootstrap interval is wide enough that the honest headline is 'information exists', not 'edge exists'.

F05/F07 — prop volatility is borrowed

The prop probabilities use a residual standard deviation pooled across 1,951 player-games rather than estimated per player, and those games come from a partner source. Both facts are stated on the props page and here, because a prop line without its volatility is a number without a meaning.

F18 — no funded rewards

Reward points are a scoring convention, not a payout. Nothing is funded and nobody is eligible. The policy is published so that when gates do pass, the reward is not renegotiated after the fact.
