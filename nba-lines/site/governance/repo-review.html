What was checked, method by method

  • Every datasource claim that a file says it used was checked against the file's own manifest, including HTTP status codes.
  • Every published coefficient was traced to its input data. Where the input was absent, that is finding R02, not an assumption that it was fine.
  • Link integrity of the inherited site was re-run, not assumed: 758 links, 0 problems (R13). This is recorded as a positive control.
  • Conflicting official claims were resolved by reading additional official pages rather than picking one. The roster oddity (R12) looked like an error and was not.
  • Workflow automation was read for branch pins and targets, which is how R06 was found.

What this review did not do

It did not re-fetch every third-party page the earlier sessions cited, and it does not certify the 47 MB partner extract's integrity beyond noting its provenance and its licensing tension. Those limits are recorded here rather than implied away.
