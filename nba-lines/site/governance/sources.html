What was read, and what that does and does not prove

  • Read in full during this session: the standings table, three game-date pages, the slate page with its season-leader cards, the injury index and the 4:00 PM ET PDF, the Stats FAQ, the stat glossary heading structure, and the problem-gambling help page.
  • Inherited and re-verified in aggregate: the 92 finals for 2026-10-20 to 2026-11-01. They were not re-read page by page; instead they were reconciled against the official standings and matched 30/30.
  • Inherited and not re-verified: the 2025-26 team table (internally consistent), and the 47 MB partner box-score extract (flagged R05, used only for volatility and correlation).
  • Not verifiable: anything requiring a market price, an official lineup feed, or a downloadable league dataset. Those are blank and flagged, not approximated.

Why reconciliation, not transcription, is the headline check

A hand-transcribed number is only as good as the transcriber. This repository has eight workspaces' worth of hand transcription and no way for a reader to audit it. Aggregating 121 finals into 30 win-loss records and diffing them against the league's own table turns that into a check anyone can re-run: if one score were wrong, the W-L record would not match. The check passed exactly, which is stronger evidence than any individual citation.
