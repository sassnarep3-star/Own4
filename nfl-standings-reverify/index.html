What was verified

The official NFL standings page was read on 2026-11-19 through the session's page-read tool (the sandbox has no direct HTTPS egress to nfl.com). The 32 club rows were parsed independently and the league identities re-derived: 300 team-games, so 150 league games played; wins equal losses (149 = 149); points for equal points against (6923 = 6923), so the league point total reconciles.

The stored comparison target is nfl-desk/data/standings_2026-11-19.json, a read of the same official page on the same day. The two parsers share no code; a disagreement on any of W, L, T, PCT, PF, PA or NET would be published, never overwritten.
