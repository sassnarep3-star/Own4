Methodological finding — the tie-count PCT rule

The identity check first tested PCT against W / (W + L + T) and flagged two clubs: NYG (5-3-1, published PCT 0.611) and WAS (3-5-1, published PCT 0.389). Under W/(W+L+T) those compute to 0.556 and 0.333, which do not match the published values.

This is not a data error. The NFL counts a tie as half a win and half a loss, so the published PCT is (W + 0.5·T) / (W + L + T): NYG = 5.5/9 = 0.611, WAS = 3.5/9 = 0.389, both matching the official page. The check was corrected to use the NFL's rule, and both clubs then pass. The finding is recorded here so a reader who re-runs the check with the naive formula sees why two rows appeared and why they are not errors.

Source: nfl.com/standings (NYG and WAS rows, 2026-11-19 read).
