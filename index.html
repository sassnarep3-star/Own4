THE OPERATING MODEL

From signal to qualified edge

See validation gates →
01

Build fair lines

Estimate outcomes from time-stamped, pregame information; calibrate probabilities and maintain separate market and model prices.

02

Measure residual signal

Test what remains after the market baseline, lineup status, schedule, opponent, and player role are accounted for.

03

Validate out of sample

Use chronological holdouts, uncertainty intervals, multiple-testing controls, and transparent benchmark comparisons.

04

Rank by evidence

Leaderboard eligibility requires every gate. Rank on robust net performance with uncertainty—not raw win counts.
