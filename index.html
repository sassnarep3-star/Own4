NBA line origination — verified research dashboard

Collect official NBA data, originate our own lines, develop signals that survive pre-registered gates, remove luck from the comparison, and rank only strategies that are selectively skill based. Snapshot 2026-11-05: everything below was re-read from nba.com on that date, and nothing is inferred.

Headline result — no edge is claimed. On the verified sample the originated winner picks do not beat the free home-team baseline (0.5714 vs 0.6000, one-sided p = 0.7003), the one nominal totals result does not survive multiple-testing control, and no strategy clears the ten gates. The competition therefore awards nothing. The value of this release is the verified data, the measurement machinery and the honest scoreboard, not a pick.

What is verified, and what is not

Verified

30 standings rows, 30 team-stat rows and 2 scheduled games re-read from nba.com on 2026-11-05; 29 final scores re-read from the official games pages; 56 inherited final scores corroborated date-by-date and league-wide; all 13 reconciliation checks pass or are explicitly noted.

Not verified — published as blank or blocked

Betting prices (no official source), player per-game logs (not available under the official-source rule), player availability, prop variances and prop-pair correlations, and the 36 games on the seven October dates that were never captured. These are listed on the Flagged for review page rather than filled in.
