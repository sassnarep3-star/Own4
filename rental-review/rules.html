LAW & TIMING

The rules that shape an increase

The ordinary annual allowance is a ceiling for an eligible increase, not a market recommendation. Apply the rules in force on the proposed effective date.

TopicRule or analysisSource
CoverageA pre-June 14, 1979 unit may be covered, but age alone does not establish coverage; some single-family homes, condos and other categories are exempt. The stated 1940 construction is not independently verified.Official source ↗
Annual allowance1.6% applies March 1, 2026–February 28, 2027; 1.4% applied March 1, 2025–February 28, 2026. Later rates are not assumed.Official source ↗
TimingFirst annual increase may be imposed 12 months after lease start; after an increase wait at least 12 months. Off-anniversary effective dates can change the anniversary and banking outcome.Official source ↗
Base rentApply the rate to lawful base rent, not temporary passthroughs or fluctuating charges. No rounding up to the nearest dollar.Official source ↗
LicenseThe owner must have a rent increase license before imposing annual and/or banked increases; Housing Inventory reporting is a prerequisite. Actual license status is unknown.Official source ↗
Banked increasesA skipped increase is banked only after 24 full months without an increase; properly banked rates are added, not compounded or prorated. Partial increases and irregular dates have special rules.Official source ↗
State capThe Rent Board says the separate 8.8% AB 1482 cap for August 1, 2026–July 31, 2027 does not apply to units under SF rent increase limitations. It does not replace stronger local protections.Official source ↗
Federal protectionsThe Fair Housing Act addresses discriminatory housing transactions and terms on protected grounds. Applicability and exemptions depend on facts; federal housing-program restrictions may also matter, but are not established here.Official source ↗
RoommatesExtra rent cannot be charged simply for a new roommate; limited Section 6.14 / Costa-Hawkins scenarios involve the last original occupant leaving and other specific prerequisites.Official source ↗
Cost recoveryOperating and maintenance expenses, capital improvements and other passthroughs have separate rules; certain petitions must be filed before notice. Costs alone do not authorize an extra charge.Official source ↗
