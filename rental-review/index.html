OVERVIEW

A clearer view of rent increases

A careful, tenant-conscious guide to two reported rent-controlled units in San Francisco’s Outer Sunset. Use this as a review framework—not authorization to increase rent.

Bottom line

The City’s ordinary annual allowance is 1.6% for effective dates March 1, 2026–February 28, 2027. It is not a guaranteed increase for either unit. License, lawful base rent, timing, lease terms, tenancy history, service and fair housing must be checked first. Rent Board announcement ↗

Executive summary

Applying the same neutral policy to both units is a practical way to assess consistency, but neither matching increases nor increasing only one unit proves or disproves discrimination. Consider zero or a modest annual-only increase, without banking; lower than the maximum is allowed. This is a risk-conscious recommendation, not verified evidence of a typical market practice or a guarantee of legal compliance.
