NFL Command Center

One page for the whole NFL brief: what is verified against an official league page, what is blocked and by which gate, and the exact number that would clear each gate. Values are captured on a dated clock and reproduced by a stdlib-only build, so every number on this site can be recomputed from the repository without network access.

The one thing to read first

The model publishes a fair side, a fair total and a moneyline probability for all 13 Week 11 games, and no reward is payable and no strategy is promoted. The sample is 116 graded games against a pre-registered floor of 300, and there is no permitted price source to score realised return or closing-line value at all. The leaderboard is published anyway, because a leaderboard whose only honest state is "empty" is information.

Removal of luck

Luck is removed by refusing to rank on anything a coin can produce. Three controls are published beside the model: always-home (Brier 0.5431), the running league-average total (MAE 23.66) and a fair coin (Brier 0.2500). The model's totals beat the naive total by 12.08 points of MAE with a game-cluster 95% interval of [9.5037, 14.6838]; its moneyline skill against always-home is 0.5796 with an interval of [0.2264, 0.4059]. Both intervals are published even where they flatter nothing.
