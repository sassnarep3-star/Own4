The two boards

The forecast board compares the own model with a naive pick-em baseline. It needs no price, so it can be scored and paid. The betting board needs a price, and no strategy in the registered family clears the gates.

Forecast board

A variant earns reward points only if gates G1, G2, G6 and G9 pass. Points are round(50 times z), where z is the paired z statistic of the holdout improvement over the naive baseline, capped at 500. Variants V1 to V6 are shown for transparency but are not eligible: they use the model as it was originally registered, before the rating decay defect was corrected.

Luck band

The luck band is the half-width of the interval that pure chance around a fifty percent base rate would produce at each strategy's sample size. An observed edge smaller than its own luck band has not separated itself from luck.

The ten gates

Gate Name Threshold Why Source
G1Minimum graded sampleat least 300 graded bets in the holdout for the strategy under evaluationBelow 300 graded bets no NFL strategy can be separated from luck at conventional confidence; this floor is a project-defined design choice.github.com
G2Positive point estimateholdout ROI > 0% and holdout ROI > 0% for the price-independent forecast-skill metricA strategy that does not make money on the holdout cannot be rewarded, regardless of significance.github.com
G3Permutation significanceone-sided permutation p-value < 0.05, labels shuffled within the strategy 10,000 timesRemoves any assumption that bets are independent.github.com
G4Interval excludes break-evenlower bound of the 95% bootstrap confidence interval for ROI > 0%A point estimate above zero whose interval includes zero is not evidence of skill.github.com
G5Multiple-comparison controlBenjamini-Hochberg FDR q < 0.05 across the whole strategy family testedTwelve strategies are tested; without family-wise control the chance of a false positive is far above 5%.github.com
G6Look-ahead integrityperturbing the outcome of any holdout game must not change any forecast made before that game (exact equality of the earlier forecast array)A forecast contaminated by a later result cannot be validated on the same data.github.com
G7Price provenanceevery price used must come from a recorded source with a retrievable URL, commit SHA and checksum, and every row must be labelled with that source's official/third-party statusAn unpriced result cannot be audited. The NFL publishes no betting lines, so every price in this project is third-party and must be labelled as such.github.com
G8Luck / placebo calibrationthe strategy's one-sided p-value must beat the 95th percentile of a 1,000-strategy placebo null on the same holdout, and the placebo false-positive rate must not exceed 10%If random strategies routinely beat the null at the stated threshold, the threshold itself is not evidence of skill.github.com
G9Skill stabilitythe strategy's price-independent forecast skill must beat the naive baseline on the holdout, and the strategy must be non-negative in at least 2 of the 3 seasons in the validation+holdout windowSkill that appears in one season and disappears in the next is luck with a time stamp.github.com
G10Reward eligibilitya strategy earns reward points only if G1-G9 all pass; points are non-monetary until the owner funds a poolSeparates the forecast board (which can be paid because it needs no prices) from the betting board (which needs prices that the NFL does not publish).github.com

Source policy. Official values link to nfl.com. Third-party values link to the named archive and say so in the column header. A value that no official source confirms is left blank and marked unverified.
