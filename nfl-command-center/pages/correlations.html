What is deliberately missing

These are covariates of published season aggregates. They are NOT bet correlations: a bet correlation needs joint outcomes across markets, and that requires a graded sample this repository does not have.

Why one row is a caution rather than a finding

The third row correlates points scored with games played. It is a schedule artefact and it is published on purpose: a correlation panel that only shows the flattering rows is how artefacts become features.
