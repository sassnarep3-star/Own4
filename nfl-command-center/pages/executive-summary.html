Overview · executive summary

Executive summary

Five paragraphs, then the numbers. Written after the captures, not before them.

1. What was done

Official NFL pages were read on 2026-11-15 and 2026-11-16 and transcribed into dated files: the league standings (all 32 clubs), the Week 10 and Week 11 schedule pages, the full Week 10 injury report, three complete Game Books and three club pages for Detroit. A standard-library build turns those files into 17 datasets, this site, and a verification pass that fails on an unsourced row or a broken internal link. No number on this site is typed by hand outside a transcription file, and no value was filled in when the official page left it blank.

2. What is verified

148 settled games reconcile exactly to the standings I read: all 32 clubs agree on wins, losses, ties, points for and points against. The league identity holds (points for sum to points against, 6824 = 6824; wins equal losses, 147 = 147). The Week 10 injury report transcribes to 347 rows across 28 club sections, and that count independently matches the 347/28/171 figure another workspace in this repository measured the day before. Three Game Books give starters, inactives, venue, roof, surface, weather and officials for three games, and one of them proves a replacement chain end to end: Sam Darnold published Out, on the Not Active list, and Drew Lock listed at quarterback with 17 passes in the same document.

3. What the model says, and what it is worth

The pre-registered singles baseline publishes a fair side, a fair total and a moneyline probability for all 13 Week 11 games. Walk-forward over 116 graded games, the total beats the naive league-average total by 12.08 points of mean absolute error, game-cluster 95% interval [9.5037, 14.6838]. Its moneyline beats "always home" on the Brier score by 0.3148, interval [0.2264, 0.4059], a ratio skill of 0.5796 with ratio interval [0.4917, 0.6465]. Those intervals are the honest headline: every published interval here excludes zero at 116 games, which is not the same thing as a promotion, because the pre-registered sample floor is 300.

4. What is blocked, and by what

Of 74 brief lines, 31 are met, 30 are partial and 13 are blocked. The recurring blockers are not coding problems: there is no permitted price source, so every ROI, closing-line value and residual cell is blank; one club of 32 publishes a depth chart this build can parse, and that club labels its own chart unofficial; and the availability history is a single dated week, which cannot support the with-and-without ablation the brief demands. Each blocker names the number that clears it on the readiness table.

5. What to do first

Decide the price policy. It is the only blocker that unblocks an entire class of results at once, and every other open question can be answered in parallel while it is being decided. The suggestions page lists five proposals with their cost, the gate each would clear and the risk; the verdict column is deliberately left for you.
