What "own line" means here, and what it does not

An own line is a model estimate expressed as a market number. It is not a claim that the number is better than a market number — that claim needs a price, and there is none. The residual column exists so that the moment a permitted price source exists, the comparison is already in place and cannot be back-fitted to make the model look good.

Totals, twice

The brief asks for every model total to be published twice until one wins. The second total is the naive running league average from the backtest, published beside the model's number on the backtest page with the gap between them, and neither is retired until the walk-forward comparison clears the sample floor.
