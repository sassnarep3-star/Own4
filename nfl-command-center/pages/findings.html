Findings

Each finding below is backed by a check that can be re-run. Nothing here is asserted without a source.

F-01 - The official data reconciles exactly

Recomputing every team's 2026 record and points from the game log reproduces the official NFL.com league standings for all 32 teams, with no mismatch. League points for and points against are both 6,291, and the single tied game is treated as a push for spread, total and moneyline bets.

F-02 - The archive's spread line is the away team's line

A regression of the actual margin on the archive's spread line has a slope of 1.04 and an intercept of -0.04, so a positive spread line means the home team is the favourite. The moneyline cross check agrees in 99.41% of 5,432 games. The rejected reading agrees in 0.57%.

F-03 - The archive's spread and total prices fail a calibration test

Games the archive prices at 0.45 for the home side to cover actually cover 69% of the time, and games it prices at 0.55 cover 45% of the time. The over price sign does not predict the over rate: 49.0% when negative and 49.5% when positive, against a 49.1% base rate. The moneyline columns, by contrast, are well calibrated and were retained.

F-04 - The forecast engine has no look-ahead

Shifting the outcome of all 422 holdout games by seven points and re-running the engine changes none of the 6,991 forecasts made before the holdout. The test is recorded in every run.

F-05 - The pre-registered model had a rating decay defect

A three percent per-game mean reversion applied to a mean-zero rating removes essentially the whole rating within a season, leaving a rating standard deviation of 1.3 points. The defect was found and fixed using the 1999 to 2022 development window only, and the fix was registered in a dated amendment before the corrected model was scored on the holdout.

F-06 - No betting strategy separates itself from chance

The best evaluated strategy returns 2.27% on 335 holdout bets with a permutation p-value of 0.672, which does not clear the 0.05 floor. The placebo null places the 95th percentile of a thousand skill-free peers above it. Nothing is activated.

F-07 - The own model beats the naive baseline but not the market

On the holdout the own model's spread error is 10.31 points, the naive baseline is 10.92 and the market line is 9.60. The forecast board rewards the first comparison, which needs no price. The betting board would need the second, and no strategy achieves it.

F-08 - The official sources rarely state a replacement starter's depth

Seven depth deficiency rows are supported by an official article or an official game book. Only the two Baltimore quarterback rows carry an official depth-chart code. The other five are blank and marked unverified rather than guessed.

F-09 - The prior workspaces carry 1,275 table rows with no source link

Across 1,127 audited files there are 12,978 table rows, of which 11,531 carry a link and 1,447 do not. 53 files are flagged and 34 still contain placeholder text. The prior namespaces are outside this session's claim, so they were read and audited but not rewritten.

F-10 - Player prop strategies cannot be priced from any official source

The NFL publishes no player prop prices and no official player game log was found for the current week. The two prop strategies are Blocked rather than evaluated: no return is reported for them and no reward is offered.
