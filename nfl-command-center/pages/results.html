What this does and does not prove

It proves the game-level sample is internally consistent with the league's own table, club by club, so a walk-forward backtest cannot be scored against a corrupted base. It does not prove the sample is complete: Week 10's Monday game had not kicked off at capture, so 148 games are settled here, not the 150 a full ten weeks would contain.
