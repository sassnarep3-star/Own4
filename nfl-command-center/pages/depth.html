Depth, roster and replacements

The brief's hardest requirement is not tracking that a starter is out — it is tracking how deep the replacement is. This page shows exactly how far that can be verified today: one club's published depth chart, one club's roster status codes, and a join to the Game Book that names replacements only where the document itself names them.

The blocker, restated precisely

The repository has been carrying the line "no official depth-order source exists". That is too strong. Detroit publishes a structured, machine-readable depth chart and links it from its official nfl.com roster page. But the club labels it Unofficial Depth Chart Compiled by Lions Football Communications, and one club is not 32. The honest blocker is coverage: 31 clubs are uncaptured, and the one captured source carries its publisher's own disclaimer.
