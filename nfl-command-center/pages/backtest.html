How to read these intervals

The intervals are game-cluster bootstraps over 116 forecasts with a fixed seed, so they reproduce exactly. The total's improvement interval excludes zero, which is a real result at this sample. The moneyline's skill interval does not, which means the moneyline is not yet distinguishable from always picking the home team. Both statements are published because both are true.
