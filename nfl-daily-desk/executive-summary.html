3 · The decision that gates the rest

Seven requirements (price store, residual columns, published residuals, price policy, favourites control, two-stage leaderboard, vig-free decomposition) are one decision: do we licence market prices, accept a named third-party archive for research only, or never use them? The repository already contains the consequence of leaving it open — a 12,573-row price store that is 21% filled on the opening side and 97% on the closing side, all of it third-party and labelled as such, next to two stores of zero rows created by sessions that reached the opposite reading of the same rules. Both behaviours are defensible; the open question is what is being paid for. The cheapest fix is not a model, it is a written decision plus a capture job started on the same day.
