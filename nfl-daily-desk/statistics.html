Rules on this page

Correlations are computed only from numbers that come from an official source or a repository row that itself carries an official link, and every value prints its n. The recipe is fixed before it runs: Pearson r, a Fisher-z 95% interval (published as a floor on width, because games in a week are not fully independent), and a two-sided permutation test with 20,000 shuffles at seed 20261119. A relationship that cannot be measured is listed with n=0 and the reason, so the reader sees the hole rather than a smoothing of it.
