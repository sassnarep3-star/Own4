Luck controls applied to every family

ControlWhat it doesWhy it matters
Exact binomial testThe hit rate is compared with the break-even rate using the exact binomial distribution, not a normal approximation.At the sample sizes in this book a normal approximation would overstate the evidence.
False-discovery controlBenjamini-Hochberg q-values are computed across every strategy tried, not one at a time.Trying eight strategies and reporting the best one is how a weak signal gets published as a strong one.
Bootstrap intervalA seeded bootstrap gives a 95% interval on the hit rate.It shows how much of the result is the sample rather than the strategy.
Relabelling placeboThe same bets are shuffled at random and the hit rate is recomputed.A placebo that reaches the observed hit rate means the sample, not the rule, produced the result.
Holdout splitThe first and second half of the out-of-sample sample are tested separately.A strategy that only works in one stretch of the season is not a strategy.
Signal and staking kept apartSkill is scored on one board and money on another, and the masking check compares the two.A lucky staking run must never be able to promote a weak signal.
