Validation gates and the demotion gate

A strategy that is live is sent back to paper when any demotion gate triggers. A strategy may only be promoted to live when every validation gate passes and the sample floor is met.

Validation gates

IDGateThresholdFieldWhat it says
G1Minimum sample100nAt least 100 out-of-sample decisions.
G2Beats break-evenp_valuep < 0.05 against the break-even rate.
G3Survives false-discovery controlq_valueq <= 0.05 across every strategy tried.
