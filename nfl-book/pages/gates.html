Validation gates & demotion

Committed BEFORE engine/backtest.py is first run (see git history). Gate thresholds are project-defined policy choices, not official NFL rules; this is flagged.

IDGateRuleStatusOutcomeSource
G1Sample sizeStaking claims require n >= 100 settled bets; signal claims require n >= 80 settled forecasts.testable—nfl.com
G2Signal skillThe strategy's pre-declared signal metric must beat its pre-declared baseline with a 95% interval excluding the baseline (accuracy vs 0.500 via Wilson interval; MAE via paired comparison).testable—nfl.com
G3No leakageLook-ahead perturbation: mutating results of games after cutoff T must change 0 predictions made before T.testablepassnfl.com
G4Luck removal (placebo)Run >= 500 placebo strategies (random side picks under the same price and sample). The strategy's staking ROI must beat the 95th percentile of the placebo ROI distribution (one-sided placebo p < 0.05).testableS1 p<0.002 pass · S2 0.79 fail · S3 1.0 failnfl.com
G5Staking confidenceThe 95% CI for staking ROI must exclude 0 on the negative side (lower bound > 0) on the settled sample.testable—nfl.com
G6Parlay dependenceParlay legs must come from different games. Same-game legs require a dependence-adjusted price, which cannot be verified without official prices.testable—nfl.com
G7Market verification (fair vs market)Requires an official source of market open/close lines to compare against the model's fair line. No official NFL odds source exists.permanently blockedblockednfl.com
G8Prospective confirmationAt least one full week of forward (post-registration) evaluation with no changes to the strategy rule.pending — Week 11 not yet played at publicationpending (Wk11)nfl.com

Demotion gate — live strategies return to paper

IDGateRuleStatusCurrent evaluationSource
D1Live-to-paper demotion gateAny strategy holding LIVE status is demoted back to PAPER status immediately when ANY of the following is true: (a) over any rolling window of 100 settled bets the upper bound of the 95% CI of ROI is below 0; (b) its G2 signal metric underperforms its declared baseline in 2 consecutive weekly evaluations; (c) a high-severity data-integrity flag is opened against an input it depends on; or (d) its G7 market-verification requirement becomes satisfiable and the strategy then fails fair-vs-market comparison. Demotion requires no human approval; re-promotion to LIVE requires passing G1-G6 and G8 afresh on data not used to design the strategy.active policyno strategy holds LIVE status; D1 is armed and would fire on any of its four conditionsnfl.com
Promotion rule: PAPER -> LIVE only after passing G1, G2, G3, G4, G5, G6 (as applicable) and G8. G7 remains blocked for all strategies until an official price source exists.
