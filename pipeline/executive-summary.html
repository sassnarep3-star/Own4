Executive Summary

Goal. Originate our own NBA lines and compete strategies on an internal leaderboard with pre-registered validation gates, removing luck via walk-forward testing, confidence intervals, and leakage controls. Singles, parlays, and player-prop combos are all in scope.

What is verified (this session)

  • 92 completed games parsed from official NBA daily pages; every final score agrees with the ESPN partner source (92/92).
  • Full standings reconciliation: 300/300 cells (W-L, win%, GB, conf, home, road, OT, last10, streak × 30 teams) computed from the games match nba.com/standings exactly.
  • Player layer: 184/184 official leader stat-lines verified; full-box sample 3/3 vs the official box-score page; 2,509 player-game rows are partner-sourced (flagged as such).
  • Prior season for the model prior transcribed from official 2025-26 standings.

Model & own lines

  • Elo walk-forward (K=20, H=25, margin multiplier, official prior): Brier 0.2309 vs 0.2366 static prior and 0.25 constant — better than both at n=92, but not validated: gate G1 needs n≥300.
  • 15 pre-tip fair moneylines/spreads originated for the Nov 2 slate (first tip 23:00 UTC) — market comparison columns are blank by policy (Q1).

Key empirical findings

  • Home win rate 0.5217 (n=92, Wilson CI [0.4209, 0.6209]) — includes 0.5; no bankable home edge detected.
  • Rest effects are null at this sample (SIG-03/SIG-10, all CIs overlap 0.5).
  • Same-game pace correlation r=0.2004 (p≈0.052) — mild dependence relevant to same-game parlay pricing (SP-3).
  • Same-day cross-game outcome φ=0.026 — independence assumption not rejected (SP-4).
  • Player trailing-mean benchmark is essentially unbiased for PTS (50.2%, n=1,002) — naive prop lines based only on trailing averages have no built-in edge (SP-5).
