What was built

A complete, reproducible NFL line-origination and strategy-validation pipeline: 7,548 archived games (1999–2026), a walk-forward ridge ratings engine, 5 registered betting strategies + 3 research-only constructions, 8 pre-registered validation gates, a 16-estimate correlation study, and a 15-game Week 9 own-line slate. Everything runs from cited, checksummed archives; 15/15 integrity checks and 13/13 official NFL.com score checks pass.

How luck is removed

  • Chronological dev (1999–2012) / validation (2013–2016) / holdout (2017–2025) split; hyperparameters and thresholds frozen before the holdout is evaluated once.
  • Look-ahead perturbation test passes: permuting future results leaves same-date predictions identical.
  • Week-clustered bootstrap 95% CIs; matched-random permutation p-values; Holm correction across the 5-strategy primary family.
  • 500 random-placebo strategies: mean ROI -2.96% (the vig), 6.2% still show positive ROI by chance — exactly why the gates exist.
  • Season-stability and single-season-profit-concentration checks; forward replication (G8) still pending.

What this does NOT show

  • No strategy beats a sportsbook at closing lines. Edges were tested only against the transparent archived market.
  • Player-prop results use a rolling-mean proxy market (2021–22), not real prop prices — Gate G7 fails by design.
  • 2026 player props are blocked (no authorized 2026 player feed). All market lines are third-party archive values.
  • Correlations and backtest metrics are computed, not official; data licensing needs review before production.
