Data & Sources

All inputs are third-party archives of official NFL results/stats plus sportsbook closing lines. Each carries a repo URL, commit SHA and sha256. No official league source publishes betting lines — those values are flagged. Official NFL.com scores were spot-checked game-by-game.

Verified data conventions (critical)

result = home_score − away_score. spread_line = points the home team is favored by (positive = home favorite) — the opposite of standard sportsbook home-spread notation. Verified vs DATASETS.md, corr(result, spread) = +0.43, and 99%+ moneyline agreement. Historical codes OAK/SD/STL → LV/LAC/LA.
