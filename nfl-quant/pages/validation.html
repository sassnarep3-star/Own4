Validation Gates & Luck Removal

Eight gates were pre-registered (committed) before any holdout backtest ran. A strategy earns credit only if it survives every gate. Luck is attacked from five angles: chronological splits, look-ahead perturbation, week-clustered bootstrap, matched-random permutation + Holm, and placebo calibration.
