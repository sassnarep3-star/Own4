Player Props

Player-prop research uses 2021–2022 weekly stats (the only authorized player-level data in scope). 2026 props are blocked. Because no archived prop prices exist, a transparent proxy line is used (rolling 4-game mean). Gate G7 fails by construction — these are signal studies, not betting edges.

Research-only: results measure signal vs a naive proxy market, not vs a sportsbook. The RB1 +3.6% proxy ROI is not a real edge. QF-04
