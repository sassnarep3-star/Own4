Parlay math (official rules)

At −110 per leg, decimal odds multiply: 1.909 × 1.909 = 3.645 → break-even hit rate 27.44%. A push/void leg is removed and the parlay recalculated (FanDuel House Rules Part B §19). Correlated/duplicate outcomes generally cannot be parlayed; Same Game Parlay is a separate repriced product. OFFICIAL ↗ OFFICIAL ↗
