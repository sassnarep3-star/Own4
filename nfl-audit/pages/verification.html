What was compared, and how

  • Reconciliation, not copying. The repository's stored Week 8 data was recomputed forward with the official Week 9 results and compared field by field with today's official standings — 160 comparisons (32 teams × W, L, T, PF, PA).
  • Sign conventions are proved, never assumed. The spread encoding in nfl-quant was derived from its own divergence column (15/15 rows) before any error was computed.
  • Rounding is stated. The league rounds winning percentage half-up: 5-2-1 is 0.313, not the 0.312 that banker's rounding gives.
  • Single-slate numbers are labelled descriptive. Fourteen games cannot establish skill, and no page says they do.
  • Blanks are data. Where the official page is blank, the audit records a blank and counts it on the flag page rather than imputing a value.
