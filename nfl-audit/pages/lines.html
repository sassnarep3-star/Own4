Own lines

What the repository originates, what can be re-graded against official finals, and the blank where a price belongs.

Originating your own lines means producing a number the market does not give you. This page is deliberately short on numbers, because no official source publishes a price. It shows what the repository claims, what can be re-graded against official finals, and where the price columns must stay blank.

No price, no edge. Every price, edge and ROI cell below is blank or explicitly labelled third-party. The repository's own provenance file records that its odds are third-party and that no official league source exists for them.
