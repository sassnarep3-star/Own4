S06Luck removalScore every strategy with a walk-forward holdout, a permutation test on the realised metric, Benjamini-Hochberg control across all strategies tested, and a bootstrap interval on the edge.A single p-value over many tested strategies is a luck detector, not a skill detector.Wider intervals and more 'fails' — which is the point.MediumLow.Q06RecommendedS2 Official NFL league standi ↗

S08InjuriesAdd transactions to the injury join: a player traded on 11/10 must not be counted against his old team's depth.November trades moved a dozen players, including starters, between teams that play this week.More moving parts in the join.MediumLow.Q03RecommendedS11 nfl.com transactions 2026 ↗
S09Line originationPublish a transparent baseline fair line (points-based rating + measured home-field advantage) with its walk-forward error, before any market price is licensed.It gives an honest, reproducible starting point and a floor that any fancy model must beat.A crude baseline will look weak next to a market line; that is the correct comparison.MediumLow.Q01RecommendedS2 Official NFL league standi ↗

S12LeaderboardAward nothing until a strategy clears every gate; show the failure reason per gate instead of a rank.A leaderboard that pays the top of a small sample pays for luck and teaches the wrong lesson.The board looks empty — which is the truthful state.LowLow.Q06, Q08Recommendedrepository · leaderboard ↗
