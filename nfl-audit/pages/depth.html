Defensive & offensive depth deficiency

Who is unavailable, where, and what is honestly unknown. String depth is blank because no official source publishes it.

You asked for tracking of defensive and offensive deficiencies when backups replace starters — 2nd, 3rd and 4th string. Here is the honest position: the official depth chart is not retrievable (nfl.com returns 404 on 2026-11-12), so string position is blank rather than guessed. What can be verified is published instead: who is not practising, at which position group, on which team, and who they play this week.

Flagged: no official depth-chart source. Rosters publish jersey number, position, experience and active status — never a depth order. Inferring "3rd string" from experience would be invention, so it is not done. See F01 and Q03.

What would close the gap

  1. Licence a depth-chart feed and store it with the same capture-time and hash discipline as the official pages.
  2. Join transactions. Twelve trades happened between 11/06 and 11/10; a player who moved must not be counted against his old team's depth (flag F10).
  3. Then estimate the marginal effect of a backup replacing a starter — as a measured coefficient with an interval, not an assumption. Until then, exposure counts are the deliverable.
