Leaderboard & rewards

Zero qualifiers, zero rewards — and the seven rules a board needs before it can pay anyone.

A leaderboard is only honest if it can show an empty board. The repository's own competition design awards zero rewards because nothing clears the gates. This page shows that state, the flaw in the current tiering, and the rules a leaderboard needs before it can pay anything.

Irregularity (F18). S1 and S4 are given a Bronze tier while their own bootstrap 5th percentile is negative (−3.63% and −7.79%). A tier assigned to a point estimate whose interval includes zero is a reward for luck, even at zero points. Fix: no tier until the lower bound is positive.
