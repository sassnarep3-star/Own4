Why "no strategy qualifies" is the right answer

A season is 272 games. Even a genuinely profitable strategy needs hundreds of graded bets before its edge is distinguishable from noise. The repository's own numbers show this clearly: its best totals result had a permutation p-value of 0.0261 on a small sample and still failed the multiple-testing gate. Publishing that as skill would be the error; publishing it as a failure is the feature.

Correlation values for parlays and prop combos are not published anywhere on this site: measuring them needs a player-game panel that the repository does not have (Q09). Any coefficient you see elsewhere in the repository without an official source is flagged.

Every number on this site was read from an nfl.com or operations.nfl.com page on 2026-11-12 and links to it. Where an official page publishes nothing, the cell is left blank and the item is listed on the Flagged-for-Review page. Nothing here is wagering advice. Prices, edges and returns are blank because no official price source exists in the pages read.
